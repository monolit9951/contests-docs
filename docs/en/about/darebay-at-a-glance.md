## What DareBay is

DareBay is a pay-per-view clipping platform. Brands, streamers and creators publish tasks with their own footage; clippers cut it into short videos, post them on their own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or other accounts named in the task, and are paid for the views the platform counts. The same product is available on the web at darebay.com and inside Telegram, with one account for both.

Two kinds of contest exist. In a **wallet-backed contest** the organizer's budget is locked on the platform before the contest opens, and the platform credits each clipper's balance after the count. In a **manual-payout contest** the organizer pays clippers directly, while the platform counts the views and records every result; the task card says which kind it is before you take it.

## What DareBay adds on top of pay per view

**Better offers.** Besides the rate per 1,000 views, an offer task can pay a fixed fee for every accepted clip and a share of the orders your promo code brings in, all on the same task card.

**Ready-made videos with built-in uniqueization.** On offer tasks the platform hands you finished videos and makes every copy unique for you, so you can post without editing software and without repeating another clipper's clip. How ready clips and a few live accounts add up to a [content farm paid per view](/en/content-farm/) has its own section, and editors, AI clippers and caption apps for cutting your own clips are compared in the [tools catalog](/en/tools/).

## How the money moves

Every task card states its terms before you start: the rate per 1,000 counted views, the view threshold a clip has to clear, and the cap on what one clip can earn, if the task sets one. The rate and threshold are task-specific (system default 1,000 views) - check the card in the catalog - and the cap is set per task. The rate and the threshold do not change after launch; the cap per clip can only be raised.

The calculation is automatic: the platform's own counter reads the public view counter at your link and works out the sum; nobody types figures in by hand. A clip with suspicious dynamics goes on hold for review. Your earnings reach your balance automatically and in full when a wallet-backed task ends, or earlier with Claim now where the task shows it: the platform takes nothing from the task budget or from your earnings. The only fee a clipper pays comes at withdrawal: request from 10 USDT and the platform does the rest, with 10% (default) coming off inside the request and the amount to receive shown before you confirm. Payout rails are USDT on the TON network and Telegram Stars; a contest can additionally name a card, a bank transfer or a gift as its reward method.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does DareBay pay per 1,000 views?

Each task states its own rate per 1,000 views. The organizer prints it on the task card, and it is not changed after the contest opens.

### Do I need followers to earn on DareBay?

No. Payment tracks the views of one specific clip, so a new account with no followers can take a brief. If a task cares about the account's topic, it says so in its terms.

### Is the budget guaranteed?

In a wallet-backed contest the organizer's budget is locked on the platform before the start and the platform pays after the count. In a manual-payout contest the organizer pays directly; the card says which kind the contest is.

### What are the fees?

Taking part is free and the contest fee is 0%: the platform takes nothing from the task budget or from what you earn. The only fee a clipper pays is 10% (default) when withdrawing your available balance, deducted inside the request: a 100 USDT request pays out 90 USDT, the minimum request is 10 USDT, and the form shows the amount to receive before you confirm.
