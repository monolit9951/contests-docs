# Does DareBay really pay?

Yes, and your figure does not hang on anyone's mood. More useful than the word is the chain behind it, because it shows where your money is at every step and why nobody can talk it down along the way.

Short version: the calculation is automatic and cannot be re-run in anyone's favour, your earnings reach your balance automatically when a wallet-backed contest ends, and a withdrawal is one request from 10 USDT: the platform does the rest.

## The chain from a published clip to money

1. **The contest is created with its terms.** The buyer sets the rate per 1000 views, the view threshold and the cap per submission. In a wallet-backed contest the budget is charged from their balance and locked against that contest at this point, before anybody sees the task.
2. **A clipper takes the task and publishes.** The clip goes out on the site the contest names. Statistics are collected today for TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and the other sites named in the task.
3. **The clipper sends the link.** The submitted URL is what attaches a clip to a contest, so the calculation follows exactly the clips you sent in: send the link for every clip you want counted.
4. **The counter is read.** The platform's independent counter reads the published view counter on the site the clip was posted on. Neither the buyer nor the clipper types that figure in, and neither can adjust it.
5. **The formula runs.** `views / 1000 * rate`, capped at the contest's cap per submission, for every clip that cleared the threshold. Clearing the threshold puts the whole counter into the calculation, not just the part above it.
6. **The result becomes an obligation.** The platform records a credit or a payout obligation with a status attached to it. That record is what you follow from here.
7. **The money reaches your balance.** In a wallet-backed contest your earnings are credited to your balance automatically when the contest ends, or earlier with Claim now where the task shows it. From there you request a withdrawal from 10 USDT and the platform does the rest: 10% comes off inside the request, and you see the amount to receive before you confirm.

None of these steps leaves the organizer room for a change of mind: the terms were fixed at creation, the figure came from outside both parties, the arithmetic is fixed, and in a wallet-backed contest the crediting runs on its own once the contest ends.

## Three cases worth knowing

**A submission gets flagged.** Suspicious view dynamics put a clip on hold and pause the accrual on it until it has been looked at. That is a pause and not a verdict, and there are three ways out of it: the clip passes and is counted normally, it stays on hold until the review window runs out and a payout on it is still possible afterwards, or moderation rejects it and it takes no part in the budget split. The hold is what keeps suspicious views from eating into the budget that honest clippers share.

**The clip disappears.** A video deleted, hidden or blocked by the site before the contest is finalized has no counter left to read, so nothing can be confirmed for it. Keep it published until the contest closes.

**The organizer settles directly.** In a contest without a locked pool the organizer is the one who pays, and DareBay keeps the obligation and its status on record, so what you are owed is written down from the moment the result is in.

## How to follow your payout

Everything is visible in the product: the contest card, the request status and the payout details you entered. Every request is checked before it goes out, so the money reaches the right wallet; the one thing worth double-checking on your side is the wallet address you type. For a question about a specific request, write to support@darebay.com and quote the request identifier.

Two things not to do: do not create a second request for the same money, and do not send anyone a "fee" to speed it up. The fee for a withdrawal is taken out of the amount you requested, inside the form, and never in advance.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a payout take?

Your earnings reach your balance automatically when a wallet-backed contest ends, or earlier with Claim now where the task shows it. From there you request a withdrawal from 10 USDT and the platform does the rest. And the money itself was never in doubt: the wallet-backed budget was locked before you started, and the recorded calculation cannot be revised in anyone's favour after the fact.

### What happens to my clip if it is flagged?

Accrual on it is paused while it is reviewed. It can be released and counted in full, remain on hold with a payout still possible once the review window closes, or be rejected by moderation and left out of the split. There is no automatic verdict in either direction.

### The contest has closed. Where is my money?

In a wallet-backed contest it heads to your balance: your earnings are credited there when the contest ends, with nobody having to send them, and you follow the status in the product. In a contest without a locked pool, the close records the organizer's obligation to you, with a status of its own. Withdrawing your balance is one request from 10 USDT under the [withdrawal terms](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals).

### Can the organizer refuse to pay after my clip has done well?

In a wallet-backed contest they have nothing left to refuse with: the budget left their balance when the contest was created. In a contest without a locked pool they remain the direct payer, which is exactly why the funding mode is on the card before you take the task.
