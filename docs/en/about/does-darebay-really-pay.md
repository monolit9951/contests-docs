# Does DareBay really pay? How the payout is secured

Yes, DareBay really pays. The platform works as an intermediary and guarantor: the contest budget is locked at creation, the results are counted by the system through platform APIs, and the payout goes out automatically by validated views or other results. The organizer cannot take the money back after publishing the contest. The participant pays nothing to enter and nothing to withdraw. Below we go through exactly how that is built and how the reward reaches you.

## How the payout is secured

The intermediary and guarantor mechanic works in three steps:

1. **The organizer locks the budget when creating the contest.** The money is charged to the organizer balance and held on the platform. After publishing, the organizer cannot get it back or redirect it.
2. **The system counts the results.** Views are pulled automatically through platform APIs (TikTok today). Votes and the winner selection also run through the platform. Neither the organizer nor the participant can adjust the numbers.
3. **The payout goes out automatically.** When a contest ends, the platform distributes the prize by validated results on its own. The organizer does not press a "pay" button, the process runs without them.

The participant risks no money: no deposit, no entry fee, no withdrawal fee.

## Why the money cannot disappear

The prize is not held in the organizer's account. Once the contest is created, the money sits on the platform and is tied to that specific contest. Neither the organizer nor the platform spends those funds on anything else: the budget stays attached to the contest until it ends and goes only to payouts for it.

The payout follows one of these paths:

- **Pay-per-view contests.** The system counts the views of each submission through platform APIs and splits the budget in proportion to validated views.
- **Contests with a vote or a jury pick.** The prize goes to the winner once the results are finalized.
- **Random selection.** The system makes a fair random pick among the participants.

If a contest ends with no participants or with an unspent remainder, the funds go back to the organizer.

## Ways to receive the reward

On DareBay the wallet is one method among several. Here is what you can receive the reward through:

- **Wallet** (USDT on TON)
- **Card** (bank card)
- **Bank transfer**
- **An item** (a physical prize from the organizer)
- **Telegram Stars**
- **Telegram gifts**

Fiat (card, bank transfer) works just as well as the wallet. Which one you use depends on the contest and on your own preference.
