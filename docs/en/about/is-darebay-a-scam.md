# Is DareBay a scam?

DareBay is not a scam and not a con. It is a platform where brands and individuals launch activities (tasks, battles, challenges, contests), and participants make content and get paid for it. The platform works as an intermediary and guarantor: the prize is locked before the start, and the payout goes out automatically by independently counted results. The organizer cannot take the locked money back, and the platform holds it until payout and hands it to the winners automatically. Below we go through why this mechanic rules out the usual patterns of fraud.

## How the money is protected

The intermediary and guarantor mechanic is built like this:

1. **The organizer locks the prize before publishing.** The money is charged when the contest is created and held on the platform. The organizer cannot get it back or spend it after launch.
2. **The system counts the results.** Views are pulled automatically through the API of the platform the video was posted on (today that is TikTok). Votes and the winner selection also run through the platform. Neither side can adjust the numbers.
3. **The payout is automatic.** When a contest closes, the platform distributes the prize by results on its own. The organizer does not press a "pay" button, it happens without them.

The participant risks no money. There is no deposit, no entry fee and no withdrawal fee.

## How DareBay differs from scam platforms

Fraud schemes share a set of tells. Here is how they line up against DareBay:

| Scam tell | How DareBay works |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Money promised with no conditions and no work | Payout only for a real result (views, votes, a jury decision). No work, no prize |
| Money demanded to withdraw or to "activate" | The participant pays nothing. The commission is paid by the organizer when the contest is created |
| Money sits "on the balance" but cannot be withdrawn | Winnings land on the balance and go out to a card, a bank account or a wallet with no extra conditions |
| No transparent mechanic, everything on trust | The prize is locked in advance, the results are counted by the system, the payout is automatic |

DareBay does neither: it does not promise easy money and it does not charge for withdrawals.

## Ways to receive the reward

The reward on DareBay is not one method but a menu:

- **Wallet** (USDT on TON)
- **Card** (bank card)
- **Bank transfer**
- **An item** (a physical prize from the organizer)
- **Telegram Stars**
- **Telegram gifts**

The wallet is not the only option: card and bank transfer work just as well.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is DareBay a scam or a con?

No. The prize is locked on the platform before the contest starts. The organizer cannot take that money back, and the platform holds it until payout. Winners are paid automatically by counted results. DareBay works as an intermediary and guarantor.

### Why can DareBay not steal the money?

Because the money is locked the moment the contest is created. The organizer cannot cancel the payout after publishing. The platform hands the prize to the winner automatically once the results are counted.

### How do I tell DareBay apart from a fraudulent platform?

Scam platforms ask for money to withdraw or promise payouts with no conditions. DareBay takes no money from participants and promises no income without work. The prize is locked in advance and the payout depends on real results. More on withdrawals: [how to withdraw your winnings](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals).

### Do I have to pay to withdraw my winnings?

No. DareBay charges nothing for withdrawals. The commission is paid by the organizer when the contest is created. The winner receives the full prize amount. More on the commission: [the DareBay commission](/en/help/what-commission).

### How can the reward be paid out?

Wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, a physical item, Telegram Stars, Telegram gifts. The wallet is not the only option: card and bank transfer work just as well.

### What if the contest ends and nobody pays me?

The payout happens automatically. The organizer takes no part in it: the money is already on the platform and the system distributes it by results.

### Can a new platform be trusted?

Trust here rests on the mechanic, not on age. The prize is locked before the start, the results are counted by the system through platform APIs, and the payout is automatic. That scheme does not depend on reputation, it works at the level of the process. More on how results are counted: [what stops fake submissions](/en/help/protection-from-view-fraud).

## The bottom line

DareBay is not a lottery and not a pyramid scheme. It is a platform with a mechanic you can follow: the budget is locked, the results are counted automatically, the payout goes out without the organizer touching it.
