To start a clipping agency, sign up where funded clipping campaigns already run, recruit two or three clippers, split the work into roles, and write down who owns each account and how payouts are split before the first clip. A clipping agency is a small team that turns long videos into short clips at volume and posts them across many accounts, paid by campaigns per view or per sale, or by clients on a retainer.

This guide is for the side doing the clipping; streamers looking for clippers should read [how to find clippers for your content](/en/for-brands/how-to-find-clippers). DareBay, which publishes this guide, runs one of the campaign marketplaces below.

## Where the paid campaigns come from

Line up demand before you recruit: paid campaigns come from three places.

| Source | How it pays | Check before you commit |
|---|---|---|
| Direct clients: streamers, podcasters, brands | Flat fee per clip, revenue share or monthly retainer | Account ownership, usage rights, reporting |
| Campaign marketplaces | Per 1,000 counted views, at each campaign's rate | Fee on payouts, payout method, countries paid |
| Per-sale offers | Commission per confirmed sale via your tracked link | Who counts the sales, refund rules |

Some marketplaces offer several of these models: Content Rewards, for example, runs CPM, retainer and per-post campaigns<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/ 2026-09-29 -->. Fees, thresholds and payout countries by platform are compared in [the best clipping platforms](/en/earnings/best-clipping-platforms).

## Roles on a clipping team

Five roles rarely mean five people: early on, the lead does QA and one person sources and edits.

| Role | Does | Hands over |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing | Reads campaign rules, pulls the campaign's footage, marks timestamps | Daily list of moments |
| Editing | Cuts vertical clips with captions, one version per account | Files labeled by account |
| Posting | Runs live accounts, one theme each, and posts on schedule | Live clips |
| QA | Checks clips against the rules, catches repeats, reads statuses | Go or no-go, rejection reasons |
| Lead | Picks campaigns, assigns accounts, keeps the books, pays splits | Weekly sheet and payouts |

Keep QA away from whoever edited the clip. On DareBay offer tasks that hand out finished clips, a unique version per account, editing drops out.

## Who owns the accounts

DareBay matches each clip's author against the connected social account and ties every account to one member, so owners should connect their accounts before the first task clip. Write down each account's owner on day one: payouts follow the owner, and so does the account if someone leaves.

- **No shared passwords.** A YouTube channel owner can give a clipper the Editor role: they upload and publish but cannot delete the channel or published content or manage permissions, and never need the owner's Google password<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328 2026-09-29 -->. Editor (Limited) adds one limit: no revenue data<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328 2026-09-29 -->.
- **No swapping out restricted accounts.** TikTok allows several accounts, but not to deceive others or break the rules: if one is restricted or banned, you may not create or use another to get around it, and buying or selling followers or engagement for financial gain counts as spam<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.
- **One version per account.** On DareBay tasks a version is posted once, on one account: platforms cut the reach of repeats, and a task's moderation can take a copy down. Warm up new accounts first with the [account warm-up guide](/en/earnings/account-warm-up-and-shadowban).
- **Live accounts only.** DareBay pays for real views; empty placeholder accounts and botted views earn nothing.

## The margin formulas

None of this is a forecast.

- **One clip:** `E = min(V ÷ 1,000 × R, C)`, with counted views V, the task's rate R per 1,000 views and its per-clip ceiling C. Below the task's view threshold, E is zero.
- **Agency income:** `I = 0.9 × (E_own + E_ref) + D`, with earnings on the agency's own accounts, referral bonuses and direct client income. The 0.9 is what stays after the 10% fee on withdrawal.
- **Costs:** `K = F × N + S + T`, with a flat fee F for N accepted clips, shares S, and tools and devices T.
- **Margin:** `M = I − K`.
- **Flat-fee break-even:** a clip covers fee F at `F ÷ (0.9 × R) × 1,000` counted views, and never when F is above 0.9 × C.

Check E against realistic views per clip in the [clipping earnings calculator](/en/earnings/clipping-earnings-calculator) before promising a flat fee.

## Paying your clippers: three ways

| Way | Whose accounts | DareBay payout lands with | The others get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own accounts | Each clipper's | Each clipper | The lead earns on own clips and referrals |
| Flat fee | The agency's | The lead | A fixed fee per accepted clip |
| Revenue share | The poster's | The account owner | An agreed share of their clips |

With own accounts nothing is split: the lead earns 10% of each invited clipper's earnings, paid by DareBay, not by the clipper. A flat fee leaves the risk with the agency, which pays even for clips that miss the threshold. A revenue share follows contribution and risk; say whether it is counted before or after the 10% withdrawal fee.

## Tracking who earned what

The platform counts the views: each member's dashboard shows clips, statuses, counted views and earnings per task, and on per-sale tasks each persona's link shows clicks from real people and sales. The lead copies this into a weekly sheet, one row per clip: member, account, campaign, status, views, earnings, share owed, date paid. With one set of numbers, splits rarely turn into arguments.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many clippers do you need to start a clipping agency?

Two or three: the lead plus one or two people on editing and posting. Add people once the team keeps pace and its clips keep clearing campaign thresholds.

### How much does a clipping agency make?

No fair monthly figure exists in advance: it depends on counted views per clip, each campaign's rate and ceiling, and team costs. The margin formulas above turn your own numbers into an estimate.

### Do you need clients before you start?

No. Marketplaces publish funded campaigns anyone can join, so start there and pitch direct clients once you have results to show.

### Can the whole agency work under one DareBay account?

No: one person can have one active DareBay account, so every clipper who posts from their own accounts signs up themselves. Editors who only cut clips for the agency's accounts need none: you pay them yourself.

### What happens to the accounts when a clipper leaves?

Each account stays with the member it is tied to on DareBay, along with the earnings from its clips. On a YouTube channel you own, removing the clipper's role in channel permissions ends their access<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328 2026-09-29 -->.

### What should the agreement with a clipper cover?

Who owns and connects each account, the daily handover, a flat fee or a share, and whether the share is counted before or after the withdrawal fee. Add your payday and what happens to accounts and unpaid earnings if someone leaves.
