A content farm, in short-video terms, is a set of TikTok, Reels and Shorts accounts that one person or a small team posts to every day, with each account getting its own version of every clip. The kind that gets paid in 2026 runs live accounts on one niche each; a content mill does the opposite and pushes the same AI slop through hundreds of empty accounts. Where the term came from and what the platforms penalize is covered in [what is a content farm](/en/content-farm/what-is-a-content-farm).

Posting at volume is the easy part; the hard part is who pays for the views. Agencies sell production to brands, courses sell the method, and creator funds pay only accounts that qualify.

On DareBay the buyer is already in place: tasks pay per counted view on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, $1–10 per 1,000 views at the rate each task sets, up to $500 per clip, with no follower minimum. Other tasks pay for every confirmed sale through your personal link.

Everything else a farm needs is on DareBay too: on offer tasks, source footage, setups and ready clips with a unique version for every account, plus tracking of views, clicks and sales, a balance credited automatically and withdrawals to your wallet, on the web and in Telegram. You post from your own live accounts, and we'll help with the setup.

## Farm models: what you do and what pays

A farm can run on clips from footage, ready clips or offer traffic, and the models mix freely. The rate, the threshold and the other terms of each come from the task. Ready clips suit faceless pages, since nobody has to appear on camera, and AI-made clips fit a task unless it says otherwise, labeled as AI wherever the platform asks.

| Model | What you do | What pays | What DareBay gives you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clips from footage | cut footage into short clips to the task's terms | counted views | the rate, the threshold, and footage when the buyer attaches it |
| Ready clips | post the clips you are given from your own accounts | counted views | ready clips on offer tasks, a unique version for each account |
| Offer traffic | bring buyers within the offer's rules | a share of paid orders | a personal link and promo code |

Terms for offer traffic are on the page for [traffic partners](/en/earn/traffic), and terms for crews on the page for [teams and agencies](/en/earn/teams). ## What gets a content farm cut off

The number of accounts is rarely the problem: TikTok allows multiple accounts, just not to deceive others or break the rules <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. What gets a farm cut off is what it posts and who watches it.

- **Recycled clips.** TikTok treats content copied from others, or largely repurposed from another source without creative edits, as unoriginal, and says it may be removed from the For You feed <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy 2026-09-29 -->. Build each account's version from the task's own material.
- **Templated AI at volume.** Among its examples of content that can't be monetized, YouTube lists AI-generated videos made with generic templates that give the impression of mass production <!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Bought views and blank accounts.** On a task, a sudden spike sends the clip to moderation, a clip with bought views can be removed entirely, and accounts kept empty just to post on don't get their views through the check. The [account warm-up guide](/en/earnings/account-warm-up-and-shadowban) shows how to give each account a niche and a history.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who pays for the views a farm gets?

The buyer behind the task: a brand or advertiser whose offer the task runs. DareBay counts the views or sales, credits your balance and sends the money to your wallet when you request a withdrawal.

### Do my accounts need YPP or Creator Rewards to earn on tasks?

No. Tasks pay per counted view on the clip itself, so they don't ask for the YouTube Partner Program, Creator Rewards or a follower minimum. Creator Rewards, by contrast, is open only in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico and Brazil, to creators with at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the last 30 days <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/eligibility 2026-09-29 -->.

### How many accounts does a content farm need?

One live account is enough to start, and most farms open one account each on TikTok, Reels and Shorts in the same niche. One person can connect up to 20 accounts to a single task across all platforms, and every clip on every account is counted on its own.

### Can one clip go on every account in the farm?

Yes, as versions: every account gets its own version of the clip, and on offer tasks the version builder makes it for you. Don't post the same file to several accounts: TikTok may remove unoriginal content from the For You feed <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy 2026-09-29 -->, and on a task the second copy can be taken down.

### Can AI-made clips go into a task?

Yes, unless the task says otherwise. TikTok, for one, requires a clear label when AI is used to realistically depict people or scenes <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->, and the [terms](/en/legal/terms) rule out work laundered through AI and passed off as original.

### Do I need a course or a YouTube automation program first?

Not to start on tasks: the terms sit on the task card, offer tasks carry a setup and often ready clips, and the basics are free in the [earnings guides](/en/earnings/). A course makes sense if you plan to sell production to clients of your own, with your own sales and editing team.

### Can a clipping team or agency join?

Yes. Every member signs up with their own DareBay account, connects their own accounts and is paid to their own wallet, and terms for your volume are agreed one to one; the details are on [teams and agencies](/en/earn/teams).
