TikTok publishes no maximum number of accounts: its help center says you can add accounts to your device and switch between them in the app <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->. Running multiple TikTok accounts means one person or team operating several profiles, each with its own login, and TikTok's Community Guidelines allow it, naming fan content and creative expression as examples, but not "to deceive others or break the rules" <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.

The real limits are logins, what each account posts and how a ban spreads. Below is what TikTok's own pages said on September 29, 2026 (the Community Guidelines version effective September 24, 2026), and how DareBay, which publishes this guide, pays when one person runs many accounts.

## What TikTok's rules say about several accounts

- **How many accounts per person or device?** No number is published; the app lets you add accounts and switch between them <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Can accounts share an email, phone number or social login?** No: each phone number, email address and social media account links to one TikTok account only <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Why may one person run several?** Fan content and creative expression are the examples given; deceiving others or breaking the rules is not allowed <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Can software run them?** Using automation to run many accounts or send repetitive content is listed as spam <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.
- **What if one account is banned?** You may not create or use another account to get around a restriction or a ban <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Can one violation cost every account?** For a serious violation or an attempt to dodge enforcement, TikTok may ban all of a person's accounts, including associated accounts <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features 2026-09-29 -->.

Nowhere do these pages limit a person to one account. The rules are about what the accounts do.

## Why the same video on two accounts loses reach

TikTok's feed rules make "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" ineligible for the For You feed <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. Its originality policy counts as unoriginal any content copied completely from others, content largely repurposed from another source without creative edits, and clips with someone else's visible watermark; such content may be removed from the For You feed and can't earn from the Creator Rewards Program <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy 2026-09-29 -->.

A second upload of the same file adds nothing new, which is the very test TikTok applies. The risk also grows with volume: when an account posts a lot of content that is ineligible for the For You feed, even without breaking a rule, TikTok may make the whole account and its content ineligible and harder to find <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features 2026-09-29 -->. A farm that pushes one file to ten accounts every day builds exactly that pattern.

A new version is whatever a viewer would notice:

- a different first second or hook;
- a new caption and on-screen text;
- a different layout or framing;
- a new voice-over;
- another moment from the source, or a different length.

Mirroring a clip, adding a color filter or changing its speed, each on its own, leaves it the same video for the person watching.

## How a ban on one account reaches the others

For deceptive account behavior, TikTok may ban the account, ban additional or new accounts you create, or restrict the account's posting, search placement and For You feed reach <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. Its list of circumvention includes spreading violative content across multiple accounts and using another account to avoid restrictions such as comment blocks <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. For a serious violation, the ban can cover every account the person has <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features 2026-09-29 -->.

So one account that breaks the rules puts all of them at risk. Keep each account clean on its own, never move a restricted account's clips to another, and never open a replacement to get around a restriction. Buying or selling followers or engagement counts as spam too, and TikTok strips fake likes and followers <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. Separate browser profiles don't change who is behind the accounts; [what antidetect browsers and cloud phones can't fix](/en/tools/antidetect-browsers-explained) is covered separately.

## The practical limit: what each extra account needs

Since TikTok publishes no number, the ceiling is how many accounts you can keep alive and original. Every extra account needs:

- **Its own email or phone number that you control.** TikTok uses them to log you in and to recover the password <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Its own niche and a warm-up.** The first week of a new account, day by day, is in the [account warm-up guide](/en/earnings/account-warm-up-and-shadowban).
- **Its own version of every clip**, as described above.
- **Your posting time.** TikTok Studio on the web lets you schedule posts up to 30 days ahead <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 -->, while using automation to run many accounts or send repetitive content counts as spam <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. Schedulers that handle several accounts are compared in [multi-account schedulers](/en/tools/multi-account-schedulers).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can two TikTok accounts share an email or phone number?

No. TikTok links each phone number, email address and social media login to a single account, so every extra account needs its own <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->. A deleted account stays deactivated for 30 days, and only then can its email or number go on a new account <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581820709597583928 2026-09-29 -->.

### How do I add a second TikTok account on the same phone?

In the TikTok app, tap Profile, tap your nickname, then tap Add account to create another one, or pick an existing account to switch to; switching also works from settings <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can TikTok ban all of my accounts at once?

Yes. For a serious violation or an attempt to dodge enforcement, TikTok may ban all of a person's accounts, including associated ones <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features 2026-09-29 -->. For deceptive behavior it may also ban the additional or new accounts you create <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can I open a new TikTok account after a ban?

Not as a way around the ban: TikTok's rules say that if your account is restricted or banned, you may not create or use another account to get around it <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. Returning after a permanent ban for severe violations counts as circumvention too <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.
