# What beginners are paid for clips on DareBay

A beginner is paid exactly like everybody else: a rate per 1000 views. The typical rate across live contests is **$0.50 per 1000 views**. There is no follower requirement: you can start from zero followers and from your very first clip. The one condition for a payout is that your submission reaches at least **2000 views** (the counted-view threshold). The contest budget is frozen on the platform in advance and the rate is fixed, so you see the terms before you start work.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## A worked example for a beginner

Say you submit a clip to a contest with a rate of $0.50 per 1000 views and a $50 cap per submission.

The formula: `(views / 1000) * the contest rate`, capped at the limit.

- The clip gets 5,000 views: 5,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$2.50**
- The clip gets 20,000 views: 20,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$10.00**
- The clip gets 100,000 views: 100,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = $50.00, and with a **$50** cap the payout is **$50.00**

The cap per submission in typical contests is **$50**. You can submit to several contests, and each submission is counted on its own. These are offer parameters, not a promise of a particular income: the outcome depends on how many views your clips get.

## What a beginner needs to start

| What | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Followers | Not needed. Zero followers is not a problem |
| View threshold | **2000 views** on a specific submission - the minimum before anything is paid |
| Minimum rate | From **$0.30** per 1000 views (depends on the contest) |
| Typical rate | **$0.50** per 1000 views (the median across live contests) |
| Cap per submission | Up to **$50** per submission (the typical figure) |
| An account on the platform | TikTok, YouTube or wherever the clip is published |
| A DareBay account | Signing up is free |

Note that "no audience requirement" does not mean "no threshold at all". There is a view threshold on each submission (**2000 views**), and it keeps the budget from being spread too thin. But there is no follower threshold: a beginner competes on exactly the same terms as a veteran.

## How to start earning

1. Sign up at [DareBay](https://darebay.com) - it is free
2. Open the contest catalogue and pick one whose subject suits you; the rate, threshold and cap are all on the card
3. Film the clip (a clip, a review, a reaction) to the contest terms and publish it on the platform with the required hashtag
4. Views are pulled automatically through the platform's API - neither you nor the organizer types figures in by hand
5. When the contest ends and your submission has cleared the threshold (**2000 views**), the payout arrives automatically to your wallet, card or another method

The platform acts as intermediary and guarantor: the budget is frozen before the contest starts and the terms are fixed. The organizer cannot change the rate or take the money back after launch.

## Why this suits a beginner

On most platforms (TikTok Creator Fund, YouTube Shorts Fund) payouts are tied to audience size, geography and algorithms - and a new creator has to spend a long time building a follower base before seeing any income at all.

DareBay's model is different: the buyer sets the rate themselves and freezes the budget. There is no follower threshold. If your clip reaches **2000 views**, you get paid by the formula regardless of how many followers you have.

The full mechanics, from locked budget to automatic payout, are in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need followers to start?

No. There is no minimum follower count. You can start from zero. The one condition is reaching **2000 views** on a specific submission.

### What is the view threshold?

**2000 views** per submission. That is the minimum at which a submission joins the budget split. Clips below it are not paid.

### What will a beginner actually make?

It depends on your clips' views and the contest rate. At the typical $0.50 per 1000 views: a clip with 10,000 views brings $5, one with 50,000 brings $25. The cap per submission in typical contests is **$50**. You can enter several contests in parallel.

### How do I start with no experience?

Sign up, open the contest catalogue, pick a subject you are close to. Film the clip to the terms and publish it. Editing experience helps but is not required - plenty of clippers start with simple formats.

### When does the payout arrive?

After the contest ends there is a results-confirmation window. Then the payout runs automatically to your wallet, card or another chosen method. The budget was frozen in advance - the platform as intermediary guarantees the payout.

### Can the organizer refuse to pay?

No. The budget is frozen on the platform when the contest is created. The organizer cannot take it back or change the terms after launch. The platform acts as guarantor and distributes payouts automatically against the results.

### Is the minimum rate too low?

The minimum rate is **$0.30** per 1000 views. The typical (median) one is **$0.50**. The rate is set by the organizer and is visible on the card before you submit. You choose which contest to enter.
