## The best clipping platforms in 2026, in short

There is no single best clipping platform: the one that pays you depends on how you want to be paid, the campaign's requirements and whether your clips pass a view threshold. For bank or PayPal payouts, look at Whop Content Rewards, which supports bank, mobile-wallet and crypto withdrawals<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods 2026-11-14 -->, and Vyro, which has sent cash-outs to a Whop wallet since 2 November 2026<!-- source: https://vyro.com/changelog/more-ways-to-get-paid-with-whop 2026-11-14 -->, from which you withdraw to a bank account, PayPal, Venmo, a mobile wallet or crypto, depending on your country<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/payouts 2026-11-14 -->.

You need no followers or application<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->. Before joining a task or requesting a payout, check that using DareBay and receiving payments are permitted under the laws that apply to you. Regional boards also offer payouts in local currency; compare the options for [India](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-india), [Indonesia](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-indonesia-and-the-philippines), Brazil and [Russia](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-russia-and-cis).

## How to read the table

A clipping platform is a marketplace where brands, streamers and creators fund campaigns, and clippers are paid per counted view. The "$ per 1,000 views" column is the advertised rate. What reaches you is that rate after the fee, only on clips past the view threshold, never above the cap, and only if the platform pays in your country. "Payout speed" shows when the money reaches you, as each platform's own pages describe it.

Each platform's figures, DareBay's included, come from its own pages, dated and checkable, never from another roundup. In the table, a number in brackets opens its source page, listed under that number for the platform in "How this comparison was built" with the date we read it. Elsewhere, the number points to "Sources" at the end of the article. "Not published" and "not stated" mean the platform's own pages do not state it.

A dollar figure in parentheses after a local-currency amount is our estimate: cross rates from the European Central Bank's euro reference rates of 23 September 2026, ₹95.74, Rp17,795 and R$5.13 per dollar<!-- source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist-90d.xml 2026-09-24 -->, and the Bank of Russia rate of 24 September 2026, 84.40 ₽ per dollar<!-- source: https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_daily.asp?date_req=24/09/2026 2026-09-24 -->.

The table lists the global boards A to Z, then the boards that pay in rupees, rupiah, reais or rubles, grouped by country; the order is not a ranking. Where a platform publishes a range, the table shows the whole range; each campaign or task sets its own rate.

## What other roundups get wrong

These claims recur in 2025–2026 roundups; each is set against what the platform's own pages said.

- **Vyro's rate and timing.** Most lists give Vyro $3 per 1,000 views, and most never say when earnings can be withdrawn. On 14 November 2026 its public board listed $1,000–$2,500 per 1M views, or $1.00–$2.50 per 1,000<!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-11-14 -->, and its help centre says earnings stay pending until the campaign ends and usually become available 7 to 10 days later<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/when-will-i-receive-my-earnings 2026-11-14 -->.
- **Whop Content Rewards' fee and timing.** Most lists leave out its fee, and several say it pays soon after approval. Under its terms of 13 November 2026, the first CPM round starts after both brand approval and minimum earnings; normally it has seven earning days and a three-day hold. Later rounds start after the previous one settles. CPM and Pool payouts carry a 10% fee, and Whop's withdrawal is separate<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->.
- **Who can be paid.** Most lists never say where Whop Content Rewards or Vyro cannot pay. Whop's sanctioned list includes Russia, Belarus and six regions of Ukraine<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-11-14 -->; Vyro lists 28 countries it does not pay, among them Russia, Belarus, Ukraine and Nigeria<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->; Try Shoot's list of 26 restricted countries includes Russia, Belarus and Ukraine<!-- source: https://tryshoot.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->.

## Why clipping became a market of its own

Tubefilter wrote in October 2025 that brands and creators embraced clipping because short-form viewing was growing much faster than long-form<!-- source: https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/10/14/mrbeast-vyro-clipping-platform-viewstats-expansion/ 2026-09-24 -->. In May 2026, Digiday called clipping "the growth hack of choice for creators" and said it had spawned its own cottage industry<!-- source: https://digiday.com/media/the-case-for-and-against-clipping/ 2026-09-24 -->. Digiday's May 2025 explainer said marketers usually paid clippers $1–$5 per 1,000 views, up to a preset cap<!-- source: https://digiday.com/media/wtf-is-clipping-the-low-lift-creator-strategy-grabbing-advertisers-attention/ 2026-09-24 -->. Most clipping, it noted, had historically been organized through private Discord servers, where you had to know someone who could add you<!-- source: https://digiday.com/media/wtf-is-clipping-the-low-lift-creator-strategy-grabbing-advertisers-attention/ 2026-09-24 -->.
