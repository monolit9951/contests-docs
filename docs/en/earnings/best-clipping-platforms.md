## How to read the table

A clipping platform is a marketplace where brands, streamers and creators fund campaigns, and clippers cut the source footage into short clips, post them on their own accounts and are paid per counted view. The rate per 1,000 views is the number every platform advertises, and it is the least useful one on its own. What reaches you is the rate after the platform's fee, only on clips that cleared the view threshold, and never above the cap per clip; and only if the platform can pay to your country at all.

So the table below puts the same nine fields next to each platform. A grey "not published" means the platform does not state that figure anywhere on its own pages; a superscript number links to the page the figure was read from, and the date on that link is the day we read it.

## Which platform is right for you

If you clip from Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan or elsewhere in CIS, start with the platforms that state they pay there; most of the large Western marketplaces exclude at least Russia and Belarus from payouts, and some exclude more. DareBay was built for exactly this audience and pays in USDT on TON or Telegram Stars.

If you clip from the US or the EU and want the largest choice of campaigns, join two or three of the big marketplaces at once: campaign budgets are finite, and a clipper who depends on one platform sits idle when its campaigns drain. Whatever you pick, check the fee on payout and the minimum payout before your first clip, because a $3 rate with a 25% fee and a high minimum can pay less than a $2 rate with none.

If you are new and have no audience, prefer platforms with no follower requirement and a clear, printed rate, threshold and cap per campaign, so that you can calculate the payout of a clip before you edit it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best clipping platform in 2026?

There is no single best platform; it depends on where you live and what you clip. For clippers in Russia and CIS, DareBay is one of the few platforms that pay there. For US and EU clippers who want the widest choice of campaigns, the large marketplaces in the table above have more supply. Compare the fee, cap and minimum payout, not only the rate.

### Which clipping platform pays the most per 1,000 views?

The highest advertised rates are not the highest net pay. A rate is reduced by the platform's fee, limited by the cap per clip and only paid on clips over the view threshold. The table shows all of these side by side; sort by the rate column and then read the fee and cap columns for the same row.

### Do I need followers to join a clipping platform?

On most campaign-based platforms, no: you are paid for the views of the clip you post, not for your follower count. Some campaigns require an account on a specific topic or a minimum account age; that is stated in the campaign terms. DareBay has no follower requirement.

### Do clipping platforms charge clippers a fee?

Many do, either as a percentage of the payout or as a withdrawal fee. The table lists the fee each platform publishes. DareBay charges 0% on contest payouts and 10% on balance withdrawals with a 10 USDT minimum request.

### Can I clip for several platforms at once?

Yes, and it is the sensible default: campaigns run out of budget, so having briefs from two or three sources keeps you working. Check each campaign's exclusivity rules before posting the same clip in two places.

### Which clipping platforms pay in Russia and CIS?

See the "Pays in Russia / CIS" column: it shows what each platform states about payouts to Russia, Belarus, Ukraine and Kazakhstan on its own pages. DareBay pays to all of them.
