## The best clipping platforms in 2026, in short

There is no single best clipping platform: the one that pays you depends on where you live, how you want to be paid and whether your clips pass a view threshold. In the US and the EU, look at Whop Content Rewards, where brands fund each campaign before it goes live<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/brands-terms 2026-09-24 -->, and Vyro, which pays by PayPal or Stripe<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-can-i-withdraw-my-earnings 2026-09-24 -->. In Russia and Belarus, which Whop Content Rewards<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-24 --> and Vyro<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-24 --> exclude, DareBay, which publishes this page, keeps no country list<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/legal/terms 2026-09-24 --> and needs no followers or application<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->. In [India](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-india), [Indonesia](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-indonesia-and-the-philippines), Brazil and [Russia](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-russia-and-cis), regional boards pay in the local currency.

**Global boards**

- **Choose Clipping.io if** you post clips on Snapchat, which its homepage names alongside TikTok, Instagram and X, though its public pages state no rate or fee and name no payout method<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Clipping.net if** you want to be paid by PayPal: its payments guide lets you add PayPal, or USDC or USDT on Ethereum<!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-24 -->, and each campaign sets which one it pays<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Clipster if** you want a wide choice on a public board: its Discover page listed 90 campaigns on 24 September 2026, at $0.05–$4 per 1,000 views<!-- source: https://www.clipster.gg/discover 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Cliptocash (cliptocash.com) if** you want a weekly payday: its terms say accumulated earnings are paid out every Monday, via Stripe or to your Whop balance<!-- source: https://cliptocash.com/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose DareBay if** you have no followers or clip from Russia, Belarus or elsewhere in CIS: a brand-new account can take a task with no application<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->, and CIS clippers are paid like everyone else<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose FindClout if** you run pages with a US or other tier-1 audience and can pass its application, which reviews your audience first<!-- source: https://app.findclout.com/ 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Prime Oracles if** you clip from a CIS state other than Ukraine: its terms name ten of them in a tax clause<!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-24 -->, and its AML policy adds enhanced checks for Russian and Belarusian residents<!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Promote.fun if** you want your earnings as USDC on Solana and can wait until a campaign ends: that is the payout its home page describes<!-- source: https://www.promote.fun/ 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Reach.cat if** you have no bank account: its clipper page says it works in 149+ countries with no bank required<!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Ssemble Clip Rewards if** you have a payout account in one of the 39 countries its Clip Rewards FAQ lists; its partner campaigns pay out on Whop instead<!-- source: https://www.ssemble.com/clip-rewards 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Vues if** your clips draw modest views: its own comparison table lists no minimum view count per clip<!-- source: https://vues.app/blog/best-clipping-platforms-that-pay-crypto 2026-09-24 -->, though a campaign can set a minimum payout per post<!-- source: https://vues.app/blog/how-cpm-payouts-work 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Vyro if** your clips regularly pass 5,000 views: each approved post starts earning only once it reaches that mark<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Whop Content Rewards if** you want your earnings in a bank account or mobile wallet: withdrawals go through Whop, which supports bank, mobile-wallet and crypto payouts in over 200 countries<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods 2026-09-24 -->.

- **Choose ClipConnect India if** you want to request a UPI withdrawal yourself as soon as your wallet reaches ₹500; it does not say when tracked views reach the wallet<!-- source: https://clipconnect.in/ 2026-09-24 -->.

- **Choose Clippo if** you are in Indonesia and want no platform fee on your earnings, since its terms charge clippers none when they withdraw<!-- source: https://app.clippo.id/terms-and-conditions 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Choose Konten.com if** you are in Indonesia and have no big following, since any clipper who joins its ordinary campaigns can submit clips<!-- source: https://konten.com/blog/kenapa-cuma-rank-tertentu-yang-bisa-ikut 2026-09-24 -->.

- **Choose Klipni (Клипни) if** you are registered in Russia as self-employed or as a sole trader on the simplified or patent tax system: under its terms, it pays rubles only to those statuses<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->.

## How to read the table

A clipping platform is a marketplace where brands, streamers and creators fund campaigns, and clippers are paid per counted view. The "$ per 1,000 views" column is the advertised rate. What reaches you is that rate after the fee, only on clips past the view threshold, never above the cap, and only if the platform pays in your country. "Payout speed" shows when the money reaches you, as each platform's own pages describe it.

Each platform's figures, DareBay's included, come from its own pages, dated and checkable, never from another roundup. In the table, a number in brackets opens its source page, listed under that number for the platform in "How this comparison was built" with the date we read it. Elsewhere, the number points to "Sources" at the end of the article. "Not published" and "not stated" mean the platform's own pages do not state it.

A dollar figure in parentheses after a local-currency amount is our estimate: cross rates from the European Central Bank's euro reference rates of 23 September 2026, ₹95.74, Rp17,795 and R$5.13 per dollar<!-- source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist-90d.xml 2026-09-24 -->, and the Bank of Russia rate of 24 September 2026, 84.40 ₽ per dollar<!-- source: https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_daily.asp?date_req=24/09/2026 2026-09-24 -->.

The table lists the global boards A to Z, then the boards that pay in rupees, rupiah, reais or rubles, grouped by country; the order is not a ranking. Where a platform publishes a range, the table shows the whole range; each campaign or task sets its own rate.

## What other roundups get wrong

These claims recur in 2025–2026 roundups; each is set against what the platform's own pages said.

- **Vyro's rate and timing.** Most lists give Vyro $3 per 1,000 views, and most never say when earnings can be withdrawn. On 24 September 2026 its live campaigns listed $1,000–$2,000 per 1M views, or $1.00–$2.00 per 1,000<!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-09-24 -->, and its help centre says earnings stay pending until the campaign ends and typically become withdrawable 7–10 days later<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/when-will-i-receive-my-earnings 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Whop Content Rewards' fee and timing.** Most lists leave out its fee, and several say it pays soon after approval. By its terms, that holds for per-post campaigns, but a CPM payout settles about 10 days after the brand approves the clip, less a 10% fee and before Whop's own withdrawal fee<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Who can be paid.** Most lists never say where Whop Content Rewards or Vyro cannot pay. Whop's sanctioned list includes Russia, Belarus and six regions of Ukraine<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-24 -->; Vyro does not support Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia or Moldova<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Rates per 100K or per 1M views.** Several lists put Clipping.net's per-100K rate beside per-1,000 rates. Its FAQ says rates are often $20–$75+ per 100K views, or $0.20–$0.75+ per 1,000<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-24 -->; Clipster's board listed $50–$4,000 per 1M views, or $0.05–$4 per 1,000, on 24 September 2026<!-- source: https://www.clipster.gg/discover 2026-09-24 -->. The table above shows both per 1,000.
- **ClipAffiliates.** Several lists still recommend it, but on checks of 24 and 25 September 2026 its site answered only with errors, so none of its fees, rates or terms could be checked on its own pages.

## Why clipping became a market of its own

Tubefilter wrote in October 2025 that brands and creators embraced clipping because short-form viewing was growing much faster than long-form<!-- source: https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/10/14/mrbeast-vyro-clipping-platform-viewstats-expansion/ 2026-09-24 -->. In May 2026, Digiday called clipping "the growth hack of choice for creators" and said it had spawned its own cottage industry<!-- source: https://digiday.com/media/the-case-for-and-against-clipping/ 2026-09-24 -->. Digiday's May 2025 explainer said marketers usually paid clippers $1–$5 per 1,000 views, up to a preset cap<!-- source: https://digiday.com/media/wtf-is-clipping-the-low-lift-creator-strategy-grabbing-advertisers-attention/ 2026-09-24 -->. Most clipping, it noted, had historically been organized through private Discord servers, where you had to know someone who could add you<!-- source: https://digiday.com/media/wtf-is-clipping-the-low-lift-creator-strategy-grabbing-advertisers-attention/ 2026-09-24 -->.

### Which clipping platforms pay per view in 2026?

### What is the best clipping platform in 2026?

It depends on where you live and how you want to be paid, so this page names no single winner. If you want a bank or PayPal payout, Whop Content Rewards pays out through Whop to a bank, mobile wallet or crypto<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods 2026-09-24 -->, and Vyro by PayPal or Stripe depending on your country<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-can-i-withdraw-my-earnings 2026-09-24 -->. On 24 September 2026, Prime Oracles' open rewards reached $8 per 1,000 views, for long YouTube reviews of its own platform; its clip cards listed $0.20–$1 per 1,000 views<!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards 2026-09-24 -->. ### Do clipping platforms charge clippers a fee?

Many do. Whop Content Rewards keeps 10% of each CPM payout, plus Whop's own withdrawal fee<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->; Prime Oracles takes 10% of each withdrawal<!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-24 -->; Klipni (Клипни) 5% of each approved payout<!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-24 -->; Ternak Klip 20% of payouts<!-- source: https://ternakklip.com/home 2026-09-24 -->; Konten.com 20% of gross earnings plus Rp10,000 per withdrawal<!-- source: https://konten.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->. DareBay, which publishes this page, takes 0% from task payouts; its only fee is 10% of what you withdraw, withdrawals start at 10 USDT, and you see the exact amount before you confirm<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/what-commission 2026-09-24 -->. Reach.cat's terms name about 10% on all CPM-based payouts<!-- source: https://reach.cat/terms 2026-09-24 -->, while its blog posts disagree on whether clippers keep 100%<!-- source: https://reach.cat/blog/best-clipping-platforms/ 2026-09-24 --> or 90%<!-- source: https://reach.cat/blog/whop-clipping-hidden-fees/ 2026-09-24 -->. Four state 0% for clippers: FindClout<!-- source: https://app.findclout.com/for-creators 2026-09-24 -->, Wondeed<!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-09-24 -->, ClipGrow<!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/for-brands 2026-09-24 --> and Clippo<!-- source: https://app.clippo.id/terms-and-conditions 2026-09-24 -->. Vyro, Promote.fun, Clipster and Clipping.net do not publish a clipper fee.

### Which clipping platform pays out fastest?

None can be ranked fastest: each starts the clock at a different point. Ssemble Clip Rewards' own campaigns make earnings payable 5 days after posting<!-- source: https://www.ssemble.com/clip-rewards 2026-09-24 -->; Reach.cat typically processes payouts 3–5 business days after final validation<!-- source: https://reach.cat/user-agreement 2026-09-24 -->; Whop Content Rewards settles CPM earnings about 10 days after brand approval<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->; Vyro makes earnings withdrawable typically 7–10 days after a campaign ends<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/when-will-i-receive-my-earnings 2026-09-24 -->. DareBay, which publishes this page, lets you lock in what a clip has earned before the task ends when the task shows Claim now, and that clip then stops earning<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/darebay-withdrawals 2026-09-24 -->; otherwise a wallet-backed task credits earnings when it ends<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/darebay-withdrawals 2026-09-24 -->.

### Do I need followers to join a clipping platform?

No, not on most clipping platforms: they pay for the views of the clip, not for your audience. Clipping.net<!-- source: https://clipping.net/ 2026-09-24 -->, DareBay<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->, Reach.cat<!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-24 -->, Ssemble Clip Rewards<!-- source: https://www.ssemble.com/clipper 2026-09-24 -->, Vues<!-- source: https://vues.app/ 2026-09-24 --> and Vyro<!-- source: https://vyro.com/about 2026-09-24 --> state no follower minimum. Clipster lets each campaign set one: 15 of the 90 it listed on 24 September 2026 set 100–1,000 followers per profile<!-- source: https://www.clipster.gg/discover 2026-09-24 -->. FindClout's FAQ asks for none<!-- source: https://app.findclout.com/faq 2026-09-24 -->, but its board is invite-only<!-- source: https://app.findclout.com/ 2026-09-24 -->. The networks' own programs differ: TikTok's needs 10,000 followers<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/eligibility 2026-09-24 --> and YouTube's 1,000 subscribers<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en 2026-09-24 -->.

### Can I clip for several platforms at once?

Yes, and it is the sensible default: campaigns run out of budget, so briefs from two or three sources keep you working. Check each campaign's exclusivity rules before posting the same clip in two places.

### Which clipping platforms pay in Russia and CIS?

Whop Content Rewards excludes Russia, Belarus and six Ukrainian regions<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-24 -->; Vyro excludes all of Russia, Belarus and Ukraine, plus Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-24 -->. DareBay, which publishes this page, keeps no country list<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->, so clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS are paid like everyone else. ### Do TikTok and YouTube pay per view too?

Yes, but each through a program of its own, not through brand campaigns: TikTok's Creator Rewards Program, YouTube Shorts revenue sharing, Facebook Content Monetization, Instagram bonuses, Snapchat's Monetization Program and X's Original Content Rewards.
