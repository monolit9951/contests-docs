## What changed on 9 October

[ClipGrow's current creator page](https://clipgrow.in/for-clippers) offers a fixed amount per approved clip at the editor's tier, paid monthly over UPI. You send the edit through its dashboard; ClipGrow publishes on its own Instagram, YouTube and Facebook pages. You do not need to build a personal account to get paid for this work. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/for-clippers 2026-10-12 -->

The [9 October terms](https://clipgrow.in/terms) expressly say that **no new campaign starts on per-view terms**. Campaigns begun earlier continue their old model until they end. The September ₹50 CPM / ₹2,000 cap examples in older accounts of ClipGrow describe those earlier campaigns, not a current fixed editing fee. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/terms 2026-10-12 -->

## Joining and getting a clip approved

The creator page describes a request to join a hiring campaign, followed by a conversation with its clip head. Once accepted, you edit one short vertical clip a day using supplied footage and the campaign SOP, then upload before midnight. An edit can be approved or returned with revision notes. The terms specify India time for that deadline. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/for-clippers 2026-10-12 --> <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/terms 2026-10-12 -->

Before accepting an offer, record the fixed rate, expected daily output, what counts as approval, whether revisions are included and what happens on a missed day. A high per-clip figure can be a poor hourly rate if revisions repeatedly double the editing time.

## How to calculate fixed-pay editing work

Suppose a written offer pays **₹200 per approved clip**. This is a hypothetical offer, not a ClipGrow rate. In a 30-day month, 26 approved clips would produce ₹5,200 before any specified deduction or tax; four missed or unapproved clips do not contribute ₹800 simply because you planned to make them.

If each approved edit takes 45 minutes and you spend another five hours on revisions and uploading, the month's 24.5 working hours produce about **₹212 per hour before deductions and tax**. Compare that with an alternative editing job using the same time budget. Viral views on the posts do not raise the fixed payment.

The current creator page states that joining and getting paid carry no ClipGrow fee. It lists UPI payment through GPay, PhonePe or Paytm after the month ends, and tier progression for consistent work. The actual campaign offer supplies the amount. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/for-clippers 2026-10-12 -->

## Why old Sunday and CPM advice still appears

Legacy campaigns pay verified views on the editor's own posts. The current terms retain a **Sunday UPI payout rule from ₹500** for those earlier campaigns, and close a video's earnings permanently when it is paid. That rule does not replace the monthly cycle for new fixed-pay work. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/terms 2026-10-12 -->

Before following an old guide, identify which campaign you joined and its start date. A legacy ₹50-per-1,000 rate, for example, would give ₹2,000 at 40,000 eligible views if its cap were ₹2,000. A new ₹200-per-approved-clip offer pays ₹200 even if the published post later reaches 40,000 views. The same footage can sit under different contracts.

## Rights and the identity of the service

ClipGrow describes itself as a short-video marketing agency operating from Kerala, India. The public homepage now describes client retainers and distribution through pages it runs, rather than a pure CPM marketplace. This is the operator's description; it is not an independent payment audit. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/ 2026-10-12 -->

For new work, save the supplied assets and SOP with the campaign offer. Check permission for music, stock footage and other added elements before delivering. Do not assume that an old campaign's permissions cover a different client's paid promotion.

## ClipGrow or a pay-per-view platform

ClipGrow's new model suits an editor who wants supplied footage and a fixed amount for approved work without running a social account. The trade-off is daily output, approval and revision work, followed by a monthly payment cycle.

A pay-per-view task suits a creator who wants to publish on their own channel and earn according to eligible reach. On DareBay, a task sets its rate per 1,000 eligible views, its threshold and any per-clip cap. Task payouts carry 0% commission; the 10% fee applies at withdrawal, with requests from 10 USDT and payouts in USDT on TON or Telegram Stars. Those DareBay facts retain their 24 September source dates. <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 --> <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/what-commission 2026-09-24 -->

## Frequently asked questions

### Does ClipGrow still pay per 1,000 views?

Only for campaigns started before 9 October 2026, until those campaigns end. Its current terms say new campaigns use fixed pay per approved clip.

### Do I need Instagram followers to work with ClipGrow?

Its new editor flow requires no personal social account: you upload the edit, and ClipGrow publishes it. A legacy per-view campaign can still require a connected account.

### Is the payout Sunday or monthly?

New fixed-pay work is paid after the month ends over UPI. The Sunday rule and ₹500 floor in the terms apply to legacy per-view campaigns.

### How much does one approved clip pay?

The campaign offer and your tier decide it. The current public creator page does not publish a universal rupee amount; request the amount and revision conditions before estimating income.
