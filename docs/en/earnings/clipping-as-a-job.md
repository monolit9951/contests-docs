# Clipping as a job - how to earn from video clips

A clipper takes a brief on the platform, films a short video (a stream clip, a review, a challenge) and publishes it on TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels with the required hashtag. Payment is for real views: from **$0.30**<!-- baseline: ppv_cpm_min --> to **$1.00 per 1000 views**<!-- baseline: ppv_cpm_max -->. Followers and experience are not needed - the money follows the reach of one specific clip. The budget is frozen on the platform before the work starts, so the buyer cannot walk away without paying.

## A worked example

Say you take a brief at $0.50<!-- baseline: ppv_cpm_median --> per 1000 views and cut a stream clip. The clip reaches 30,000 counted views (above the threshold). The maths: 30,000 / 1000 * $0.50<!-- baseline: ppv_cpm_median --> = **$15** for one submission. The cap per submission in current contests is $50<!-- baseline: ppv_max_per_work_typical -->, well above this example, so the whole amount is paid out. Several submissions to one contest are allowed where the rules permit. Three clips at the same reach is already $45.

This is a hypothetical but realistic scenario. Your actual figure depends on the contest rate and on your clip's views. The rate is always on the brief card before you submit.

## How to start: 5 steps

1. **Sign up at [DareBay](https://darebay.com)** - the account is free and no channel verification is needed
2. **Pick a brief** - the contest catalogue shows the rate, the budget, the subject and the deadlines
3. **Film the video to the brief** - a stream clip, a review, a challenge or any other format the contest asks for
4. **Publish it with the hashtag** - TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels; the hashtag is named in the brief
5. **Submit the work to the contest** - once it ends, the platform counts the views and credits the payment automatically

## Why this is safe

The platform works as intermediary and guarantor. The buyer freezes the budget when creating the brief - the money sits on the platform and cannot be pulled back before the contest ends. Views are counted by the platform automatically through the platforms' APIs, not by the buyer by hand. The payout runs without the organizer. This is not "take our word for it", it is a mechanic in which the buyer physically cannot avoid paying for the views you earned.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an audience to start?

No. Followers do not affect the payment. You are paid for the views of one specific clip, not for the size of your channel. TikTok and YouTube Shorts algorithms surface clips in recommendations regardless of follower count, so even a new account can rack up views.

### How much can I earn?

It depends on the contest rate and on your clips' views. Rates across live contests run from $0.30<!-- baseline: ppv_cpm_min --> to $1.00<!-- baseline: ppv_cpm_max --> per 1000 views. Nobody can guarantee a figure in advance, because it depends on your content's reach. We publish the offer parameters, we do not promise an income.

### How do I get the money?

DareBay supports several payout methods: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts. You pick the one you want in settings.

### Is it safe? Could I be scammed?

The budget is frozen on the platform before the work starts. The buyer takes no part in the payout - the money is split automatically by formula. The platform acts as intermediary and guarantor so that neither side gets scammed.

### What is the view threshold?

The default is **1000 views**<!-- baseline: ppv_default_min_views_threshold -->. In current live contests the actual threshold is **2000 views**<!-- baseline: ppv_min_views_threshold_live -->. The threshold exists to filter out submissions with a handful of views and to split the budget between authors with real reach.

### What do I need to take part?

A DareBay account (free) and an account on the platform you publish to (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram). No experience, portfolio, channel verification or follower minimum is required.

### Can I submit more than one clip?

It depends on the contest rules. Many contests allow several submissions from one author. The terms are on the brief card.
