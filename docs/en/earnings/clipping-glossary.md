Clipping runs on jargon: CPM or per thousand, threshold or cap, claim now or withdrawal. This glossary collects 52 terms from DareBay task cards, payout rules and clipper chats, each explained in a sentence or two.

The definitions follow the platform's own rules: every figure comes from DareBay's data as of 23 August 2026 and matches the task cards. Every term has its own anchor you can link to from a chat. For the whole mechanic, start with [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

## The terms people mix up most often

### CPM and rate per view

In advertising, CPM is the price of a thousand impressions paid to a platform. In clipping it is the rate a buyer pays a clipper for 1000 counted views, seen from the clipper's side. A price per single view is the same thing: $0.001 per view and $1 per 1000 views are one rate. Compare platforms per thousand; the spread is in [how much clipping pays](/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays).

### Threshold and cap

The threshold looks at views, the cap at money. The threshold is the minimum without which a clip is not paid at all: typically 2000 views in open tasks, 1000 by default, never deducted. The cap is the most one video can earn: the typical cap is $100, and a second clip is counted on its own with no limit per author. 20,000 views at a $1 rate make 20,000 / 1000 × $1.00 = **$20.00**, well under the $100 cap; the threshold check is in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

### Counted and displayed views

A displayed view is the figure in your account statistics the moment you look. A counted view is what the platform's counter read from the site's public counter by your link and what survived moderation: suspicious growth puts a work on hold, confirmed fraud is filtered out. Only the second kind is paid; screenshots play no part.

### Offer and pay-per-view task

A pay-per-view task pays one thing: the rate per 1000 counted views, up to the cap. An offer task adds a fixed fee per accepted clip and a share of sales through your affiliate link or promo code, often with a ready-made uniqueized setup. All three parts are on the card; brands run CPA offers on DareBay directly, no affiliate network in between.

### Withdrawal and Stars

A withdrawal is a request for USDT on the TON network: from 10 USDT, a withdrawal fee of 10% of the requested amount, processed by hand, a personal rate possible. Stars are the second route in the same wizard: the amount goes out as Telegram Stars and stays inside Telegram, so it is not a withdrawal. Both routes use the available balance once fresh earnings have matured for 24 hours; details on the [withdrawals page](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals).

## Frequently asked questions

### How is clipping different from video editing?

An editor hands the client a file and gets a flat fee; the clip lives on someone else's channel. A clipper posts the clip on their own account and earns from its views, so the moment, the hook and the watch time matter more than clean cuts. On offer tasks there is no editing at all. The craft as a whole is on [clipping as a job](/en/earnings/clipping-as-a-job).

### What does CPM mean on a clipping task?

It is the rate per 1000 counted views of a clip, and a DareBay card labels it exactly that way: per 1000 views. One number for every participant, set by the buyer and unchanged after launch. Divide the views by a thousand, multiply by the rate, check against the cap per video.

### Do I need all these terms before my first task?

No. Five are enough: rate, threshold, cap, requirements and submitting the work by link. The rest arrives as you go: offer and setup with your first offer task, withdrawal and maturing with your first earnings. The first task itself is on [where to find clipping work](/en/earnings/where-to-find-clipping-work).
