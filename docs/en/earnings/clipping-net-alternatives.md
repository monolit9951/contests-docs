## A payout cycle is not a withdrawal button

Clipping.net's current terms tie payments to the campaign cycle closing and sponsor review/approval. Clearing a view threshold does not establish the payout date, and a fixed withdrawal-button deadline should not be assumed.<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-10-12 -->

For a **hypothetical** brief with a 1000-view minimum per post and 25,000 qualifying views across your campaign posts, 20 posts at 1300 each give 26,000 and meet both gates. At an assumed $40 per 100,000 views that is $10.40 gross, still subject to the cycle and approval. Twenty posts at 900 each do not pass the per-post gate even though their total is 18,000. Compare alternatives by the event that makes funds payable, not merely their posted rate.

## Is Clipping.net legit? What its own pages said in September and October 2026

Clipping.net's own pages describe a clipping network with public campaign pages and written clipper terms, and they leave one question open. A caveat first: on 14 November 2026 the site answered automated readers with a security check instead of its pages, so this section quotes dated archive copies of them. Open the live page before you rely on a figure. <!-- source: https://clipping.net/ 2026-11-14 -->

**Who operates it: not published.** The Terms of Service, last updated on 1 January 2026, are an agreement "between you and CLIPPING" and give an email address and "CLIPPING Legal Department" as the contact. They name no company, no country and no governing law. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261003063715/https://clipping.net/policies/terms-of-service 2026-10-03 -->

**What it pays.** Each campaign sets a rate per 100,000 views. The FAQ puts it "often in the $20 to $75+ per 100K views range", which is $0.20–$0.75+ per 1,000. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260917191233/https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-17 --> Bounties go higher: one campaign page advertised up to $600 per 100K on 12 October. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261012161559/https://clipping.net/c/elf-x-clipping 2026-10-12 -->

**When it pays.** The Clipper Terms say "There is no withdraw button". Everyone in a campaign is paid in one run after the sponsor closes a payout cycle, a review has removed bad actors and the sponsor has approved the payout sheet, and "There is no fixed timeframe" for a cycle. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261003063735/https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-10-03 --> The FAQ adds that payouts follow "typically within one to two weeks" of a cycle closing. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260917191233/https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-17 -->

**Payout methods and minimum.** The homepage names PayPal, or USDC and USDT on Ethereum. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261006124622/https://clipping.net/ 2026-10-06 --> The terms add that each campaign pays by one method and "alternatives are not offered". No minimum amount is published; the floor is counted in views: a post needs 1,000 to count and a campaign typically 25,000 in total. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261003063735/https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-10-03 --> A campaign page can ask for more; the one cited above set 100,000. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261012161559/https://clipping.net/c/elf-x-clipping 2026-10-12 -->

**Countries.** There is no list. You must be "a legal resident in the territories where we operate" and at least 18. <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261003063735/https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-10-03 -->

**Read these conditions first.** Clips "must remain public and active until payment is received". A payment sent to outdated details "cannot be reissued". A fraud finding brings forfeiture of pending payouts and a clawback of payouts already made, and "Staff decisions are final". <!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261003063735/https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-10-03 -->

The short answer: the network and its rules are public, the company behind it is not, and the payout date is the sponsor's call.

## How Clipping.net works and why clippers look elsewhere

Clipping.net is a clipping network around streamer and brand campaigns: connect TikTok, Reels, Shorts or X, join a campaign, cut, post on your own account; counters are read over the networks' APIs with viewbot detection<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. No following or application, 18+.

Rates are quoted per 100,000 views, the calculator working between $20 and $75+ per 100K<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. Two thresholds apply: 1,000 views per post and usually 25,000 per campaign<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->, and the live Kick and Drake cards ask for 100,000 views to qualify<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->.

Payouts are cycle-based: no withdraw button, the sponsor closes the cycle, cycles run for weeks or months<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->. Money goes out by PayPal or in USDC or USDT on Ethereum only<!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-05 -->. Its FAQ says joining is free and "You keep your earnings minus any team commission you opt into"<!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260917191233/https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-17 -->, and instead of a country list the terms require legal residence where the network operates<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->. Clippers leave because the model is built for volume, not because its rules are hidden: low rate, high entry bar, money once per cycle.

## What separates the alternatives: four axes

**Threshold and cap.** Vyro pays after 5,000 views per post on most campaigns, and each brief states its own minimum<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-11-14 -->; Whop has no platform-wide threshold, the brand sets a minimum payout per video<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/memberships-and-access/third-party-apps/content-rewards 2026-09-02 -->. On DareBay the task owner sets it, 1,000 views by default, and it is not deducted: clear it and every view is paid. Caps: up to $1,000 per clip on Vyro per its help pages<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-11-14 -->, though its Creator Terms of 28 October 2026 say view payouts have no per-clip cap<!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->; on Whop the cap is set per campaign and per network, its help centre calls $200 the usual cap, and the campaign pages checked on 24 September 2026 ran from $80 to $1,500 per clip<!-- source: https://help.contentrewards.com/en/articles/15319800-which-pricing-model-should-i-use 2026-09-24 -->; on DareBay a task can set a cap stated on the task card.

**Who holds the budget.** Whop keeps the brand's budget against the campaign<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/ 2026-09-18 -->, Klipni takes prepayment from the brand by invoice<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/advertiser 2026-09-21 -->, Vues states that campaign money stays with the brand<!-- source: https://vues.app/terms 2026-09-02 -->; Reach.cat's brands prepay the budget plus 10%, but no terms say how it is held<!-- source: https://reach.cat/blog/reach-cat-pricing-brands-2026/ 2026-09-18 -->; Clipping.net does not say, and Clipping.io describes no upfront budget: its homepage tells brands they are charged only once their content reaches the views they need<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-24 -->. On DareBay a wallet-backed contest has its budget locked on the platform before the start; a manual-payout contest, where the organizer pays, is labelled on the card.

**Countries and payout rails.** Whop's sanctioned list blocks Russia, Belarus and six regions of Ukraine<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-18 -->, Vyro lists 28 countries it does not pay, among them Russia, Belarus, all of Ukraine and Nigeria<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->; Clipping.net, Vues, Reach.cat and Clipping.io publish no list. Rails: a Whop wallet at Vyro since 2 November 2026, from which you withdraw to a bank account, PayPal, Venmo, a mobile wallet or crypto, depending on your country<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/payouts 2026-11-14 -->, USDT at Reach.cat, whose creator FAQ says bank transfer is coming "very soon"<!-- source: https://reach.cat/creator 2026-09-24 -->, rubles to a self-employed person's or sole trader's bank account at Klipni<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay keeps no country list: it pays USDT on TON, or you receive the payout in Telegram Stars; request a withdrawal from 10 USDT and the platform does the rest, with 10% (default) of what you withdraw deducted inside the request.

**Follower gates.** None of the eight requires followers; the gates sit elsewhere: some Whop campaigns need an application<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-18 -->, Reach.cat's terms require an identity check once total payout requests pass $500, while its clipper page says no ID check<!-- source: https://reach.cat/terms 2026-09-24 -->, Klipni needs Russian self-employed status<!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->, and a Klipni campaign may set its own follower minimum<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. On DareBay a brand-new account can take a task, and the clip counts after you send the link.

## Seven alternatives

**Clipping.io.** Hard to say who it suits: its public pages show no rates, thresholds, fees, minimums or countries, and payout methods only as logo images<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-24 -->; its homepage names Snapchat and X alongside TikTok and Instagram. The minus: its public terms are a generic template that says nothing about pay<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/terms-of-use 2026-09-24 -->.

**DareBay.** For clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS, since it keeps no country list, and for beginners with no audience: a task-set rate per 1,000 views, threshold and any cap on the card, 0% fee on contest payouts and 10% (default) of what you withdraw; withdrawals start at 10 USDT. Offer tasks can add a fixed fee per accepted clip and a share of sales, and hand you ready-made videos with uniqueization when enabled by the task. Every field: [DareBay fact sheet](/en/about/darebay-at-a-glance).

**Klipni.** For Russian self-employed clippers who want rubles in a bank account: the rate is set per campaign, and the platform's own calculator defaults to 30 ₽ per 1,000 views with presets from 20 to 60 ₽<!-- source: https://klipni.com/calculator 2026-09-21 -->, a 5% fee on each approved payout, no lower limit<!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->, money within five business days once the bank accepts the payout<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. VK Clips count too, and there are apps in the App Store and RuStore<!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->. The minus: Russian self-employed or sole-trader status required, and the view threshold and the cap are set per campaign, with no platform-wide figure published<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. Head to head: [DareBay vs Klipni](/en/earnings/darebay-vs-klipni).

**Reach.cat.** For USDT to a crypto wallet: $1–$3 per 1,000 promised to new clippers<!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, no view minimum in its terms or FAQs<!-- source: https://reach.cat/creator 2026-09-24 -->, the brand pays 10% on top of the budget<!-- source: https://reach.cat/blog/reach-cat-pricing-brands-2026/ 2026-09-18 -->, it publishes no minimum amount, 149+ countries claimed<!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, and approved payouts are typically processed within 3–5 business days<!-- source: https://reach.cat/user-agreement 2026-09-24 -->. The minus: Russia, Belarus and six regions of Ukraine are on the sanctioned list<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-18 -->. ## Which one to pick

**A beginner with no audience.** You need a threshold per clip and a payout you trigger yourself. On Clipping.net the real gate is 25,000 per campaign and the 100,000 bars on the cards. On DareBay, where a task shows "Claim now", an approved submission can be locked in once its earnings are 24 hours old, and you file the withdrawal request from 10 USDT yourself. Checklist: [nine questions before choosing a platform](/en/earnings/how-to-choose-a-clipping-platform).

**A clipper in Russia, Ukraine or CIS.** Clipping.net names no countries, so ask support in writing before your first clip; Whop is closed to Russia and Belarus, Vyro to Ukraine as well. DareBay keeps no country list and pays clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS, in USDT on TON, or you receive the payout in Telegram Stars. Russian self-employed clippers can use Klipni; Prime Oracles' terms name ten CIS states in a tax clause, Ukraine not among them<!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-24 -->. The rest: [comparison of clipping platforms](/en/earnings/best-clipping-platforms).

**A high-volume clipper.** If you pull hundreds of thousands of views a week, Clipping.net's model works for you: the bars are within reach and pot-style campaigns pay by share of views. Vyro's help pages cap a clip at up to $1,000<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-11-14 -->, while its Creator Terms of 28 October 2026 say view payouts have no per-clip cap<!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->. On DareBay a task can set a cap stated on the task card, so volume comes from several tasks in parallel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does Clipping.net quote rates per 100,000 views?

That is how its cards and calculator work<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. Divide by 100: "up to $40 per 100K" is $0.40 per 1,000. On DareBay and Whop the card already shows the rate per 1,000; Vyro's cards show it per 1M views and its briefs per 1,000<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/why-arent-my-earnings-matching-the-cpm-on-the-campaign 2026-11-14 -->.

### What does "min 100K views to qualify" mean on a Clipping.net campaign?

It is the entry condition for a payout, above the usual 25,000 per campaign<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->: until the bar is met nothing is paid; ask the campaign's Discord how it is counted. Prefer a threshold per clip: set by each task on DareBay, 1,000 views by default, and 5,000 per post on most Vyro campaigns<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-11-14 -->.

### Which countries does Clipping.net pay to?

It publishes no country list: the terms require legal residence where it operates<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->, and it pays by PayPal or in USDC and USDT on Ethereum<!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-05 -->. DareBay keeps no country list: only people on sanctions lists are barred<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->; Klipni pays Russian self-employed clippers.

### How is DareBay different from Clipping.net for a clipper?

The threshold sits on the clip, not the campaign: each task sets it and prints it on the card, then every view is paid by the formula views / 1,000 × rate, never above any cap the task sets. DareBay's tasks pay a rate set by each task per 1,000 eligible views, while Clipping.net's own FAQ says its rates are often $0.20–$0.75+<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-24 -->, and an archived copy of one campaign page advertised up to $600 per 100K views with bounties on 12 October 2026, which is $6 per 1,000<!-- source: https://web.archive.org/web/20261012161559/https://clipping.net/c/elf-x-clipping 2026-10-12 -->. On DareBay you file the withdrawal request yourself: the fee is 10% (default) of what you withdraw, and withdrawals start at 10 USDT. DareBay also keeps no country list.
