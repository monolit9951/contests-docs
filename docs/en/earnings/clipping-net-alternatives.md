## How Clipping.net works and why clippers look elsewhere

Clipping.net is a clipping network around streamer and brand campaigns: connect TikTok, Reels, Shorts or X, join a campaign, cut, post on your own account; counters are read over the networks' APIs with viewbot detection <!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. No following or application, 18+.

Rates are quoted per 100,000 views, the calculator working between $20 and $75+ per 100K <!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. Two thresholds apply: 1,000 views per post and usually 25,000 per campaign <!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->, and the live Kick and Drake cards ask for 100,000 views to qualify <!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->.

Payouts are cycle-based: no withdraw button, the sponsor closes the cycle, cycles run for weeks or months <!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->. Money goes out by PayPal or in USDC or USDT on Ethereum only <!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-05 -->. No clipper fee is named, and instead of a country list the terms require legal residence where the network operates <!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->. Clippers leave because the model is built for volume, not because anything is hidden: low rate, high entry bar, money once per cycle.

## What separates the alternatives: four axes

**Threshold and cap.** Vyro pays after 5,000 views per post <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-09-02 -->; Whop has no platform-wide threshold, the brand sets a minimum payout per video <!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/memberships-and-access/third-party-apps/content-rewards 2026-09-02 -->. On DareBay the task owner sets it: 1,000 views by default, typically 2,000, not deducted: clear it and every view is paid. Caps: 1 million views per clip on Vyro <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-02 -->, $400–$600 per video in live Whop examples <!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/creators 2026-09-02 -->, typically $100 on DareBay, up to $500 in large tasks.

**Who holds the budget.** Whop keeps the brand's budget against the campaign <!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/brand 2026-09-02 -->, Klipni takes prepayment <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/advertiser 2026-09-02 -->, Vues states that campaign money stays with the brand <!-- source: https://vues.app/terms 2026-09-02 -->; Clipping.net, Reach.cat and Clipping.io say nothing. On DareBay a wallet-backed contest has its budget locked on the platform before the start; a manual-payout contest, where the organizer pays, is labelled on the card.

**Countries and payout rails.** Whop's sanctioned list blocks Russia, Belarus and Ukraine <!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-02 -->, Vyro's unsupported list adds Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-02 -->; Clipping.net, Vues, Reach.cat and Clipping.io publish no list. Rails: PayPal or Stripe at Vyro <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-can-i-withdraw-my-earnings 2026-09-02 -->, weekly USDT at Reach.cat <!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, a self-employed card at Klipni <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-02 -->. DareBay pays worldwide: USDT on TON or Telegram Stars, withdrawal requests from 10 USDT, a 10% withdrawal fee, manual processing.

**Follower gates.** None of the eight requires followers; the gates sit elsewhere: some Whop campaigns need an application <!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/creators 2026-09-02 -->, Reach.cat asks for identity verification before withdrawal <!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, Klipni needs Russian self-employed status <!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-02 -->. On DareBay a brand-new account can take a task, and the clip counts after you send the link.

## Seven alternatives

**DareBay.** For clippers anywhere, above all in CIS, and for beginners with no audience: $1–$2 per 1,000 views in open tasks, threshold typically 2,000, 0% fee on contest payouts, withdrawals from 10 USDT with a 10% fee. Offer tasks add a fixed fee per accepted clip and a share of sales, hand you ready-made videos with built-in uniqueization and let you work with CPA offers directly. The minus: young platform, fewer open tasks than Clipping.net has campaigns. Every field: [DareBay fact sheet](/en/about/darebay-at-a-glance).

**Whop Content Rewards.** For US and EU clippers who want the most campaigns: $0.50–$5 per 1,000 <!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-02 -->, 10% of CPM payouts and 25%→7% tiers in the FAQ <!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/creators 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: Russia, Belarus and Ukraine are on the sanctioned list <!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-02 -->. More: [Whop alternatives](/en/earnings/whop-content-rewards-alternatives).

**Vyro.** For clippers of large US creators: $1–$2 per 1,000 <!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-09-02 -->, 5,000 views per post <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-09-02 -->, a cap of 1 million views per clip <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova are unsupported <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-02 -->, and no clipper fee is published <!-- source: https://www.vyro.com/clipper-terms 2026-09-02 -->.

**Vues.** For crypto or PayPal payouts with no audience requirement: the rate shows only in the app, site example $3.00 per 1,000 <!-- source: https://vues.app/campaigns/polymarket 2026-09-02 -->, 0% from the clipper and 10% from the brand, plus an unpublished withdrawal processing fee <!-- source: https://vues.app/affiliate 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: campaign money stays with the brand <!-- source: https://vues.app/terms 2026-09-02 -->; threshold and country list unpublished.

**Reach.cat.** For weekly USDT payouts: $1–$3 per 1,000 promised to new clippers <!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, payment kicks in at 1,000 views <!-- source: https://reach.cat/creator 2026-09-02 -->, the brand pays 10% <!-- source: https://reach.cat/clipping-fee-calculator/ 2026-09-02 -->, 149+ countries claimed <!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: its own pages disagree on rates and identity checks; clipper fee and minimum payout unpublished.

**Klipni.** For Russian self-employed clippers who want rubles on a card: site examples of 50 ₽ per 1,000 views, a 5% fee on each approved payout, no lower limit <!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-02 -->, money on the card within five business days <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: Russian self-employed or sole-trader status required <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-02 -->, no view threshold published.

**Clipping.io.** Hard to say who it suits: the public pages show no rates, thresholds, fees, minimums, payout methods or countries <!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-02 -->, though Snapchat and X are counted alongside TikTok, Instagram and YouTube <!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: the terms open only after login.

## Which one to pick

**A beginner with no audience.** You need a threshold per clip and a payout you trigger yourself. On Clipping.net the real gate is 25,000 per campaign and the 100,000 bars on the cards. On DareBay an approved submission can be locked in with "Claim now", fresh accruals mature for 24 hours, and you file the withdrawal request from 10 USDT yourself. Checklist: [nine questions before choosing a platform](/en/earnings/how-to-choose-a-clipping-platform).

**A clipper in Russia, Ukraine or CIS.** Clipping.net names no countries, so ask support in writing before your first clip; Whop and Vyro are closed. DareBay pays worldwide in USDT on TON or Telegram Stars. Russian self-employed clippers can use Klipni; Prime Oracles serves Russia, Belarus and Kazakhstan <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-02 -->. The rest: [ranking of clipping platforms](/en/earnings/best-clipping-platforms).

**A high-volume clipper.** If you pull hundreds of thousands of views a week, Clipping.net's model works for you: the bars are within reach and pot-style campaigns pay by share of views. Vyro caps a clip at 1 million views <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-02 -->. On DareBay the cap per clip is typically $100, up to $500 in large tasks, so volume comes from several tasks in parallel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does Clipping.net quote rates per 100,000 views?

That is how its cards and calculator work <!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. Divide by 100: "up to $40 per 100K" is $0.40 per 1,000. On DareBay, Whop and Vyro the card already shows the rate per 1,000.

### What does "min 100K views to qualify" mean on a Clipping.net campaign?

It is the entry condition for a payout, above the usual 25,000 per campaign <!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->: until the bar is met nothing is paid; ask the campaign's Discord how it is counted. Prefer a threshold per clip: typically 2,000 views on DareBay, 5,000 per post on Vyro.

### Which countries does Clipping.net pay to?

It publishes no country list: the terms require legal residence where it operates <!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->, and it pays by PayPal or in USDC and USDT on Ethereum <!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-05 -->. DareBay pays with no exclusion list; Klipni pays Russian self-employed clippers.

### How is DareBay different from Clipping.net for a clipper?

The threshold sits on the clip, not the campaign: typically 2,000 views, then every view is paid by the formula views / 1,000 × rate, never above the cap. Open tasks pay $1–$2 per 1,000 against $0.20–$0.75 on Clipping.net <!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->, and you file the withdrawal request from 10 USDT yourself with a 10% withdrawal fee. DareBay pays worldwide but has fewer open tasks.
