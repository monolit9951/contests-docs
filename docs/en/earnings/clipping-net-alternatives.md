## How Clipping.net works and why clippers look elsewhere

Clipping.net is a clipping network around streamer and brand campaigns: connect TikTok, Reels, Shorts or X, join a campaign, cut, post on your own account; counters are read over the networks' APIs with viewbot detection<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. No following or application, 18+.

Rates are quoted per 100,000 views, the calculator working between $20 and $75+ per 100K<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. Two thresholds apply: 1,000 views per post and usually 25,000 per campaign<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->, and the live Kick and Drake cards ask for 100,000 views to qualify<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->.

Payouts are cycle-based: no withdraw button, the sponsor closes the cycle, cycles run for weeks or months<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->. Money goes out by PayPal or in USDC or USDT on Ethereum only<!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-05 -->. No clipper fee is named, and instead of a country list the terms require legal residence where the network operates<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->. Clippers leave because the model is built for volume, not because anything is hidden: low rate, high entry bar, money once per cycle.

## What separates the alternatives: four axes

**Threshold and cap.** Vyro pays after 5,000 views per post<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-09-02 -->; Whop has no platform-wide threshold, the brand sets a minimum payout per video<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/memberships-and-access/third-party-apps/content-rewards 2026-09-02 -->. On DareBay the task owner sets it, 1,000 views by default, and it is not deducted: clear it and every view is paid. Caps: 1 million views per clip on Vyro<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-02 -->; on Whop the cap is set per campaign and per network, its help centre calls $200 the usual cap, and the campaign pages checked on 24 September 2026 ran from $80 to $1,500 per clip<!-- source: https://help.contentrewards.com/en/articles/15319800-which-pricing-model-should-i-use 2026-09-24 -->; on DareBay a task can set a cap of up to $500 per clip.

**Who holds the budget.** Whop keeps the brand's budget against the campaign<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/ 2026-09-18 -->, Klipni takes prepayment from the brand by invoice<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/advertiser 2026-09-21 -->, Vues states that campaign money stays with the brand<!-- source: https://vues.app/terms 2026-09-02 -->; Reach.cat's brands prepay the budget plus 10%, but no terms say how it is held<!-- source: https://reach.cat/blog/reach-cat-pricing-brands-2026/ 2026-09-18 -->; Clipping.net does not say, and Clipping.io describes no upfront budget: its homepage tells brands they are charged only once their content reaches the views they need<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-24 -->. On DareBay a wallet-backed contest has its budget locked on the platform before the start; a manual-payout contest, where the organizer pays, is labelled on the card.

**Countries and payout rails.** Whop's sanctioned list blocks Russia, Belarus and six regions of Ukraine<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-18 -->, Vyro's unsupported list covers Russia, Belarus, all of Ukraine, Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-02 -->; Clipping.net, Vues, Reach.cat and Clipping.io publish no list. Rails: PayPal or Stripe at Vyro<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-can-i-withdraw-my-earnings 2026-09-02 -->, USDT at Reach.cat, whose creator FAQ says bank transfer is coming "very soon"<!-- source: https://reach.cat/creator 2026-09-24 -->, rubles to a self-employed person's or sole trader's bank account at Klipni<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay keeps no country list: it pays USDT on TON, or you receive the payout in Telegram Stars; withdrawals start at 10 USDT, the fee is 10% of what you withdraw, and the team processes each request.

**Follower gates.** None of the eight requires followers; the gates sit elsewhere: some Whop campaigns need an application<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-18 -->, Reach.cat's terms require an identity check once total payout requests pass $500, while its clipper page says no ID check<!-- source: https://reach.cat/terms 2026-09-24 -->, Klipni needs Russian self-employed status<!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->, and a Klipni campaign may set its own follower minimum<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. On DareBay a brand-new account can take a task, and the clip counts after you send the link.

## Seven alternatives

**DareBay.** For clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS, since it keeps no country list, and for beginners with no audience: $1–$10 per 1,000 views and a view threshold, both set by each task, up to $500 per clip, 0% fee on contest payouts and 10% of what you withdraw; withdrawals start at 10 USDT. Offer tasks can add a fixed fee per accepted clip and a share of sales, and hand you ready-made videos with built-in uniqueization. The minus: a young platform, with fewer campaigns than Clipping.net so far. Every field: [DareBay fact sheet](/en/about/darebay-at-a-glance).

**Whop Content Rewards.** For US and EU clippers who want dozens of brand-funded campaigns: the first 50 campaigns on its Discover board on 24 September 2026 listed $0.05–$3 per 1,000 views for their best-paying network, most often $1<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-24 -->, and its terms take 10% of CPM payouts<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->. The minus: Russia, Belarus and six regions of Ukraine are on the sanctioned list<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-18 -->. More: [Whop alternatives](/en/earnings/whop-content-rewards-alternatives).

**Vyro.** For clippers of large US creators: $1–$2 per 1,000<!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-09-02 -->, 5,000 views per post<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-09-02 -->, a cap of 1 million views per clip<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-02 -->. The minus: Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova are unsupported<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-02 -->, and no clipper fee is published<!-- source: https://www.vyro.com/clipper-terms 2026-09-02 -->.

**Vues.** For crypto or PayPal payouts with no audience requirement: the rate shows only in the app, the homepage calculator using $3.00 per 1,000, and the clipper fee is not published as a rate — its homepage puts Vues' cut on the brand's budget and shows clippers a processing fee on withdrawal<!-- source: https://vues.app/ 2026-09-18 -->, while its referral page illustrates a 10% platform fee on each cash-out<!-- source: https://vues.app/affiliate 2026-09-18 -->. The minus: campaign money stays with the brand<!-- source: https://vues.app/terms 2026-09-02 -->; a campaign can set a minimum and a cap per post, with amounts shown only in the app<!-- source: https://vues.app/blog/how-cpm-payouts-work 2026-09-18 -->; country list unpublished.

**Reach.cat.** For USDT to a crypto wallet: $1–$3 per 1,000 promised to new clippers<!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, no view minimum in its terms or FAQs<!-- source: https://reach.cat/creator 2026-09-24 -->, the brand pays 10% on top of the budget<!-- source: https://reach.cat/blog/reach-cat-pricing-brands-2026/ 2026-09-18 -->, it publishes no minimum amount, 149+ countries claimed<!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-02 -->, and approved payouts are typically processed within 3–5 business days<!-- source: https://reach.cat/user-agreement 2026-09-24 -->. The minus: its terms name a commission of approximately 10% on all CPM-based payouts and require an identity check once total payout requests pass $500, while its landing pages say no ID check<!-- source: https://reach.cat/terms 2026-09-24 -->; its own pages disagree on rates.

**Klipni.** For Russian self-employed clippers who want rubles in a bank account: the rate is set per campaign, and the platform's own calculator defaults to 30 ₽ per 1,000 views with presets from 20 to 60 ₽<!-- source: https://klipni.com/calculator 2026-09-21 -->, a 5% fee on each approved payout, no lower limit<!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->, money within five business days once the bank accepts the payout<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. VK Clips count too, and there are apps in the App Store and RuStore<!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->. The minus: Russian self-employed or sole-trader status required, and the view threshold and the cap are set per campaign, with no platform-wide figure published<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. Head to head: [DareBay vs Klipni](/en/earnings/darebay-vs-klipni).

**Clipping.io.** Hard to say who it suits: its public pages show no rates, thresholds, fees, minimums or countries, and payout methods only as logo images<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/ 2026-09-24 -->; its homepage names Snapchat and X alongside TikTok and Instagram. The minus: its public terms are a generic template that says nothing about pay<!-- source: https://www.clipping.io/terms-of-use 2026-09-24 -->.

## Which one to pick

**A beginner with no audience.** You need a threshold per clip and a payout you trigger yourself. On Clipping.net the real gate is 25,000 per campaign and the 100,000 bars on the cards. On DareBay, where a task shows "Claim now", an approved submission can be locked in once its earnings are 24 hours old, and you file the withdrawal request from 10 USDT yourself. Checklist: [nine questions before choosing a platform](/en/earnings/how-to-choose-a-clipping-platform).

**A clipper in Russia, Ukraine or CIS.** Clipping.net names no countries, so ask support in writing before your first clip; Whop is closed to Russia and Belarus, Vyro to Ukraine as well. DareBay keeps no country list and pays clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS, in USDT on TON, or you receive the payout in Telegram Stars. Russian self-employed clippers can use Klipni; Prime Oracles' terms name ten CIS states in a tax clause, Ukraine not among them<!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-24 -->. The rest: [ranking of clipping platforms](/en/earnings/best-clipping-platforms).

**A high-volume clipper.** If you pull hundreds of thousands of views a week, Clipping.net's model works for you: the bars are within reach and pot-style campaigns pay by share of views. Vyro caps a clip at 1 million views<!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-02 -->. On DareBay a task can set a cap of up to $500 per clip, so volume comes from several tasks in parallel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does Clipping.net quote rates per 100,000 views?

That is how its cards and calculator work<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-05 -->. Divide by 100: "up to $40 per 100K" is $0.40 per 1,000. On DareBay, Whop and Vyro the card already shows the rate per 1,000.

### What does "min 100K views to qualify" mean on a Clipping.net campaign?

It is the entry condition for a payout, above the usual 25,000 per campaign<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->: until the bar is met nothing is paid; ask the campaign's Discord how it is counted. Prefer a threshold per clip: set by each task on DareBay, 1,000 views by default, and 5,000 per post on Vyro.

### Which countries does Clipping.net pay to?

It publishes no country list: the terms require legal residence where it operates<!-- source: https://clipping.net/policies/clipper-terms-and-conditions 2026-09-05 -->, and it pays by PayPal or in USDC and USDT on Ethereum<!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-05 -->. DareBay keeps no country list: only people on sanctions lists are barred<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->; Klipni pays Russian self-employed clippers.

### How is DareBay different from Clipping.net for a clipper?

The threshold sits on the clip, not the campaign: each task sets it and prints it on the card, then every view is paid by the formula views / 1,000 × rate, never above any cap the task sets. DareBay's tasks pay $1–$10 per 1,000 views, set by each task, while Clipping.net's own FAQ says its rates are often $0.20–$0.75+, and its campaign pages show up to $3 with bounties<!-- source: https://clipping.net/clip 2026-09-24 -->. On DareBay you file the withdrawal request yourself: the fee is 10% of what you withdraw, and withdrawals start at 10 USDT. DareBay also keeps no country list.
