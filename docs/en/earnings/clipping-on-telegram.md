## Three ways to earn from clips through Telegram

"Telegram clip channel income" is really three different mechanics, and mixing them up costs money. First, your own clip channel, where Telegram pays a share of ad revenue and viewers pay with Stars. Second, DareBay tasks you take in the Telegram version while posting the clips on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or Facebook Reels: views count only on those four sites. Third, clipping services for streamers and brands, sold through a portfolio channel.

| Route | Who pays, and for what | What you need to start | How the money arrives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your own channel | Telegram: 50% of the revenue from ads in the channel <!-- source: https://telegram.org/blog/monetization-for-channels 2026-09-04 -->, Stars for reactions and paid subscriptions | A public channel with at least 1,000 subscribers <!-- source: https://telegram.org/blog/monetization-for-channels 2026-09-04 -->, rights to the footage | Toncoin through Fragment, Stars on the channel balance |
| DareBay tasks | The task buyer: $1–$2 per 1,000 counted views on TikTok, Reels, Shorts or Facebook Reels | An account on the posting site, no followers needed | The DareBay wallet, then USDT on the TON network or Telegram Stars |
| Clipping services | A streamer or brand directly: a fixed fee per clip or per batch | A portfolio channel and a clear price | However you agree, the platform is not part of the deal |

The first route pays for an audience you already have; the second pays for the reach of one clip and works from day one. How tasks differ from the platforms' own creator programmes is in [what TikTok pays for views](/en/earnings/tiktok-view-payouts). The third is pure negotiation, with the channel as a shop window: where to find those buyers is in [where to find clipping work](/en/earnings/where-to-find-clipping-work).

## DareBay tasks in Telegram: how it works

DareBay has two equal front doors and one account: the website and Telegram (the app, the bot, the group). Tasks, submissions and balance are the same on both sides, so the whole cycle runs without leaving the messenger.

1. Open the task catalogue in the Telegram version and pick a card: rate per 1,000 views, threshold, cap per clip and the sites where the clip will count.
2. Download the materials: a stream recording, the brand's footage, or a ready-made video with built-in uniqueization.
3. Edit the clip and post it on your own TikTok, Reels, Shorts or Facebook Reels account. A post in a Telegram channel never enters the task: it has no public counter to check.
4. Send the link to the task; without it the submission does not take part. A moderator checks the terms, and the platform reads views from the site's public counter several times a day.
5. Once the submission is approved, press "Claim now": what has accrued is locked into the wallet, and fresh amounts mature for 24 hours.

Open tasks currently pay from $1 to $2 per 1,000 views, typically $1. The threshold per clip is usually 2,000 views and the cap per submission is usually $100; the formula is views / 1,000 × rate, never above the cap, with the threshold not deducted. A hypothetical clip with 20,000 views at $1.00 makes `20,000 / 1,000 × $1.00 = **$20.00**`. Put your own numbers into the [clipping earnings calculator](/en/earnings/clipping-earnings-calculator).

Money leaves the wallet by two routes. A withdrawal request from 10 USDT to a wallet on the TON network: the fee is 10% of the requested amount, a personal rate may apply, processing is manual. Or "get it in Stars": the amount arrives as Telegram Stars, and that is not a cash withdrawal, because under Telegram's terms Stars on a personal balance cannot be sold or transferred. The request procedure is in [DareBay withdrawals](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals).

## A clip channel: what it takes to make money

A channel earns not by existing but by holding an audience. Four conditions.

- **One topic, not everything.** A channel about one streamer, one game or one podcast grows faster than a mixed bag: the subscriber knows why to come back, the advertiser sees who is looking at the ad.
- **Rights to the footage.** Other people's streams and films without permission mean rights-holder claims that lose channels. Footage from DareBay tasks is safer: the buyer handed over the recording to be clipped.
- **Regularity.** Telegram's ad platform checks that a promoted channel has had at least one post in the two weeks before review <!-- source: https://ads.telegram.org/guidelines 2026-09-04 -->; to a viewer, a month of silence is an abandoned channel.
- **Programme requirements, in words.** For the ad revenue share Telegram names a public channel with a subscriber floor. Star reactions go to the channel owner in full, 100% <!-- source: https://telegram.org/blog/superchannels-star-reactions-subscriptions 2026-09-04 -->, plus paid subscriptions and mini-app affiliate programmes paying a commission on referred users' purchases. Toncoin rewards are collected through Fragment; Telegram sets the minimum amount and timing, so check them in the channel settings.

Count the channel only once it actually shows ads: until then the "revenue share" is zero whatever the quality of the clips.

## Rights and restrictions

- **Other people's content.** A downloaded clip or a repost gets the "Someone else's video" rejection in a task and becomes a rights-holder complaint in your own channel. Cut from the task materials or from recordings you have permission for.
- **Music and footage.** The buyer grants rights to their own recording, not to the track you laid under it; the sites' own libraries are safer than any "hit from the feed".
- **Casino ads and prohibited topics.** Telegram Ads does not accept ads for real-money gambling, alcohol, tobacco, weapons or dubious financial schemes <!-- source: https://ads.telegram.org/guidelines 2026-09-04 -->. On DareBay a clip from the [prohibited content](/en/help/prohibited-content) list is removed and not restored.
- **Fake engagement.** Bought subscribers make a channel useless to advertisers; bought views on a task clip lead to a hold and a rejection, as explained in [protection from view fraud](/en/help/protection-from-view-fraud).

## A weekly routine for a clipper with a channel

The channel and the tasks feed each other: tasks supply legal footage and money for reach, the channel builds the audience Telegram will pay for later.

- **Monday.** In the Telegram version pick two tasks on the channel's topic, download the materials, reread the terms: site, hashtag, date window.
- **Tuesday and Wednesday.** An editing batch: six to eight clips, each with its own moment and its own first second.
- **Thursday to Saturday.** One or two posts a day on TikTok, Reels or Shorts, with the link sent to the task the same day. Post the best clip of the day in the channel with a caption saying where the moment comes from.
- **Sunday.** Check the counters, press "Claim now" on clips that have stopped growing, and see which moments brought the most views and subscribers.

Half of all rejections are avoidable before posting: the reasons are listed in [why clips get rejected](/en/earnings/why-clips-get-rejected).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does DareBay pay for views of a clip in a Telegram channel?

No. Tasks count views only on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, where there is a public counter to check. The Telegram version is for taking tasks, sending links and receiving what you earned, including in Stars.

### How many subscribers does a channel need for Telegram's ad revenue share?

Telegram names public channels with at least 1,000 subscribers <!-- source: https://telegram.org/blog/monetization-for-channels 2026-09-04 -->. That is the entry threshold, not the income: the amount depends on how many ads were shown in your channel, with half of the revenue going to the owner.

### Can I take a task in Telegram and post the clip on TikTok?

That is exactly the design. The task and the wallet live in the Telegram version, the clip goes out on the site named on the card. Keep the account public, send the link to the task, and leave the clip in place until the payout.

### Can Telegram Stars be exchanged for money?

Not from a personal balance: under Telegram's terms those Stars are spent inside the messenger, not sold. If you need money, choose USDT on the TON network in the wizard and file a request from 10 USDT.
