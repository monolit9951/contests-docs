## How X pays for video

X pays creators out of its own revenue: a rewards programme for original content, where the money depends on how much attention your posts collect among paying, verified users. The entry conditions come in three parts: an active Premium subscription, a minimum number of verified followers, and a minimum of impressions from verified users over a rolling window of recent months. The exact thresholds are not printed here because X keeps moving them and renaming the programme: in 2026 the old Creator Revenue Sharing scheme is being wound down and applications go to Original Content Rewards, so check the figures on help.x.com on the day you apply.

What is paid for matters more. X rewards original posts that earned impressions and reactions from a verified audience; replies and reposts do not count. There is no fixed rate per 1,000 views: the sum comes out of a closed formula over impressions and engagement, and you learn it when the payout lands.

## Which clips work on X

X is a text feed: a video is opened not by its thumbnail but by the first line of the post. A clip for X is therefore two parts: the text hook above the video and the first second of the video. The hook is a claim or a contested thesis, not a description ("what the streamer said about..." loses to "the streamer admitted for the first time..."). A quote from the clip in the post text beats a retelling: the reader sees the line and wants to hear it.

Frame format is freer on X than on vertical-video platforms: vertical 9:16 fills a phone screen and is the standard for stream clips, horizontal 16:9 suits gaming and film moments where the whole scene matters. What does not work is a link to TikTok or YouTube instead of a video: the feed gives reach to native video, not to a post with an external link.

Formats that gain steadily on X:

- a reaction or a contested thesis, 30–40 seconds, with the claim as the text above;
- a quote pulled into the post text, with the clip as proof;
- a thread of clips from one stream, hook first;
- a vertical clip with subtitles, since sound is off by default.

## Two income streams from one clip

DareBay's clip tasks count views on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, as each task states: a clip task does not accept a link to an X post, and views on X do not enter its calculation. Combining still works: the same clip is published twice, on TikTok, in Reels or in Shorts for a task that pays a rate per view, and on X for the network's own programme, as long as the task's terms do not demand exclusivity.

A buyer publishes a task, hands over materials and sets a rate per 1,000 views, from $1 to $10. A clip takes part once you have sent the link and a moderator has checked the terms. The view threshold is set by each task - check the card in the catalog - and it is not deducted: clear it and the whole counter is paid. The cap per clip runs up to $500. Formula: views / 1,000 × rate, never above the cap. A hypothetical example: 20,000 / 1,000 × $1.00 = **$20.00**. ## An account for X: where to start

Topic. X rewards the account, not individual clips, so a profile with one theme (one streamer, one game, one genre) gathers verified followers faster than a mixed bag.

Regularity. Impressions are counted over a rolling window, so a month's pause wipes out the progress: several posts a day from one stream, not one a week.

Subscription. There is no way in without Premium, and it is charged monthly while the programme pays nothing, so pay for it once the account is close to the thresholds.

Rules. X keeps separate monetization standards: what it does not pay for and what gets you removed. Read them before the first application.

## Mistakes that zero out income on X

- Reposting someone else's clip without rights. A clip downloaded from TikTok or a film fragment without permission is not original content: X does not reward it, and the rights holder can have it removed. On a DareBay task the buyer supplies the materials, which settles the rights.
- A link in the first post. The feed cuts the reach of a video with an external link in its text; put the source or your TikTok in a reply.
- Buying impressions and reactions. X counts verified engagement and filters out what was bought; DareBay filters inflated views too, and a submission with suspicious growth goes on review: see [protection from view fraud](/en/help/protection-from-view-fraud).
- A link instead of a file. A TikTok link gets no reach on X and does not count as a video.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will DareBay count a clip published on X?

Not in a clip task: it accepts links and counts views on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, as the task states. Publish the task clip there and run the X copy separately for the X programme.

### Is Premium mandatory before a clip on X earns anything?

For the X programme, yes: without an active subscription the application is not accepted. DareBay tasks need neither a subscription nor followers: you are paid for the views of one clip at the rate on the card.

### Can I post on X the same clip that went to TikTok for a task?

Yes, unless the task requires an exclusive publication: if the card allows one platform only, an X post breaks the terms; otherwise the X copy is a second income stream from the same edit.

### What does X treat as original content, and how does that apply to clips?

X rewards posts you created yourself and does not pay for reposts and replies. A clip from someone else's stream counts as your work when you hold the right to the source: the streamer's permission, materials from the buyer, or an open licence.
