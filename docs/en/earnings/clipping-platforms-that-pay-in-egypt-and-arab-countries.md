Two questions decide whether clipping is worth your evening in Cairo, Casablanca, Algiers, Baghdad, Riyadh or Dubai. Can the platform send money to your country at all, and what does your own regulator say about the rail it would use. This page keeps them apart, using the regulators' own publications and the platforms' own pages. It does not rank platforms — that is [the best clipping platforms](/en/earnings/best-clipping-platforms) — and it does not explain rates, which are on [how much clipping pays](/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays).

## Egypt first: what can actually land in an Egyptian account

A platform paying through PayPal cannot reach an Egyptian clipper: PayPal's table of countries eligible to receive a payout lists Morocco, Saudi Arabia and the UAE, and does not list Egypt. <!-- source: https://developer.paypal.com/docs/payouts/standard/reference/country-feature/ 2026-09-18 --> Stripe does not list Egypt either, and limits cross-border payouts to connected accounts in the United States, United Kingdom, EEA, Canada and Switzerland, stating that it "doesn't support self-serve cross-border payouts to countries outside the listed regions". <!-- source: https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts 2026-09-18 --> Vyro states the consequence plainly: it pays only through PayPal or Stripe, <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-will-i-get-paid 2026-09-18 --> and Egypt, Algeria and Iraq are on its unsupported-countries list. <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-18 -->

The part that is routinely misread: a PayPal account in Egypt is not useless. PayPal's fee schedule prices a withdrawal of your own balance to an Egyptian bank at 125.00 EGP per EGP withdrawal. <!-- source: https://www.paypal.com/ae/webapps/mpp/paypal-fees 2026-09-18 --> That is a different operation from a platform sending you a payout, and only the payout decides whether a campaign turns into money. Wise has the same shape: it sends Egyptian pounds into Egypt, but Egypt is absent from its list of countries where a person may hold money. <!-- source: https://wise.com/help/articles/2571907/what-countries-can-i-send-money-from 2026-09-18 --> Payoneer publishes no public country list at all, only "190+ countries and territories". <!-- source: https://www.payoneer.com/withdraw-funds/ 2026-09-18 -->

On tax, Law No. 6 of 2025, in the Official Gazette of 21 February 2025, taxes turnover from 0.4% to 1.5% for enterprises up to twenty million pounds a year, explicitly including professional activity whether or not the person is tax-registered. <!-- source: https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-02/law_no.6.of_.2025.pdf 2026-09-18 --> The general picture is on [taxes for clippers](/en/earnings/taxes-for-clippers).

## InstaPay and Vodafone Cash: a domestic rail has edges

InstaPay runs on the Instant Payment Network, which the Central Bank of Egypt describes as "a national network linking all operating banks within the Arab Republic of Egypt", with limits of 70,000 EGP per transaction and 400,000 EGP per month per bank. Money from abroad enters through a separate service, IPN International Remittance, working "by partnering with local banks, money transfer operators, and exchange houses". <!-- source: https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/payment-systems-and-services/instant-payment-network 2026-09-18 --> An InstaPay address therefore receives a remittance that already crossed the border through a registered institution; it is not an address a campaign platform can select. Vodafone Cash describes depositing and withdrawing through Vodafone stores, dealers, Fawry, Aman and Basata branches and ATMs, and no inbound international route. <!-- source: https://web.vodafone.com.eg/en/vodafone-cash 2026-09-18 --> That is an absence of a statement rather than a refusal, but it leaves nothing published to rely on.

## Where a USDT payout runs into the law

The three countries a fiat rail cannot reach are the three with the strictest rules on the rail that could.

Algeria is the strictest, and the only one where the prohibition is a criminal provision. Loi n° 25-10 of 24 July 2025, in Journal Officiel No. 48, inserted Article 6 bis into the 2005 anti-money-laundering law: issuing, buying, selling, using, holding, trading or promoting virtual assets is prohibited, as payment and as investment. Article 31 bis sets two months to one year in prison and a fine of 200,000 to 1,000,000 dinars. <!-- source: https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2025/F2025048.pdf 2026-09-18 -->

In Egypt the regulator's own words are the source: the Central Bank of Egypt's statement of 8 March 2023 calls this "legally criminalized activities, in accordance with" Law No. 194 of 2020, and adds that "no license has ever been issued or granted" for such trading in the Egyptian market. <!-- source: https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2023/03/08/warning-statement 2026-09-18 -->

In Iraq and Saudi Arabia the published rules are addressed to institutions, not to you. Iraq's central bank barred banks and electronic payment companies from letting cards and e-wallets be used for crypto trading, in circular No. 125/5/9 of 29 March 2022. <!-- source: https://cbi.iq/news/view/2226 2026-09-18 --> Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Finance warns that virtual currencies sit "outside the scope of the regulatory framework" and are not traded by local financial institutions. <!-- source: https://www.mof.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/news/Pages/News_20082019.aspx 2026-09-18 -->

## Morocco, Algeria and the Gulf: three different answers

Morocco works. PayPal lists it among the countries eligible to receive a payout, noting that Morocco is served "through third party partners" and that there "receiving money with PayPal requires linking your account to a licensed local partner". <!-- source: https://www.paypal.com/ma/cshelp/article/help1010 2026-09-18 --> Al Barid Bank tells customers they can receive money from abroad into the account by SWIFT, and CIH Bank advertises inbound Western Union transfers without a bank account. <!-- source: https://www.cihbank.ma/MDM/nos-offres/Transfert-fonds-au-Maroc 2026-09-18 --> On crypto the position is narrower than usually described: the Office des Changes text of 20 November 2017 is a communiqué, not a statute, and treats virtual-currency transactions as a breach of the exchange regulations, <!-- source: https://www.oc.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2018-05/communique%CC%81%20monnaies%20virtuelles%20fr.pdf 2026-09-18 --> while the crypto-asset bill 42.25 was still a draft open for public comment on 18 September 2026. <!-- source: https://www.sgg.gov.ma/portals/0/AvantProjet/274/Avp_Loi_42.25_Ar.PDF 2026-09-18 --> One further rule matters: the exchange instruction requires a resident providing services to a non-resident to repatriate the full proceeds within ninety days. <!-- source: https://www.oc.gov.ma/sites/default/files/reglementation/pdf/2026-01/IGOC%202026_0.pdf 2026-09-18 -->

Algeria does not work in either direction: absent from PayPal's eligible-payout list and from Stripe, not served by Wise, and carrying the criminal provision above.

Both Gulf countries are on PayPal's eligible-payout list with the full "Send, receive, and withdraw" feature set, and the UAE is the only one of the six on Stripe's list. A UAE resident can withdraw a PayPal balance to a local bank free above 750 AED; in Saudi Arabia the priced routes are a card withdrawal at a fixed 19.00 SAR or a third-party provider. <!-- source: https://www.paypal.com/ae/webapps/mpp/paypal-fees 2026-09-18 --> Saudi Arabia is also the one country here whose residents may open a Wise account.

Crypto in the UAE is regulated rather than prohibited, so the question is under which licence. Chairman's Resolution No. 26 of 2023 provides that a virtual asset may not be traded in the country unless it is on the official list of a platform operator licensed by the Authority and registered with it. <!-- source: https://www.uaecma.gov.ae/assets/e2284abc/26-2023-e.aspx 2026-09-18 --> In Dubai a second layer applies: VARA's Virtual Assets and Related Activities Regulations 2023 provide that no entity — "Entity" is defined to include an individual — may carry out a virtual-asset activity by way of business without a VARA licence. <!-- source: https://rulebooks.vara.ae/sites/default/files/en_net_file_store/VARA_EN_18_VER992_2.pdf 2026-09-18 -->

## Iraq is the hard case

Iraq is closed from both ends. PayPal runs no country site for Iraq, Stripe does not list it, Wise offers no accounts there. Whop's trust-and-safety page puts Iraq on its sanctioned-country list, stating that creators in those countries "can't register a Whop account, sell access passes, or receive payouts". <!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-18 -->

The reason is worth stating precisely: the European Union's Iraq regulation is a listed-persons measure — it freezes the funds of named former-regime figures and restricts trade in looted cultural property — not a country-wide embargo on paying Iraqi residents. <!-- source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02003R1210-20251224 2026-09-18 --> Platforms still apply their own lists, and those lists are what their payment partners enforce. Local wallets and banks do bring money into Iraq from abroad, but that is remittance infrastructure rather than a rail a platform can select.

## Paid in Stars: spending power, not cash

Telegram's own terms describe Stars as "virtual items that allow users to purchase digital goods and services from bots and mini apps inside the Telegram ecosystem", and for a personal balance the wording is explicit: "You may not sell, withdraw, transfer or use Stars in your personal balance for any other purpose than those explicitly outlined in these Terms." <!-- source: https://telegram.org/tos/stars 2026-09-18 --> Turning Stars into money belongs to channel and bot owners, is processed by Fragment 21 days after a balance accrues, and Telegram states that "Fragment may be unable to issue rewards for certain users or in certain countries". <!-- source: https://telegram.org/tos/content-creator-rewards 2026-09-18 --> Stars are purchasing power inside Telegram, not a payout that arrives as money.

## Nine platforms, read from their own pages

Only Whop publishes both a sanctions list and a payout-country list: it names Egypt, Morocco, Algeria, Saudi Arabia and the UAE among its payout countries and excludes Iraq. <!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/set-up-payouts 2026-09-18 --> Clipping.net publishes no country list and pays by PayPal or USDC and USDT on Ethereum only. <!-- source: https://clipping.net/docs/clippers/payments 2026-09-18 --> Reach.cat publishes none either and pays to a crypto wallet; the only Arab country it names anywhere is Morocco, in a testimonial. <!-- source: https://reach.cat/become-a-clipper/ 2026-09-18 --> Its app's payout screen also offers a bank transfer by IBAN <!-- source: https://reach.cat/dashboard/creator/earnings 2026-09-18 -->, and its terms effective 13 September 2026 require identity verification through iDenfy once payout requests pass $500. <!-- source: https://reach.cat/terms 2026-09-18 --> On Vues the brand funds each payout, so the method depends on the campaign.

One regional platform belongs here although it is not in the table: Nashr, an Arabic-only pay-per-view marketplace in Saudi Arabia, whose terms state that withdrawal goes to a Saudi bank account or a supported digital wallet, minimum 100 riyals. <!-- source: https://nashrapp.com/terms 2026-09-18 --> Usable in Riyadh, of no help in Cairo, Casablanca or Algiers. No Egyptian, Emirati, Moroccan, Algerian or Iraqi equivalent was found with its own site and published terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will a USDT payout get me in trouble in Egypt?

That is a question for a local lawyer; this page only repeats the regulator. The Central Bank of Egypt's statement of 8 March 2023 describes dealing in cryptocurrencies as legally criminalized under Law No. 194 of 2020 and says no licence has ever been granted for it.

### Can PayPal pay me into an Egyptian bank account?

Two separate answers. A platform cannot send you a PayPal payout: Egypt is absent from PayPal's list of countries eligible to receive payouts. Withdrawing a balance you already hold is a different operation, priced at 125.00 EGP per EGP withdrawal, and is not an answer to the first question.

### Can a platform pay straight into InstaPay or Vodafone Cash?

No. The Central Bank of Egypt describes the Instant Payment Network as linking banks inside Egypt; money from abroad enters through IPN International Remittance, via partner banks, money transfer operators and exchange houses. Vodafone Cash's own page states no inbound international route.

### Do clippers in Morocco and Algeria actually get paid?

Morocco yes; Algeria only on Whop's list. Morocco is on PayPal's payout list through a licensed local partner and on Whop's, and Al Barid Bank receives SWIFT into the account; Algeria is absent from PayPal's payout list, is not served by Wise and is named on Vyro's unsupported-countries page, though Whop names it among its payout countries. <!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/set-up-payouts 2026-09-18 -->

### Why is Iraq shut out by so many platforms?

Because the rails stop there and platforms follow their payment partners: PayPal has no Iraqi country site, Stripe does not list Iraq, and Whop places Iraq on its sanctioned-country list, which blocks registration, selling and payouts. The EU measure is a listed-persons regime rather than an embargo, but a platform's own list is what its provider enforces.

### Can Telegram Stars be turned into money?

Not from a personal balance: Telegram's terms state that you may not sell, withdraw or transfer Stars held there. Converting them belongs to channel and bot owners, runs through Fragment after a 21-day wait, and Telegram warns that Fragment may be unable to issue rewards in certain countries.

### Do I owe tax in Egypt on what I earn online?

There is a published regime that may apply: Law No. 6 of 2025 taxes turnover from 0.4% to 1.5% for enterprises up to twenty million pounds a year, explicitly including professional activity whether or not the person is tax-registered. Whether an individual falls inside it is a question for the tax authority or an accountant.

### Do I need English to take a clipping brief?

For DareBay, you need to read the brief in the language it is written in: the interface is English, Russian and Ukrainian, with no Arabic version, and each task card is written in its organizer's language, most often Russian. The work itself is editing and posting. Nashr is the one platform here with an Arabic-only interface, and it pays only to Saudi accounts.
