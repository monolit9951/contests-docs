## Clipster at a glance: the operator, the move to .com, the apps

Clipster is a campaign board. A brand posts a paid brief, you publish a clip, a music edit or a logo placement on your own TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or X account, submit the link, and earn on the views the post gains while the campaign is active. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/campaigns-and-rules/how-do-campaigns-work 2026-11-14 -->

The Creator Terms name the other side of your contract: "Saikyo LLC d/b/a Clipster", with a notice address in Sheridan, Wyoming. Wyoming law governs, disputes go to individual arbitration before the American Arbitration Association unless you opt out by letter within 30 days, and Clipster's total liability to a creator is capped at $500. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/creator-tou 2026-11-14 -->

The address is new. clipster.gg now redirects to clipster.com, and Clipster's own reviews page calls it "the same platform". <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/reviews 2026-11-14 --> A press release of 9 November 2026 announced the move together with "more than $10 million" paid to creators. That total is the company's own figure, distributed through a paid newswire and datelined Dubai, while the terms name the Wyoming company. <!-- source: https://financewire.com/2026/11/09/clipster-surpasses-10-million-in-creator-payouts/ 2026-11-14 -->

There are two apps. On 14 November 2026 Google Play showed 4.6 stars from 7.85K reviews, 100K+ downloads and an update dated 12 November <!-- source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gg.clipster 2026-11-14 -->; the US App Store showed 4.5 from 107 ratings for an iPhone-only app sold by Saikyo, LLC. <!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clipster-get-paid-for-content/id6746810841 2026-11-14 --> Store ratings show that people use the app; they are not an audit of payouts.

## What a Clipster campaign pays, and what is left after the fee

The public Discover board listed 109 campaigns on 14 November 2026: 65 music, 29 clipping, 13 logo and 2 UGC, and all but one accepted TikTok. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/discover 2026-11-14 --> Each card prints its rate per 1 million views, so divide by 1,000 to compare it with other platforms: the most common card, $1,000 per 1M, is $1 per 1,000 views. The lowest card that day paid $100 per 1M views and the highest $4,000 (one of Clipster's own campaigns, by application only), which is $0.10 and $4 per 1,000. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/discover 2026-11-14 --> The table below carries the view minimums and the caps.

Three rules decide what one post earns.

- **A minimum per post.** A post earns nothing until it reaches the campaign's "Min Views for Earnings" on its own; views on your other posts do not add up. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/when-do-i-get-paid 2026-11-14 -->
- **Two caps.** "Cap per Post" stops one post from earning more; "Cap per Profile" stops you in that campaign. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/when-do-i-get-paid 2026-11-14 -->
- **Only views after you submit.** You have 24 hours from publishing to submit the link, and views gained before you submit do not count. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/submissions-and-views/how-do-i-submit-a-clip-and-what-counts-as-a-valid-post 2026-11-14 -->

Clipster's own example makes the last rule concrete: a post with 22,000 views at submission that later reaches 82,000 has 60,000 eligible views. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/submissions-and-views/how-do-i-submit-a-clip-and-what-counts-as-a-valid-post 2026-11-14 --> On a $1,000-per-1M campaign those 60,000 views are worth $60. That clears the $50 withdrawal floor, the 5% fee takes $3, and $57 reaches you. On a logo campaign at $100 per 1M, the lowest rate on the board that day, the same 60,000 views are worth $6, and the $50 floor takes 500,000 eligible views in all. Nothing is rounded up: the help centre's example is a payout of $398.18 where you expected $400. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/when-do-i-get-paid 2026-11-14 -->

## Clipster withdrawals: a $50 floor, 5% and five routes

You can withdraw once your balance is at least $50. The help centre says this minimum "cannot be waived" and applies to referral bonuses too. Clipster takes 5% of each withdrawal; its own example is a $54 withdrawal that pays out about $51. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 -->

You choose the route each time you withdraw: <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 -->

- **PayPal, a Visa Prepaid Card or an Amazon Gift Card.** A partner Clipster does not name emails you a link to redeem. Clipster recommends a PayPal Business account, because personal accounts in some countries cannot receive payments.
- **USDT on ERC20.** "Usually within 1 hour", to an address starting with 0x; a transfer to a wrong address cannot be reversed.
- **A bank transfer, in two regions only.** Europe by SEPA, paid in EUR, and India by IMPS, paid in INR. The form shows the exchange rate and the exact amount before you confirm.

Clipster "does not provide invoices, receipts or official earnings documents", which matters if you have to declare the income. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 -->

The slow part comes before the withdrawal. Earnings stay pending until the campaign ends, and a campaign ends when its budget is used up or when Clipster or the brand ends it. Most run for up to about a month, the end date is not shown in advance, and you get no notification. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/campaigns-and-rules/how-do-campaigns-work 2026-11-14 --> How long it then takes for the money to reach your balance depends on which Clipster page you read: "usually under 24 hours" on the reviews page <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/reviews 2026-11-14 -->, "24-48 hours" in the homepage FAQ <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/ 2026-11-14 -->, "within 2 days" in the payout article <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/when-do-i-get-paid 2026-11-14 --> and "usually about 1 week" in the how-it-works article. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/getting-started/how-does-clipster-work 2026-11-14 --> Plan around the longest one.

## Who can join Clipster: no country block, an audience rule instead

The help centre says Clipster "does not block creators based on their country" and that payouts depend on which payment methods work where you live. A VPN is allowed, and there is no general follower minimum. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/getting-started/how-do-i-set-up-my-account-and-link-my-socials 2026-11-14 --> You must be 18 or older. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/creator-tou 2026-11-14 -->

The filter sits on the audience. Campaigns target what Clipster calls Tier 1 audiences, such as the US, the UK, Canada and Australia, and "Your own location does not matter. What matters is who your content is for." Content aimed mainly at a South Asian audience is rejected; its examples are cricket, Bollywood, Free Fire, PUBG Mobile and content in Hindi, Urdu, Bengali or Tamil. Content aimed mainly at an African audience "may also be rejected", and captions must be in English. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/campaigns-and-rules/what-rules-does-every-clip-need-to-follow 2026-11-14 --> Music campaigns add a ban on posts in Southeast Asian, Russian or Indian languages. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/campaigns-and-rules/what-do-logo-and-music-campaigns-require 2026-11-14 -->

Individual campaigns go further. On 14 November, 10 of the 109 listed campaigns set requirements for the account's own audience, such as a US share of at least 30% or an Indian share of at most 15%; 28 set a follower minimum of 100–1,000; 8 took applications only. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/discover 2026-11-14 --> So a clipper in India can be paid in rupees, but not for clips aimed mainly at a South Asian audience.

## Four places where Clipster's own pages disagree

The Creator and Brand Terms are the contract; the help centre, the FAQ and the reviews page are the explanation. On 14 November 2026 they did not always say the same thing.

- **An unclaimed balance.** The terms give you 30 days to name a payment account after you earn a fee; miss it and "you waive all rights to such Fees". <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/creator-tou 2026-11-14 --> The help centre says "Your balance does not expire. It is not wiped if you do not withdraw." <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/when-do-i-get-paid 2026-11-14 -->
- **Who decides that a post is paid.** The terms: no fee is owed "unless and until a Brand approves your Creator Content", brands approve "in their sole discretion", and Clipster has no obligation to review or monitor content. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/creator-tou 2026-11-14 --> The reviews page: "Everything that's eligible for earnings is checked by a human moderator." <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/reviews 2026-11-14 -->
- **Whether the brand has paid before you post.** The homepage FAQ says a self-serve campaign goes live "as soon as your balance is funded". <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/ 2026-11-14 --> The Brand Terms make campaign fees due "within thirty (30) days of the first date of the Campaign". <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/brand-tou 2026-11-14 --> Neither text says how a funded balance is kept.
- **An old FAQ still online.** clipster.com/help lists only "USDT (ERC20) and PayPal" and tells you to link an account with a code in your bio. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help 2026-11-14 --> The help centre lists five routes <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 --> and says new accounts can no longer be added that way. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/getting-started/how-do-i-set-up-my-account-and-link-my-socials 2026-11-14 -->

On one point the terms and the help centre agree, and it is in your favour: your claim is against Clipster, not the brand. The terms say "Clipster will pay Fees to you" <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/creator-tou 2026-11-14 -->, and the help centre adds that approved posts are paid in full when a campaign ends before its budget is used. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/when-do-i-get-paid 2026-11-14 -->

Four more conditions to read before the first post:

- **A permanent suspension voids the balance.** In the help centre's words, "your balance is not paid out"; a suspension can be appealed once, and the decision is final. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/reviews-appeals-and-suspensions/why-was-my-account-suspended-and-can-i-appeal 2026-11-14 -->
- **Your post can become the brand's ad.** Linking a social account lets Clipster issue "Boost Codes", so the brand can run your post as a paid ad; a boost fee is credited to you. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/creator-tou 2026-11-14 -->
- **Paid posts stay up for 30 days after payout.** A missing post draws a warning, and after 7 days a strike. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/submissions-and-views/what-happens-if-my-post-is-deleted-removed-or-goes-offline 2026-11-14 -->
- **Copyright is your risk.** Clipster "has no policy against copyrighted clips such as movie scenes", but a post the social network removes or restricts cannot be reviewed. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/campaigns-and-rules/what-rules-does-every-clip-need-to-follow 2026-11-14 -->

## Who Clipster suits: music edits, a Tier 1 audience and cash-outs past $50

- **You make music edits for TikTok.** Music campaigns were 65 of the 109 on the board. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/discover 2026-11-14 -->
- **You want the money somewhere other than a crypto wallet.** PayPal, a prepaid Visa card, an Amazon gift card, SEPA in euros or IMPS in rupees are all on its list.
- **Your USDT wallet is on Ethereum.** Clipster sends USDT on ERC20.
- **You want to read every campaign before you sign up.** A card shows its rate, view minimum, both caps and follower minimum to a visitor without an account. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/discover 2026-11-14 -->
- **Your posts already reach an English-speaking, Tier 1 audience** and your earnings pass $50, so the audience rule and the floor cost you nothing.

It is the weaker choice when you clip for a South Asian, African or Southeast Asian audience or post in Russian, when your earnings are likely to stay under $50, or when you need the money before a campaign closes.

## Clipster or DareBay: the floor, the audience rule and the budget

Both pay per counted view on your own account, and both print the rate, the view threshold and the cap before you post. They differ in four places.

- **The payout floor and the fee.** Clipster: a $50 balance, then 5% of each withdrawal. DareBay: task earnings carry a 0% fee, 10% by default is deducted when you withdraw, and a withdrawal request starts at 10 USDT; the team checks the request before sending. <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/darebay-withdrawals 2026-11-14 -->
- **The rail.** Clipster: PayPal, prepaid card, gift card, USDT on ERC20, or a bank transfer in Europe and India. DareBay: USDT on the TON network, or Telegram Stars. <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/darebay-withdrawals 2026-11-14 -->
- **Who may take part.** Clipster does not block countries but rejects content for several audiences and languages, and some campaigns ask for followers or an application. DareBay keeps no country list, and there is no application and no follower minimum. <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/about/darebay-at-a-glance 2026-11-14 -->
- **The budget.** On Clipster the public pages and the Brand Terms describe the brand's payment differently, as quoted above. On DareBay a wallet-backed task locks its budget on the platform before it starts, and a manual-payout task, where the organizer pays directly, is labelled on the card. <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/about/payout-guarantee 2026-11-14 -->

On DareBay the rate per 1,000 counted views, the view threshold and the cap per clip are set by each task and printed on its card: $1–$10 per 1,000 views depending on the task, up to $500 per clip. <!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/about/darebay-at-a-glance 2026-11-14 -->

A working rule: start from the constraint you cannot change. If it is the payout route, check the two lists above: PayPal, cards and bank transfers are on Clipster's, USDT on the TON network and Telegram Stars are DareBay's. If it is the size of your first payouts, compare the two floors: a $50 balance against a withdrawal request that starts at 10 USDT. If it is your audience or your language, read Clipster's rules above and then the terms on a DareBay task card. Nothing stops you from using both.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Clipster legit, and who is the company behind it?

Clipster names its operator, Saikyo LLC of Wyoming, publishes its fee and minimum, and keeps a public campaign board. That makes it a real service, not a guaranteed payout: its terms pay only for posts the brand approves, and a permanent suspension voids the balance.

### What is the minimum withdrawal on Clipster?

A $50 balance. The help centre says the minimum cannot be waived and also applies to referral bonuses. Pending earnings do not count until the campaign has ended and been paid out. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 -->

### How much does Clipster take from a withdrawal?

5% of each withdrawal, taken from the amount you withdraw: $60 becomes $57. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 --> On the brand side, the homepage FAQ says Clipster charges a 20% fee. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/ 2026-11-14 -->

### How long does a Clipster payout take?

First the campaign has to end, which for most campaigns takes up to about a month. Then the money has to reach your balance, which Clipster's pages put at anything from under 24 hours to about a week. After that, USDT usually arrives within an hour, and PayPal and gift cards as soon as you redeem the email link. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/earnings-and-payouts/how-do-i-withdraw-my-earnings-and-what-are-the-fees 2026-11-14 -->

### Does Clipster work in my country?

Clipster says it does not block creators by country; what differs is the payout route, so check the withdrawal list first. Bank transfers cover only Europe and India. The audience rule matters more than your passport: content for a South Asian audience is rejected wherever you live.

### Is Clipster the same as clipster.gg?

Yes. clipster.gg redirects to clipster.com, and Clipster's own pages call it the same platform. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/reviews 2026-11-14 --> Its Android app still carries the gg.clipster identifier <!-- source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gg.clipster 2026-11-14 -->, and one help article still tells you to sign up at clipster.gg. <!-- source: https://www.clipster.com/help-centre/getting-started/how-do-i-set-up-my-account-and-link-my-socials 2026-11-14 -->
