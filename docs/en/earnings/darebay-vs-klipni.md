## The short answer

If you are in Russia, registered as self-employed or willing to register in the app, and you prefer rubles on a card, Klipni is a straightforward option: a 5% fee on approved payouts, any balance can be cashed out, money within five business days, and VK Clips counted alongside YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. The brand pays the budget in advance, so a campaign is funded before it starts.

If you are outside Russia, do not want a tax status just to clip, or need to be paid in USDT, DareBay covers that: it pays clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and all of CIS, and worldwide, in USDT on TON or Telegram Stars, with no application and no followers. Open contests pay from $1.00 to $2.00 per 1,000 counted views; withdrawals start at 10 USDT with a 10% fee.

## Where Klipni is stronger

- **Rubles to a card** through self-employed status, no crypto and no wallets.
- **A 5% fee** on approved payouts and any positive balance can be cashed out.
- **VK Clips counted** together with the global networks, which matters for a Russian audience.
- **Brands on a subscription** from 4,900 rubles a month with no platform cut from the budget: the money deposited goes to creators in full.

## Where DareBay is stronger

- **Geography:** pays in Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and all of CIS, not only Russia, and worldwide.
- **No tax status:** any account can take a brief; self-employed or sole-trader status is not needed.
- **Payout in USDT on TON or Telegram Stars,** available in every country of the region.
- **Terms on the card:** rate, threshold and cap are fixed before you submit; in wallet-backed contests the budget is locked on the platform, and manual-payout contests are labelled.

## What reaches your hands

On Klipni 5% is deducted from an approved payout, there is no minimum, and the money reaches a card within five business days after the bank accepts the payout. On DareBay nothing is deducted from a contest payout, the amount is credited to your balance after the count, and 10% is charged when you withdraw the balance, with a 10 USDT minimum request. Klipni does not publish a view threshold; DareBay's is typically 2,000 views per clip; both set the cap per clip in the campaign.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which pays more, DareBay or Klipni?

Both print the rate on the campaign card, and it differs per campaign. Klipni shows examples of 50 rubles per 1,000 views; DareBay's open contests run from $1.00 to $2.00 per 1,000. Compare the specific campaign, the fee and the threshold, not an average rate.

### Do I need self-employed status?

On Klipni yes: payouts go through Russian self-employed or sole-trader status registered in the app. On DareBay no: no tax status is needed to take a brief and be paid.

### Does Klipni pay in Belarus, Ukraine or Kazakhstan?

Klipni publishes no country list but requires Russian tax status for payouts, so in practice it pays only in Russia. DareBay pays in all of these countries.

### Which platform takes the budget in advance?

Both. Klipni takes prepayment by invoice, and the budget stays the advertiser's money until spent. DareBay locks the organizer's budget in wallet-backed contests before the start; in a manual-payout contest the organizer pays, and the card says so.

### Can I work on both?

Yes. Campaigns end on every platform, and briefs from two sources keep you working. Check a campaign's exclusivity rules before posting the same clip twice.
