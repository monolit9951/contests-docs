## The short answer

DareBay suits you if you clip from Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan or elsewhere in CIS, do not want a tax status just to clip, or want to be paid in USDT: it keeps no country list and asks for no status. Klipni suits you if you live in Russia, are registered as self-employed or as a sole trader, and want rubles in a bank account: it pays only authors with self-employed or sole-trader status<!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->.

DareBay deducts nothing from a task payout and takes 10% of what you withdraw; withdrawals start at 10 USDT. Klipni deducts 5% from an approved payout and sets no floor on the amount<!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay counts TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels as each task states; Klipni counts VK Clips, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Threads and Likee<!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->.

## Rates: what each platform publishes

Neither Klipni nor DareBay has a single rate: a campaign or a task sets it, and both print it on the card before you start editing. Klipni's earnings calculator defaults to 30 rubles per 1,000 views with presets from 20 to 60 rubles, and the platform calls its default rates the starting rates of brand campaigns <!-- source: https://klipni.com/calculator 2026-09-21 -->; DareBay's tasks pay from $1.00 to $10.00 per 1,000 counted views, set by each task. Klipni's other pages give different guide figures: its article on rates says "примерно 40–60 ₽", roughly 40 to 60 rubles <!-- source: https://klipni.com/blog/skolko-platyat-za-narezki 2026-09-21 -->, and its RuStore listing says "обычно от 50 до 300 ₽ за 1000", usually 50 to 300 rubles per 1,000 <!-- source: https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.klipni.app 2026-09-21 -->. The figures disagree, so read the campaign card. We do not convert rubles to dollars.

Klipni's starting rate depends on the format: 30 rubles for clipping, 200 for a video you shoot to a brand's brief, 350 for an integration, all per 1,000 views, and a brand may go higher or lower <!-- source: https://klipni.com/calculator 2026-09-21 -->; on DareBay the task owner sets the rate per 1,000 views inside the $1.00–$10.00 band. In the three case studies on Klipni's site a thousand views cost the brand 8.5, 8.7 and 254 rubles as of 21 September 2026 <!-- source: https://klipni.com/cases 2026-09-21 -->, so Klipni has no average rate, and neither does DareBay: read the card.

The figure of 50 rubles per 1,000 views sits on Klipni's home page inside a mock-up of a brand's dashboard <!-- source: https://klipni.com/ 2026-09-21 -->. It is an interface example, not a tariff. Both platforms print the real rate on the card.

## Where Klipni is stronger

- **Rubles to a bank account** through self-employed status: Sber, Alfa, T-Bank, VTB or any other bank you name, with the self-employed receipt issued for you <!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay has no ruble payout; the money leaves as USDT on TON.
- **A 5% fee** on approved payouts and no floor on the amount <!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->, while DareBay takes 10% of what you withdraw, and withdrawals start at 10 USDT.
- **VK Clips, Threads and Likee** count alongside the global networks <!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->, which matters for a Russian audience; DareBay's clip tasks count TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, as each task states.
- **Apps in the App Store and RuStore:** campaigns, submissions and payout requests from a phone <!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay opens on the web and inside Telegram.
- **A community in plain sight:** on 21 September 2026 Klipni's Telegram channel had 1.2K subscribers <!-- source: https://t.me/s/klipni_official 2026-09-21 -->, its creators' chat had 1,394 members <!-- source: https://t.me/klipni_squad 2026-09-21 -->, and the platform's own counter showed more than 11,700 creators <!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay posts its news in its own Telegram channel; most of its clippers are in CIS.
- **Brands on a subscription** from 4,900 rubles a month with no platform cut from the budget: the money deposited goes to creators in full <!-- source: https://klipni.com/pricing 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay also charges 0% to create or top up a contest.

## Where DareBay is stronger

- **No country list:** it pays clippers not only in Russia but in Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS; only people on sanctions lists are barred.
- **More than a per-view rate:** an offer task pays per view and can add a fixed fee per accepted clip and a share of sales, all on one card.
- **Ready-made unique videos** on offer tasks, no editing software needed.
- **No tax status:** any account can take a brief; self-employed or sole-trader status is not needed.
- **Payout in USDT on TON to your own wallet,** or you receive the payout in Telegram Stars: no Russian bank account is needed.
- **Terms on the card:** rate, threshold and any cap are printed before you submit, the rate and the threshold do not change after launch and the cap per clip can only be raised; in wallet-backed contests the budget is locked on the platform, and manual-payout contests are labelled.

## What reaches your hands

On Klipni 5% is deducted from an approved payout, there is no floor, the accrual then waits out a hold period whose length the campaign sets, and the transfer to your bank details takes up to five business days once the bank accepts it <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->. On DareBay nothing is deducted from a contest payout, the amount is credited to your balance after the count, and the only fee is 10% of what you withdraw to your wallet, deducted inside the request; withdrawals start at 10 USDT, a 100 USDT request pays out 90 USDT, and the form shows the amount to receive before you confirm. A person checks each request, so no settlement time is promised.

Klipni sets the view threshold and the cap per clip in each campaign and publishes no platform-wide figure for either <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->; on DareBay the view threshold is set by each task - check the card in the catalog - and the cap per clip runs up to $500.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which pays more, DareBay or Klipni?

Both print the rate on the campaign card, and it differs per campaign. Klipni's own guide figures disagree: its calculator defaults to 30 rubles per 1,000 views<!-- source: https://klipni.com/calculator 2026-09-21 -->, its article on rates says roughly 40 to 60<!-- source: https://klipni.com/blog/skolko-platyat-za-narezki 2026-09-21 -->, and its RuStore listing says usually 50 to 300<!-- source: https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.klipni.app 2026-09-21 -->; DareBay's tasks pay $1.00 to $10.00 per 1,000, set by each task. The currencies differ and we do not convert them: compare the specific campaign, the fee and the threshold, not an average rate.

### Do I need self-employed status?

On Klipni yes: it pays self-employed authors and sole traders on Russia's simplified or patent tax regimes <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/terms 2026-09-21 -->, and its creators page says of self-employed status, in Russian, "Без неё не выплачиваем": without it we do not pay out <!-- source: https://klipni.com/creators 2026-09-21 -->. On DareBay no: no tax status is needed to take a brief and be paid.

### Does Klipni pay in Belarus, Ukraine or Kazakhstan?

Klipni does not say so directly: it publishes no country list. Its About page names Russia as its region <!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->, and a payout requires self-employed or sole-trader status with a Russian tax number <!-- source: https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.klipni.app 2026-09-21 -->. Without that status you cannot cash out. DareBay pays in all of these countries.

### Which platform takes the budget in advance?

Both, and neither runs an escrow account. Klipni takes prepayment from the brand by invoice, shows the campaign to authors once the payment arrives and refunds what is unspent; its advertiser terms call the balance in the brand's dashboard an accounting figure <!-- source: https://klipni.com/legal/advertiser 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay locks the organizer's budget in a wallet-backed contest before the start and keeps it on the platform as an intermediary and guarantor; in a manual-payout contest the organizer pays, and the card says so.

### Does Klipni have social channels and an app?

Yes. Its About page lists six official channels: Instagram, Threads, a Telegram channel, a creators' chat on Telegram, and apps in the App Store and RuStore <!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay runs a Telegram channel, and the product itself opens on the web and inside Telegram.

### Who runs each platform?

Klipni is operated by a sole trader registered in Russia and says it has been running since 2025; the registration details are printed on its About page, and we do not repeat them <!-- source: https://klipni.com/about 2026-09-21 -->. DareBay was founded by Ruslan Bei, who is named on its legal pages.

### Can I work on both?

Yes. Campaigns end on every platform, and briefs from two sources keep you working. Check a campaign's exclusivity rules before posting the same clip twice.
