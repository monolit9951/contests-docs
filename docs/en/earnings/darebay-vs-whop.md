## The short answer

If you are in Russia or Belarus, Whop is not available to you at all: its sanctioned-country list blocks registration and payouts, and of Ukraine it names six regions (Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson)<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/sanctioned-countries 2026-09-24 -->. The rest of Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the other CIS countries are on Whop's payout list, so there the choice comes down to the fields below. DareBay, which publishes this page, has no country list: only people on sanctions lists are barred, so clippers in Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS are paid the same way as everyone else, and its community is largest there. DareBay's rates are $1–$10 per 1,000 views, set by each task<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->, the threshold and any cap are printed on the task card, no followers or application are needed, and in wallet-backed contests the organizer's budget is locked on the platform before the start. Nothing is taken from contest payouts; the only fee is 10% of what you withdraw, withdrawals start at 10 USDT, and you can withdraw USDT on TON or receive the payout in Telegram Stars<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/what-commission 2026-09-24 -->.

If you are in the US, the EU or most of the world and want more clipping campaigns to choose from, Whop Content Rewards is the bigger platform, with payouts in more than 200 countries<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods 2026-09-24 -->. The first 50 campaigns on its Discover board on 24 September 2026 listed $0.05–$3 per 1,000 views for their best-paying network, 21 of them at $1<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-24 -->. Its terms take 10% from CPM payouts, plus Whop's own withdrawal fee<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->, and a clip earns for 7 days after approval with a 3-day hold after that<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->.

## Where Whop Content Rewards is stronger

- **Supply.** Whop is the bigger clipping marketplace: its Discover board lists dozens of live campaigns with their rates<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-24 -->; DareBay is young, with fewer campaigns so far.
- **Payout rails.** Whop pays to bank accounts, mobile wallets and crypto in 200+ countries<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods 2026-09-24 -->, with no minimum to your Whop balance per its pricing page, a $1 minimum per its help centre<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/pricing/creators 2026-09-24 -->, and a $10 minimum "if applicable" in Whop's own payout guide<!-- source: https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/set-up-payouts 2026-09-24 -->; DareBay pays out in USDT on TON, or you can receive the payout in Telegram Stars; requests start at 10 USDT<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/darebay-withdrawals 2026-09-24 --> and are handled by the team.
- **Per-clip ceiling.** On Whop the cap is set per campaign and per network: its help centre calls $200 the usual cap, and campaign pages checked on 24 September 2026 ran from $80 to $1,500 per clip<!-- source: https://help.contentrewards.com/en/articles/15319800-which-pricing-model-should-i-use 2026-09-24 -->; a DareBay contest can set a cap on one submission, up to $500 per clip<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->.

## Where DareBay is stronger

- **No country list.** Our platform keeps no exclusion list, so it pays clippers in Russia and Belarus, which Whop's sanctioned list excludes, and in Ukraine; only people on sanctions lists are barred.
- **More than a per-view rate.** An offer task pays the rate per view and can add a fixed fee per accepted clip and a share of the orders your promo code brings, all on one card.
- **Ready-made unique videos.** On offer tasks the platform hands you finished videos and makes every copy unique for you; no editing software needed.
- **Fee on the payout itself.** DareBay takes 0% of contest payouts<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/what-commission 2026-09-24 -->; Whop's terms take 10% of CPM payouts<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->.
- **Entry.** No follower requirement and no application before your first brief on DareBay, and identity verification applies where the law requires it; Whop requires identity verification before payouts and some campaigns require an application.
- **Terms on the card.** Rate, threshold and any cap are on every DareBay task card before you submit; the rate and the threshold do not change after launch, and the cap per clip can only be raised.

## Fees and what actually reaches you

Take one clip with 50,000 counted views. On a Whop campaign at $2 per 1,000 views the clip earns $100 gross, the terms deduct 10% at payout, and Whop's own withdrawal fee comes off when you cash out<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->. On a DareBay contest at $2 per 1,000 views the same clip earns $100, the contest fee is 0%, so the full $100 lands on your balance; a withdrawal to your wallet then takes 10% of what you withdraw, withdrawals start at 10 USDT, and a $100 request pays out $90. On both platforms the payout for one clip stops at the ceiling the campaign or contest printed, if it set one.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which pays more per 1,000 views, DareBay or Whop?

On advertised rates, DareBay: its tasks pay $1–$10 per 1,000 views, set by each task<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->. It takes 0% from contest payouts, and the only fee is 10% of what you withdraw; withdrawals start at 10 USDT<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/help/what-commission 2026-09-24 -->. The first 50 campaigns on Whop's Discover board on 24 September 2026 listed $0.05–$3 per 1,000 views for their best-paying network, most often $1<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/discover 2026-09-24 -->, and Whop's terms take 10% of CPM payouts, plus Whop's own withdrawal fee<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->. The net difference depends on the campaign you can actually take, and on whether Whop can pay you at all.

### Does Whop pay clippers in Russia or Ukraine?

Not in Russia or Belarus: Whop's sanctioned-country list blocks registration and payouts there. In Ukraine it does, except the six regions the same list names: Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson. DareBay keeps no country list, so it pays clippers in Russia, Belarus and Ukraine; only people on sanctions lists are barred.

### Do I need followers on either platform?

No. DareBay, our platform, asks for no followers and no application, so a brand-new account can take a task<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->. Whop Content Rewards publishes no follower minimum, but some of its campaigns require an application first<!-- source: https://contentrewards.com/terms 2026-09-24 -->.

### Which platform holds the budget before I clip?

Both do in their main mode: DareBay locks the organizer's budget in wallet-backed contests, and Whop holds the brand's budget against the campaign. DareBay also runs manual-payout contests where the organizer pays directly; the card says which kind a contest is.

### Can I be on both?

Yes, if Whop can register you. Campaign budgets run out on every platform, so most clippers keep briefs from two or three sources.
