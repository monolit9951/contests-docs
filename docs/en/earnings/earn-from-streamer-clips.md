# How to earn from streamer clips on DareBay

The loop is simple: take a contest, cut a clip out of the stream, upload it with the hashtag, send in the link and get paid for the views. Rates across live contests run from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views** (typically **$0.50**). A clip that reaches 100,000 counted views at the median rate pays $50, exactly the cap per submission in a typical contest (**$50**). The budget is locked on the platform before the start, so the money arrives. No followers are needed, and the entry threshold is **2000 views** per submission. A phone and basic editing skills are all it takes to start today.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## Quick maths: what you can get

The formula: `rate * views / 1000`, capped at the per-submission limit.

Your clip reaches 60,000 counted views at a rate of **$0.50 per 1000**:

`$0.50 * 60,000 / 1000 = $30`

At 120,000 views the formula gives $60, but the cap holds the payout at **$50**.

Several clips across several contests means several payouts. The cap applies to each submission separately and the number of contests is not limited: three clips at the cap is $150. That is the arithmetic of the formula on specific inputs.

## Contest parameters

| Parameter | Value | What it means for you |
|-----------|-------|----------------------|
| Rate per 1000 views | $0.30 - $1.00 | The range across live contests. The median is $0.50 |
| View threshold | 2000 | The minimum for a submission to join the payout |
| Cap per submission | $50 | The most one submission can earn in a typical contest |

The rate, threshold and cap are set by the buyer when creating the contest and cannot change after launch. All three are on the card before you submit.

## Step by step

1. **Find a streamer contest.** Open the contest catalogue on DareBay and filter for PPV contests. Every card states the rate, the threshold and the cap. Take a contest with a good rate and a streamer you know.

2. **Read the terms and get the source.** Go through the contest description: what material you may use, which hashtag is required, how long the contest runs. Find the source stream or the highlighted fragments.

3. **Cut the clip.** Pull out a strong moment and give it pace. The hook in the first second decides whether you clear the 2000-view threshold.

4. **Upload it with the contest hashtag, then send the link.** Publish the clip on TikTok, YouTube Shorts or whichever platform the terms name, with the hashtag from the contest card. Then open the contest on DareBay and paste in the link to the published video: only then does the submission join the budget split, because the hashtag alone will not get it picked up.

5. **The oracle counts the views.** The platform pulls view data through the platform's API. Neither you nor the buyer types figures in by hand, and they cannot be nudged. More on the counting mechanics in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

6. **Get paid.** The contest ends, the platform computes the amount by formula, and the money reaches your wallet, card or another chosen method. The budget was locked in advance: the platform acts as intermediary and guarantor so that neither side gets scammed.

## Why there is a cap per submission

The **$50** cap per submission protects the way the budget is split. Without it a single viral clip would take the whole contest budget and leave every other clipper with nothing. With it, the budget is shared between several authors. The cap applies to each submission separately: if you entered several submissions and the contest rules allow it, each can earn up to the cap.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an audience or followers?

No. DareBay has no minimum follower requirement. The only condition is clearing the view threshold (2000) on a specific submission. Beginners compete on the same terms as veterans.

### What is the cap on one submission?

In typical contests the cap per submission is $50. Even if your clip reaches a million views, the payout for that one submission stops at the cap. The number of contests you enter, on the other hand, is not limited.

### How do I get the money?

After the contest ends the payout reaches you. There are five methods: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts. You choose yours in your profile settings.

### What if somebody inflates their views?

Views are pulled through the platforms' APIs (TikTok, YouTube), not typed in by hand. The platforms filter inflated views on their own side. It is an independent count that neither the buyer nor the clipper can adjust.

### When does the payout arrive?

After the contest ends there is a selection and results-confirmation window, a few days in all. Then the platform computes the amount and sends the money. The budget was locked in advance, so there is nothing for the payout to wait on.

### Can I submit to several contests at once?

Yes. There is no limit on how many contests you take part in. The cap applies to each submission in each contest separately. This is the main way to scale what you earn.

### Can the rate be cut after the contest starts?

No. The rate, threshold and cap are fixed when the contest is created. The buyer cannot change the terms after launch. The budget is frozen on the platform.
