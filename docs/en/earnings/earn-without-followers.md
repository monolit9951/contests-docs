# Earning from short videos with no followers

DareBay has no follower requirement. You are paid for the real views your submission gets, not for the size of your audience. The rate in PPV contests runs from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views**. The one condition: a submission has to reach at least **1000 views** (the default threshold; the exact figure is on the contest card) to join the budget split. This is not "no threshold at all" — there is one, but it is about the views on that specific video, not about followers on your channel.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## Why followers are not required

Most platform partner programmes — the TikTok Creator Fund, YouTube Shorts monetization and others — require a follower count before you can join. Until you clear it there is no access to earnings at all, even if your videos are getting views.

DareBay works differently. A buyer (a brand or an individual) creates a contest and locks the budget on the platform. Participants film short videos to the brief. When the contest ends, the platform counts views through the platforms' APIs and splits the budget in proportion to those views. Followers do not appear in that formula anywhere.

The platform acts as intermediary and guarantor: the budget is locked before the start and the view count is independent, so neither side gets burned.

## What you need, and what you do not

| What you need | What you do NOT need |
|---------------|----------------------|
| A DareBay account | A minimum follower count |
| An account on the contest's platform (currently TikTok) | Channel verification or a partner programme |
| To film a video to the brief | A portfolio or prior experience |
| To reach at least 1000 views on the submission | A referral or an invite |
| To publish with the required hashtag | Paid access or an entry fee |

## Payout parameters

| Parameter | Value | What it means |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Minimum rate | $0.30 per 1000 views | The low end across live contests |
| Maximum rate | $1.00 per 1000 views | The high end across live contests |
| View threshold | 1000 views | The default; the buyer can set their own, shown on the contest card |

The buyer sets the rate and the rest of the terms when creating the contest. Every parameter is visible on the card before you submit.

## What the process looks like

1. Find a contest whose subject suits you in the [DareBay catalogue](https://darebay.com)
2. Read the terms on the card: rate, threshold, budget, deadlines
3. Film the video to the brief and publish it with the required hashtag
4. Submit it to the contest
5. When the contest ends the platform counts the views and credits the payout

More on how counting and splitting work in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

## Frequently asked questions

### Really, with no followers?

Yes. There is no follower minimum. An account with zero followers competes on the same terms as one with a million. What you are paid depends only on the views of that submission.

### Is the view threshold hard to reach?

The default is **1000 views** on a single submission. For short video on TikTok that is reachable even for a new account: the platform's algorithm surfaces clips in recommendations regardless of follower count. The threshold exists to filter out submissions with a handful of views and give the budget to those that actually got reach.

### What kind of videos qualify?

Short videos made to the contest brief. The subject depends on the buyer: streamer clips, reviews, challenges, creative work to a brief. Each contest describes exactly what to film.

### How much can one submission earn?

It depends on the contest rate and the views. At 20,000 counted views with a typical $0.50 per 1000: 20,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = $10. A payout per submission is usually capped, typically at $50 in live contests. The cap applies per submission, not per creator: you can enter several different videos.

### How are views counted?

Automatically, through the platform's API (currently TikTok). It is an independent count: neither the buyer nor the participant types a figure in by hand.

### How is the money paid out?

Several ways: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts. The wallet is one option among them, not the only one.

### Is there a fee to take part?

No. Entering contests is free. The commission is paid by the buyer when creating the contest, never by the participant.
