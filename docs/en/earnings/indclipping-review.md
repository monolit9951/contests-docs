## What INDclipping is, and the operator its terms leave unnamed

INDclipping calls itself "India's First Clipping Marketplace". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/ 2026-11-14 --> Its terms describe a two-sided marketplace: brands publish performance-based clipping campaigns, and independent clippers post short clips on their own social accounts and are paid according to the verified views those clips earn. The same clause calls INDclipping an intermediary under India's Information Technology Act that settles payment through the marketplace. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->

The party you contract with is harder to find. The terms are "a binding agreement between you and the operator of INDclipping", and no page says who that is: no company name, registration number, GSTIN, city or address appears anywhere, and the footer reads "© 2026 INDclipping". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> The public contact page lists email addresses only: a support address for clippers and a brands address for advertisers, each marked "Usually within a few hours". A grievance address for escalations appears in the terms and the refund policy. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/contact 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/refund-policy 2026-11-14 -->

Three dates give the context. The .com registry shows the domain was registered on 22 August 2026. <!-- source: https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/indclipping.com 2026-11-14 --> The terms, privacy, refund and cookie pages all read "Last updated: 26 August 2026". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> On 14 November 2026 the home page counted "2500+ Registered Clippers". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/ 2026-11-14 --> The site links to no app-store listing: the website is the product. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/ 2026-11-14 -->

## ₹30–₹100 per 1,000 views: the rate card and what reaches your wallet

The pricing page states the band in one line: "You earn ₹30–₹100 / 1,000 views", where the rate "is the CPM set by the brand, shown on every campaign before you join". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> In dollars that is roughly $0.32–$1.06, our estimate at ₹94.24 per dollar, the cross rate of the European Central Bank's euro reference rates of 13 November 2026. <!-- source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-hist-90d.xml 2026-11-14 --> Nothing is taken from it: there is "no cut taken out of what you earn", and the brand pays an 8% commission on its spend. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->

Two gates sit between a view and a withdrawal. The first is the one the terms say clippers "most often misread": "Earnings on a campaign are displayed as they accrue, and are released to your wallet once your approved views on that campaign reach 5,000. Below that threshold views are tracked and earnings are shown, but no money moves." <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> The count is per campaign, not per account. The second gate is the ₹500 minimum. With INDclipping's own example rates:

| What you did | Shown as earned | Released to the wallet | Can you withdraw? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000 approved views on one ₹40 campaign, the first row of its pricing table <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> | ₹400 | ₹400 | Not yet: under ₹500 |
| 4,000 approved views on each of two ₹60 campaigns | ₹480 | ₹0: neither campaign has reached 5,000 | No |
| 50,000 approved views on one ₹60 campaign, the example in its terms <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> | ₹3,000 | ₹3,000 | Yes |

The middle row is our arithmetic on the published rule: 8,000 views in total and nothing released. At ₹100 the two gates meet, since 5,000 views earn exactly ₹500; at ₹30 the minimum needs about 16,700 views. Spreading a small account across many campaigns is the slowest way to be paid here.

There is a ceiling as well. "A campaign pays only up to its funded budget", and INDclipping "may cap, discount or refuse views on a submission where the pattern of growth is inconsistent with organic distribution". No cap per clip is published. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> The home page's "Clippers are earning ₹5,000–₹80,000/month" belongs next to clause 10 of the terms: such figures "are illustrations, not promises". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/ 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->

The band above is the platform's statement, not a card we read. The server sends that page uncached, so this was the board's state that evening, not a stale copy. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/campaigns 2026-11-14 --> No campaign rate, budget, brand or deadline could therefore be checked.

The blog tells a different story. ## UPI or NEFT/IMPS: the ₹500 floor, 24–48 hours and the tax line

The withdrawal clause is short: "Minimum withdrawal is ₹500. Requests are paid by UPI or bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) and are processed within 24–48 hours of approval." <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> ₹500 is about $5.31 at the rate quoted above. The clock starts at approval, not at your request, and no page says how long approval takes. No payout day or cycle is named: you ask for each withdrawal. A failed transfer "is credited straight back to your wallet", while money sent to details you typed wrongly is not recovered. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/refund-policy 2026-11-14 -->

The payer is INDclipping, not the brand. Brands fund a campaign through Razorpay before it goes live, the refund policy speaks of "the money we are actually holding for that campaign", and withdrawals go to the UPI ID or the bank account you give the platform. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/refund-policy 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/privacy 2026-11-14 --> One sentence of that policy deserves a second reading: a campaign may have been "launched against a budget larger than the amount received". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/refund-policy 2026-11-14 --> The headline budget on a card is not proof of the money behind it.

Tax takes one line: "Where Indian law requires tax to be deducted at source on a payment to you, we will deduct it and account for it. You are responsible for your own income tax and for any GST registration or filing that applies to you." <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> The terms name no section, threshold or rate. The privacy policy's list of what INDclipping collects from a clipper has no PAN on it, and the words KYC, PAN and Aadhaar appear on no page. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/privacy 2026-11-14 --> Before your first withdrawal, ask support in writing how a deduction would be credited to you.

A withdrawal can also wait: INDclipping "may withhold a withdrawal while a submission connected to it is under investigation for artificial views, plagiarism or a brand complaint". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->

## Who can join INDclipping: 18+, three networks, an Indian payout account

Sign-up takes a name, an email address, a verification code and a password, or a Google sign-in, plus a choice between the clipper and the brand role. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/signup 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/privacy 2026-11-14 --> You must be 18 or older, with one account per person. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> The pricing page sets no follower minimum, while one blog post narrows that to no minimum "for most campaigns". <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/blog/how-clipping-helps-creators-grow-india 2026-11-14 -->

Clips count on three networks: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 --> TikTok and X are not on that list. Each account is proved with a one-time code placed in the profile bio. YouTube accounts are confirmed through YouTube's API, while Instagram and Facebook accounts "are reviewed by a person", usually within one working day. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/terms 2026-11-14 -->

The terms set no residency rule, but everything around them is Indian: the site is "Built for the Indian market with UPI payments, INR pricing, and local support", its file for AI assistants says "Country: India. Currency: INR", and the only payout details it collects are a UPI ID or an Indian bank account. <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/about 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/llms.txt 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.indclipping.com/privacy 2026-11-14 --> Whether an account opened abroad is accepted is not published; without an Indian payout account there is nowhere for the money to go.

## Five conditions under which INDclipping fits

INDclipping fits best when all of these are true:

- You hold a UPI ID or an Indian bank account and want rupees, with no token or exchange step between the platform and your bank.
- You post on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or Facebook Reels.
- You want per-view pay on your own account. ClipGrow's current offer is a fixed amount for every approved clip, posted on ClipGrow's pages instead of yours. <!-- source: https://clipgrow.in/for-clippers 2026-11-14 -->
- You want nothing taken from what a clip earns. ClipConnect India's terms deduct 10% from a clipper's earnings at withdrawal <!-- source: https://clipconnect.in/legal?tab=terms 2026-11-14 -->; INDclipping charges the brand instead.
- A campaign you can see is live, and its rate lets you clear 5,000 approved views and ₹500 on that one campaign.

Even then, start small: take one campaign, screenshot its card and your dashboard at each stage, and request a first ₹500 withdrawal before you commit a month of posting. Your first bank credit is the only check of the operator you can run.
