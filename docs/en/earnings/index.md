# Earning on DareBay

How much creators earn on DareBay and how: pay per view, contest prizes, worked examples for
clippers. Every figure comes from the platform's own data.

The platform holds the prize up front and pays it out automatically against independently
counted results, so neither side has to take the other's word for it. The mechanics are in
[how it works](/en/how-it-works).
