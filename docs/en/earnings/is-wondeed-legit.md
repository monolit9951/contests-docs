## What changed at Wondeed between 12 October and 14 November 2026

Until our read of 12 October 2026, Wondeed was an Indian board. Its terms page, at /terms, admitted only Indian residents aged 18 or over and described UPI payment within seven business days of a request. Its creator page, at /clippers, set a 72-hour hold and a ₹500 balance before a payout request. Those sentences rest on our own dated reads: neither address opens any more.

On 14 November 2026 the same domain serves a different product. The home page is headed "Pay-per-view clipping marketplace", prices every campaign "per 100K views" in dollars and promises "monthly payouts via PayPal or crypto". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-11-14 --> The old /terms, /clippers, /refund, /gst, /help and /press addresses return a 404 page. The terms now sit at /policies/terms-of-service under the line "Last updated: October 2026 · Beta terms". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/policies/terms-of-service 2026-11-14 --> No page carries a rupee sign, and India is mentioned only on the Grievance Officer page and in llms.txt.

| | Our reads of September and October 2026 | Wondeed's pages on 14 November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Who can join | Indian residents, 18+ (terms, 12 October) | 13+, "or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction"; no country is named <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/policies/terms-of-service 2026-11-14 --> |
| Currency and unit | rupees (creator page, 12 October) | dollars, per 100K views <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-11-14 --> |
| Taken from the clipper | 0%, with brands paying a monthly subscription (pricing page, 24 September) | a platform fee "set per campaign, standard rate 20%" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clip 2026-11-14 --> |

| Tax | TDS under Section 194-O deducted before payout (GST page, 18 September) | no deduction is mentioned on any page |

The switch cannot be dated more precisely than that: the new terms carry only the month.

## Who runs Wondeed now: no company, no court, a Gmail address for grievances

The terms describe Wondeed as "a marketplace that connects brands with clippers (content creators)" and add: "The service is in beta: features, rates, and workflows may change". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/policies/terms-of-service 2026-11-14 --> They name no legal entity, registration number, address, governing law or court. The footer reads "© 2026 Wondeed". The earlier terms at least said "operated by Wondeed" and pointed to the courts of "[Registered City], India"; the new ones say less.

Contact is one address, support@wondeed.com, plus a web form. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/contact 2026-11-14 --> You sign in with a Google or Microsoft account. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/login 2026-11-14 -->

The one law the site names is Indian. Its Grievance Officer page is published "As required under the IT Act 2000 and the DPDP Act 2023" and says complaints about illegal content are "handled in line with our obligations under Indian law". It promises an acknowledgment within 24 hours and a resolution within 30 days, and the officer's contact is a Gmail address, not an address at wondeed.com. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/policies/grievance-officer 2026-11-14 -->

One file still carries another name: llms.txt, the summary the site publishes for AI assistants, opens with "# ClipForge" and places it at a vercel.app address. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/llms.txt 2026-11-14 --> That address served the same Wondeed pages on 14 November 2026, so it reads as a working name left in one file, not as a second brand.

Wondeed is also not wondeea.com: that address, one letter apart, serves an online shop, so shop reviews filed under that name say nothing about the clipping marketplace.

## The 20% platform fee, the $10 minimum and the monthly cycle

The fee is stated twice, from two sides. For brands: "The platform take is agreed per campaign — the standard rate is 20% of campaign spend." <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> For clippers: "Wondeed takes a platform fee from campaign payouts (set per campaign, standard rate 20%) — you keep the rest of the published rate." <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clip 2026-11-14 --> The rate on a card is therefore a gross figure, and because the fee is set per campaign, the card is where to read it. Joining costs nothing.

The pricing page sets a "$10 payout minimum" and says that "sub-minimum balances carry forward". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> The payouts guide adds: "Campaigns may set a minimum payout amount". It lists two payout methods: PayPal, by the email on your PayPal account, and "Crypto (USDC / USDT)" to a wallet address, with the warning that "crypto payouts can't be reversed". The network is not named. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/docs/payouts 2026-11-14 -->

The calendar is fixed. The clipper page shows a cycle that closes on the 1st of each month at 03:00 UTC, then "Admin review", then "Payout". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clip 2026-11-14 --> The Clipper Terms say each payout "passes admin review before release, which can take a few business days", and no page gives a payout date. A clip posted early in a month therefore waits about a month for the close, plus the review. Until then the post must stay up: "private or deleted posts can't be verified, and the earnings may be lost". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/policies/clipper-terms 2026-11-14 -->

## What is left of India on wondeed.com

Three traces remain on its pages. The clipper page still lists UPI beside PayPal and crypto, four times, while the payouts guide and the home page list PayPal and crypto only. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clip 2026-11-14 --> The Grievance Officer page cites Indian law, as quoted above. And the callback form on the brand page asks for a mobile number behind a fixed +91 prefix. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/brands 2026-11-14 -->

For a clipper in India the change is the currency and the rail: rates are in dollars, the documented methods are PayPal and stablecoins, and nothing about tax deducted at source is published any more. If UPI is why you are looking at Wondeed, ask support in writing whether it is offered before you post. The rupee boards that still pay by UPI are compared in [clipping platforms that pay in India](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-india).

For a clipper elsewhere, the residency clause is gone and nothing replaces it: no page lists supported or excluded countries, so access comes down to whether you can receive PayPal or hold a crypto wallet.

## Who holds the brand's budget, and what the beta terms leave open

Brands pay first. A brand "tops up their balance before anything goes live", and the pricing page calls the model "Prepay only". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> The site never says who keeps that balance, and the word escrow, which the refund policy we read on 24 September used for an "escrow pool", appears nowhere now.

The terms promise less than the marketing pages. "Payouts are processed in cycles and each payout is subject to admin review before release. We may hold or cancel payouts while fraud is being investigated." Until then the numbers are provisional: "Campaign budgets, view counts, and earnings are estimates until verified and paid." During beta Wondeed "may modify or discontinue features with or without notice". If your account is closed, "legitimate unpaid earnings from completed, verified cycles remain payable subject to review". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/policies/terms-of-service 2026-11-14 -->

Rejected clips "come back with a reason", and a fraud flag can be appealed through the Grievance Officer, "with a decision back within 30 days". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/trust 2026-11-14 --> With no named company and no court clause, that appeal is the whole dispute route.

## Two situations where Wondeed is the stronger pick

- **You want PayPal.** Wondeed documents PayPal payouts; DareBay pays in USDT on the TON network or in Telegram Stars.
- **Your Instagram or YouTube account already has 1,000 followers and you can wait a month.** That is the rule its docs enforce, and the cycle is monthly whatever a clip earns.

Even then, start with one campaign. Screenshot the card with its rate, fee and threshold, keep the post public, and let one cycle close and pay before you plan a month of posting. The first payout is the only check of the operator you can run.

## Wondeed or DareBay: a monthly PayPal cycle or a task card with its own limits

On Wondeed you see a campaign only after signing in; the rate is per 100K views, a standard 20% comes out of it, and the payout follows the monthly close by PayPal or stablecoin.

On DareBay the rate per 1,000 counted views, the view threshold and the cap per clip are set by each task and printed on its card: $1–$10 per 1,000 views depending on the task, up to $500 per clip. There is no application and no follower minimum, and DareBay keeps no country list: creators join worldwide under its Terms. A wallet-backed task locks its budget on the platform before it starts, and a manual-payout task, where the organizer pays directly, is labelled on the card. DareBay takes 0% of task earnings and deducts 10% by default when you withdraw; a withdrawal request starts at 10 USDT, the team checks it before sending, and the payout is USDT on the TON network or Telegram Stars.

Choose Wondeed for PayPal and a monthly rhythm on Instagram or YouTube. Choose DareBay when your account is new, when you want the limits in front of you before you edit, or when a wallet suits you better than PayPal. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Wondeed legit after it dropped its India-only rules?

Wondeed is a live site with public pricing, docs and beta terms, and it states its money rules in numbers: a standard 20% platform fee, a $10 minimum and monthly cycles. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-11-14 --> It does not publish who operates it, its pages contradict each other, and the product changed within a month of our October read. We executed no payout, so this page cannot confirm that it pays.

### Does Wondeed still pay in rupees by UPI?

Not as it did. The ₹500 UPI rule and the Indian-residency clause are gone, and rates are quoted in dollars. UPI appears on the clipper page only; the payouts guide lists PayPal and USDC/USDT. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/docs/payouts 2026-11-14 -->

### What do Wondeed's per-100K rates come to per 1,000 views?

Divide by 100. Its own examples of $40 and $50 per 100K views are $0.40 and $0.50 per 1,000, or $0.32 and $0.40 after the standard 20% fee. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clip 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-11-14 --> Real campaign rates are visible only after sign-in.

### Do I need 1,000 followers to earn on Wondeed?

According to its docs, yes: the account checker confirms "a 1,000+ follower count" before an account becomes Active. <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/docs/quickstart 2026-11-14 --> The home page says the opposite, "No following required". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-11-14 --> Treat 1,000 as the working rule.

### Can I post Wondeed clips on TikTok or X?

The home page and the docs name both, but the FAQ says "TikTok and X aren't available yet". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/faq 2026-11-14 --> Check a campaign's accepted platforms before you post.

### What is ClipForge in Wondeed's llms.txt?

It is the name the file gives the site: "ClipForge is a pay-per-view clipping marketplace". <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/llms.txt 2026-11-14 --> The address it names served the same Wondeed pages that day, so it looks like a working name never replaced in that file.
