## Who is behind Wondeed

Wondeed presents itself as "India's first performance-based short-form video clipping marketplace" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-09-18 -->. Its press page gives 2025 as the founding year and describes an "early-stage team", "India · Remote-first" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/press 2026-09-18 -->, while the about page names nobody <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/about 2026-09-18 -->. Contact is by email, with separate addresses such as trust@wondeed.com and grievance@wondeed.com <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/trust 2026-09-18 -->, and the terms, privacy, refund and grievance pages are all public.

What those pages leave open is the company. The terms say "Wondeed is operated by Wondeed" and date from 1 May 2025 <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->, and the GST page lists the legal entity as "Wondeed" and the GSTIN as "To be updated" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/gst 2026-09-18 -->. That says nothing about payouts either way; it means the payer's name has to come from a document you receive, described in the TDS section below.

## Wondeed, wondeea.com and other lookalike names

On 18 September 2026 Trustpilot had no page for wondeed.com, and the reviews under an almost identical spelling belong to wondeea.com, a separate website. That day wondeed.com served only the clipping marketplace: addresses a web shop would use, such as a contact or products page, led to its sign-in screen. We found no Wondeed app in Google Play or the App Store; the help centre describes sign-up on the website with an Indian mobile number and a one-time code <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/help 2026-09-18 -->.

## Tier card: ₹5, ₹7 or ₹10 per 1,000 views

The clipper page prints the rates in one line: "Rates: Standard ₹5 / 1K views · Medium ₹7 / 1K · High ₹10 / 1K. Actual rates vary by campaign. No ceiling — a viral clip keeps earning as long as views come in." <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 --> The ₹90 per 1,000 on the home page belongs to an illustrated campaign brief, not to that card <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-09-18 -->. Which row you can reach depends on your tier:

| Tier | Campaigns open to you | Active at once | Payout window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · New Clipper | standard-earning | up to 3 | 14 days |
| 2 · Verified | adds medium-earning | up to 8 | 7 days |
| 3 · Top Performer | all, with "higher per-view rates" | unlimited | 3-day fast-track |

The table condenses the clipper page, which adds that "Tier moves are reviewed monthly" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 -->. A brand on the free plan reaches Tier 1 only <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-09-18 -->, the tier you join a campaign at "is the rate you're paid for the duration" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/trust 2026-09-18 -->, and open campaigns appear only after sign-up, "filtered to your Tier" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/creator-guide 2026-09-18 -->.

## The payout clock: 72-hour hold, ₹500, UPI

A view passes three checks before it becomes money: it is pulled from "the official Instagram Graph API and YouTube Data API", screened on "watch-time, engagement rate and view velocity", and held for 72 hours <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/trust 2026-09-18 -->. You connect your account read-only ("without it, you can't earn"), a clip's approval "usually takes 24–48 hours" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/creator-guide 2026-09-18 -->, and the clip must stay public while it is tracked <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->.

Once the balance passes ₹500 you request a payout to a UPI ID linked to "a valid Indian bank account" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/help 2026-09-18 -->. The transfer time depends on the page: "within 7 days" in the clipper FAQ and a "14-day payout window" for Tier 1 on the same page <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 -->, "within 7 business days of a payout request" in the terms <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->. Nothing is taken from you, "0% taken from clippers" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-09-18 -->; clips keep earning for 7 days after a brand ends a campaign <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/faq 2026-09-18 -->, and views earned before a network removes a clip "are still paid" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 -->.

## How Wondeed makes money

"Wondeed earns from subscriptions, never from your view payouts," says the pricing page: Pro is free, Premium ₹8,000 a month and Enterprise ₹20,000, and a campaign starts at ₹20,000 <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/pricing 2026-09-18 -->. The terms call this charge a "platform fee to Brands, separate from the campaign budget deposited" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->, invoiced with GST at 18% <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/gst 2026-09-18 -->. The budget itself sits in what Wondeed calls an escrow wallet and moves "only after each view passes all three verification layers" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/trust 2026-09-18 -->; the privacy policy lists Meta, Google and Supabase as processors but does not name the payment processor <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/privacy 2026-09-18 -->.

## The terms, the GST page and the grievance officer

For a clipper the terms cover the essentials: 18 or over and "an Indian resident"; Wondeed's view count "is final"; unpaid earnings are forfeited on termination "for cause" and paid on termination "without cause"; liability is capped at three months of payments <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->. Two fields remain unfilled. Disputes go to "the courts of [Registered City], India" and may first be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->, and the GSTIN reads "To be updated" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/gst 2026-09-18 -->.

The grievance page says it follows Rule 3(2) of the IT Rules, 2021. The page and the rule, side by side:

## TDS: what Wondeed deducts and what the certificate shows

Where ClipGrow's terms leave tax to the clipper, Wondeed says it deducts first: "Clippers earning above the TDS threshold under Section 194-O of the Income Tax Act, 1961 will have TDS deducted at the applicable rate before payout", with "Form 26AS-reconcilable TDS certificates" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/gst 2026-09-18 -->. It names neither the threshold nor the rate. Section 194-O covers goods or services "facilitated by an e-commerce operator through its digital or electronic facility or platform" <!-- source: https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/budget2024-25/doc/memo.pdf 2026-09-18 -->, and for payments since 1 April 2026 the Income Tax Act, 2025 applies, with TDS rates and thresholds that "have been retained" <!-- source: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/%20tds%20compliance-faq 2026-09-18 -->.

The deduction is also the one check no web page can offer, because a TDS certificate names whoever deducted. The Form 16A template on the TRACES portal shows the "Name and address of the deductor", its PAN and TAN, and the "PAN of the deductee" <!-- source: https://traces61contents.tdscpc.gov.in/forms/Form16A.pdf 2026-09-18 -->, and the credit lands in your annual tax statement, which for tax year 2026-27 the department says will be Form No. 168 <!-- source: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/%20tds%20compliance-faq 2026-09-18 -->. Wondeed's privacy policy lists a mobile number and a UPI ID as the identity and payout data it collects and does not mention PAN <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/privacy 2026-09-18 -->, so ask before your first payout how a deduction will be credited to you.

## If a brand rejects your clip

Brands get 72 hours to reject a clip, and the home page says the view payout then "reverses automatically" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/ 2026-09-18 -->. The refund policy puts it differently for money already credited: earnings "once verified and credited to a Clipper's account balance, are non-refundable to the Brand", including views earned before a rejection inside that window <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/refund 2026-09-18 -->. To contest a rejection, the trust page offers "a second-pair-of-eyes review" within 48 hours <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/trust 2026-09-18 -->, the help centre shows the reason and frees your campaign slot <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/help 2026-09-18 -->, and the grievance address takes "payment disputes" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/grievance 2026-09-18 -->. With two written versions of one event, screenshot your dashboard at each step.

## Where Wondeed is the better pick than DareBay

Wondeed is the better pick when:

- you live in India and want rupees in a bank account through UPI. DareBay pays out in USDT on the TON network or in Telegram Stars, which Telegram's terms do not let you sell or withdraw from a personal balance <!-- source: https://telegram.org/tos/stars 2026-09-18 -->;
- you would rather keep virtual digital assets out of the picture: the 30% tax on their gains <!-- source: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/FileITR-2Online-FAQ 2026-09-18 --> is covered in the country view on [clipping platforms that pay in India](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-india);
- you want a published clock and tax deducted for you, whereas DareBay has each withdrawal request checked by a person and promises no settlement time;
- you want no platform cut: Wondeed takes 0% from clippers, and DareBay takes nothing from a task budget but 10% of each withdrawal request, deducted inside it, with requests from 10 USDT.

DareBay is the better pick when you are not an Indian resident, since it keeps no country list; when you are new, because a Wondeed beginner starts at Tier 1 on ₹5 per 1,000 views, three campaigns and a 14-day window, while a new DareBay account takes a task on the same terms as anyone, with no application or follower count; when you post on TikTok, which Wondeed calls "planned but not available yet" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/help 2026-09-18 -->, or prefer to submit a link rather than connect an account; and when the rate decides it: open DareBay tasks pay $1.00–$2.00 per 1,000 views with a cap of up to $500 per clip, next to Wondeed's ₹5–₹10, each in its own currency. The same questions about ClipGrow are answered in the [ClipGrow review](/en/earnings/clipgrow-review); [the platform comparison](/en/earnings/best-clipping-platforms) ranks the global boards, and [how much clipping pays](/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays) covers the per-view arithmetic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Wondeed legit?

Wondeed publishes working rules: tier rates, a 72-hour hold, UPI payouts requested from ₹500, a dispute route and a grievance address <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 -->. It does not publish who runs it, and we found no independent reviews either way. Three checks help: a first payout at the ₹500 minimum, the deductor named on your TDS certificate once tax is deducted, and a written answer from grievance@wondeed.com.

### Is Wondeed the same as wondeea.com?

No. Wondeed is the clipping marketplace at wondeed.com; reviews filed under wondeea.com, one letter apart, concern a different website.

### What is Wondeed's rate for 1,000 views?

The clipper page lists ₹5 for standard, ₹7 for medium and ₹10 for high-earning campaigns, adding "Actual rates vary by campaign"; new clippers start at Tier 1, which opens standard campaigns only <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 -->. The ₹90 on the home page is an illustration.

### How long do Wondeed UPI payouts take?

After the 72-hour hold you request a payout from ₹500. The clipper FAQ says "within 7 days", Tier 1 carries a 14-day window and Tier 3 a 3-day fast-track <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/clippers 2026-09-18 -->, and the terms say 7 business days <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->.

### Does Wondeed deduct TDS from clippers?

Its GST page says it deducts TDS under Section 194-O above a threshold it does not state and issues certificates that reconcile with Form 26AS <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/gst 2026-09-18 -->. You still file your own return.

### Who owns Wondeed?

It is not published. The terms say Wondeed "is operated by Wondeed" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/terms 2026-09-18 -->, the GST page leaves the GSTIN "To be updated" <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/gst 2026-09-18 -->, and the press page gives only the founding year, 2025 <!-- source: https://www.wondeed.com/press 2026-09-18 -->.
