# What DareBay pays per 1000 views

On DareBay the rate per 1000 views in pay-per-view contests runs from **$0.30 to $1.00**. The typical (median) rate in live contests is **$0.50 per 1000 views**. That is a parameter of the offer, set by the organizer when the contest is created — not a guaranteed income: what you end up with depends on how many views your submission gets. To take part in the budget split, a submission has to clear a view threshold: the typical threshold in live contests is **2000 views**, and the exact figure is stated on the contest card. The rate is always visible on the card before you submit.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## Pay-per-view parameters

| Parameter | Value | What it means |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Minimum rate | $0.30 per 1000 views | The low end across live contests |
| Median rate | $0.50 per 1000 views | The typical rate: half of contests pay this or more |
| Maximum rate | $1.00 per 1000 views | The high end across live contests |
| View threshold | 2000 views | Typical threshold in live contests (set by the organizer) |

The organizer sets the rate when creating the contest; across live contests it currently sits in this range. The rate is stated on the contest card: you always see what it pays before you submit.

## How the payout is calculated

The formula: `(your submission's views / 1000) * the contest rate`.

At a rate of $0.50 per 1000 views:

- 10,000 views: 10,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$5.00**
- 50,000 views: 50,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$25.00**
- 100,000 views: 100,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$50.00**

A contest may also cap the payout per submission, so the budget spreads across several creators. The typical cap in live contests is **$50** per submission — in the example above, 100,000 views at $0.50 lands exactly on it. The cap applies per submission: you can enter several different videos, and three at the cap is $150. The cap is shown on the contest card.

For the full mechanics, from locking the budget to the automatic payout, see [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

## How this compares with other platforms

Platforms like the TikTok Creator Fund or the YouTube Shorts Fund pay creators for views out of their own pool. Those rates depend on where the audience is, the niche and the platform's algorithms, and can swing noticeably month to month.

DareBay works differently: the rate is set by a specific buyer — the contest organizer — and is fixed for the whole contest. You see it on the card before you submit and know exactly what you are entering. The budget is locked on the platform up front: the platform acts as an intermediary and guarantor so neither side gets burned.

## Frequently asked questions

### What determines the rate per 1000 views?

The contest organizer sets it, based on their budget, niche and goals. The range across live contests is currently $0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views. The rate is fixed for the whole contest and does not change after launch.

### When does the money arrive?

After a contest ends there is a selection window and a results-confirmation window, usually a few days. After that the payout goes out automatically to your wallet, card or whichever method you chose. The platform guarantees the payout as intermediary: the budget was locked in advance.

### How are views counted?

Views are pulled automatically through the TikTok API. Neither the organizer nor the participant types a number in by hand. It is an independent count that neither side can adjust.

### Why is there a view threshold?

The threshold keeps the budget from being spread too thin. Without it, dozens of submissions with a handful of views each would take the budget apart a cent at a time. It rewards content that actually reaches people. The typical threshold in live contests is 2000 views; the exact figure is the organizer's.

### Can I take part without an audience?

Yes. There is no minimum follower count. The only condition is clearing the contest's view threshold (usually 2000) on the individual submission. Beginners compete on equal terms.

### Can the rate be changed after I submit?

No. The rate is fixed when the contest is created. The organizer cannot lower it after launch. The budget is frozen and the terms are locked.

### What payout methods are available?

DareBay supports several: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts. The wallet is one option among them, not the only one.
