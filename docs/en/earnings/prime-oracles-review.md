## What Prime Oracles is, and who runs it

The platform describes itself in the title of its own Russian-language home page as "маркетплейс цифровых продуктов и реварды", a marketplace of digital products and rewards <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->. There are two services behind that. One is a task board where "Заказчики пополняют Баланс Награды в криптовалюте" — customers fund a reward balance in crypto and post content tasks against it. The other is a marketplace where the buyer's money is "удерживаются в эскроу Площадки до подтверждения сделки", held in the platform's escrow until the deal is confirmed <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->.

The header of all three legal documents names the operator: a sole trader registered in Georgia, with an address in Tbilisi. His name and tax ID are printed there; we do not repeat them. Georgian law applies, and disputes that survive negotiation go to the courts of Tbilisi <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. The AML policy adds that no dedicated compliance officer has been appointed yet and that the operator performs the role personally <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-11-14 -->. The agreement also states what a balance is not: "Баланс Пользователя и Баланс Награды не являются банковскими счетами, депозитами или иной формой хранения денежных средств", not bank accounts, deposits or any other form of holding money, and no interest is paid on what sits there <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

The interface runs in two languages, Russian and English <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->. English pages exist throughout, and clause 22.5 settles which text counts: the Russian version prevails over any translation <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

## What a reward card prints

A card is readable before you register, and it prints the rate, the minimum payout per video together with the view count that reaches it, the maximum per video, the budget, how much has been paid out and what is left, the dates, the platforms, the brief and any penalties: "RATE PER 1K $1 · MIN. PAYOUT PER VIDEO $1 (1K views) · MAX. PAYOUT PER VIDEO $100 · CAMPAIGN BUDGET $3,496 · PAID OUT $2,961 (85%)" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards/861acf3b-b3cd-4168-a7f2-4f8219d0d2a6 2026-11-14 -->.

On 19 September 2026 there were 20 open rewards, with rates from $0.02 to $8.00 per 1,000 views and per-clip caps from $15 to $300 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards 2026-09-19 -->. The conversion to 1,000 views is ours: cards quote per 1K, per 10K or per 100K. Banner formats sit at the bottom of that range and UGC at the top, which the platform states itself, calling UGC the format with "самые высокие ставки за 1к просмотров", the highest rates per 1,000 views <!-- source: https://t.me/primeoracles/51 2026-09-19 -->. Fourteen of the twenty cards open that day carried Russian titles, by our count of what the board published; the platforms a card can ask for are TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->.

There is no platform-wide view threshold. It is derived by dividing the minimum payout by the rate: "$1 (1K views)" at $1 per 1,000, "$1 (5K views)" at $0.20 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards/ab95250c-fc25-4cda-afd1-ec1bdd688c89 2026-09-19 -->.

The mechanic that decides your income here is not the rate but the moment you submit: "одно видео — одна заявка", one video, one request. You choose when the post has gathered enough views, and "После выплаты повторно подать это же видео нельзя" — once it is paid, the same video cannot be submitted again <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->. The second limit is the till: "Заявки оплачиваются в порядке отправки. Дошли до нуля - награда закрывается, даже если срок формально ещё идёт", submissions are paid in the order they arrive and the reward shuts when the budget reaches zero, even if its end date has not passed <!-- source: https://t.me/primeoracles/40 2026-09-19 -->. And without the result there is no payment, "даже если контент создан, опубликован и соответствует всем требованиям", even where the content was made, published and met every requirement <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

The agreement of 9 November 2026 adds a second regime for the UGC and Clip formats, which it calls Continuous Bounties, and there you no longer pick the moment. In the platform's own English wording, "A Submission must be submitted within 60 (sixty) minutes of the publication of the content", and "After a Submission has been accepted, the Platform automatically tracks the views of the content for 7 (seven) days from the moment of submission"; to be paid you then send "at least one screen recording (video) and at least one screenshot from the official analytics of the publication platform" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->. Eight of the 30 claimable card pages on 14 November 2026 showed the step "Submit the link within 60 minutes" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards 2026-11-14 -->. The home-page FAQ has not caught up: it still says you "choose when the publication has reached an optimal or maximum allowed view count" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-11-14 -->. One more clause matters on those cards: "The remuneration accrued under a Continuous Bounty may exceed the part of the Reward Pool available at that moment. Such excess (the Over-Budget Amount, clause 6.7.5) is not secured by the Bounty Balance" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->. An amount the tracker shows above the pool is not money set aside for you.

## What a withdrawal costs and where it goes

The agreement prints the table of rails: USDT on Tron (TRC-20), BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20) and Solana (SPL), USDC on Ethereum (ERC-20) and Solana, and TON on its own network. Payments run "через сервис NowPayments (Эстония)", the minimum withdrawal is "от 5 до 12 USD в зависимости от выбранной сети", and the network fee comes off the withdrawal on top of everything else <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. The exact figure per network the agreement leaves to the interface and does not state itself.

The fee is published in the agreement itself: "Получение вознаграждения Креатором — 10% от суммы вывода", 10% of the withdrawal for a creator, against "Создание Награды (активация) — 0%" for posting a task <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. The same 10% appears in the home-page FAQ <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 --> and on the About page <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/about 2026-11-14 -->.

The edition of 9 November 2026 adds a charge on the customer's side: "The standard rate of the Bounty Commission is 15% (fifteen per cent) of the Bounty Balance", and it "is borne solely by the Customer and does not reduce the remuneration credited to the Creator" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->. The About page says the same in plainer words: "A 15% commission is reserved from the campaign budget. It is earned only on amounts paid to creators, and the unspent part is refunded in full." <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/about 2026-11-14 --> The home-page FAQ still mentions only the 10%: "All core platform actions are fee-free: purchases, refunds, balance top-ups, and using funds inside the platform. A 10% fee applies only to earned income and is withheld on withdrawal." <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-11-14 --> For a clipper this does not change the payout: the commission is the customer's cost, and 15% of the reward balance is reserved for it <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->.

The US-dollar channel is written into the agreement. Clause 9.4 provides for withdrawal "в долларах США (USD) через платёжного партнёра Garna (Кипр)", in US dollars through the payment partner Garna in Cyprus, says KYC on that channel is performed by Garna alone, and requires a verified Garna account to use it <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. What Garna charges on its own side the agreement does not publish. Garna's own page says "Garna is not a financial institution" and that its fintech services come from certified partners <!-- source: https://garna.io/en/for-contractors 2026-09-19 -->; its user agreement promises payment "within 10 (ten) banking days" and bars withdrawals to Crimea, Cuba, the so-called DPR and LPR, Iran, North Korea and Syria. Russia, Belarus and Kazakhstan are not on that list, but the next paragraph of the same agreement closes the service to anyone located in a country under a comprehensive embargo or financial sanctions of the UN Security Council, the EU, the UK or the US, and Garna does not name those countries <!-- source: https://app.garna.io/api/documents/agreement?lang=en 2026-09-19 -->. From a Garna account the money can move on to "your bank in USD, local currencies, debit cards, PayPal, Payoneer, or crypto wallets" <!-- source: https://garna.io/en/for-contractors 2026-09-19 -->, which is what the route is worth to a clipper who turns out to have it.

No timing is promised to a clipper. The home page says "Вывод средств — автоматический", calling the withdrawal automatic <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->, but no document says how long the money takes to travel from a request to a wallet. Money can be frozen "до 30 (тридцати) календарных дней", up to thirty calendar days; a customer is asked to review a submission within 48 hours, and after seven days of silence the platform may decide it instead <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. On a continuously tracked reward that review comes later still, because views are first tracked for 7 days from submission <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->.

## Who is barred, and which countries are named

Until November, clause 19.1 closed three markets at once. The agreement of 9 November 2026 rewrote it, in the platform's own English wording: "Residents and nationals of the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United States of America may access and use the Platform solely in the capacity of Creators — that is, to perform Content Bounties and to earn remuneration, which is credited to their User Balance and may be withdrawn in accordance with Section 9." The same clause bars them from funding a reward as customers, from buying on the marketplace and from any other crypto top-up or payment, and it keeps one outright bar: the services "are not provided in any form to residents or nationals of any jurisdiction in which the use of cryptocurrency services is restricted or prohibited under applicable law". The Russian text, which prevails, says the same: "исключительно в качестве Креаторов". The age floor is 18 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->.

The AML policy was not re-issued with the agreement and still carries 1 June 2026. It says the opposite: "The Platform does not provide services to the following categories of persons: Residents and nationals of the European Union, the United Kingdom and the United States of America", and "The Platform implements technical measures (IP geo-blocking, wallet-address screening, device fingerprinting) to prevent the above-mentioned persons from accessing its services." <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-11-14 --> The agreement calls that policy an integral part of itself, and neither document says which one wins <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->. We did not test access from those regions, so whether the geo-block has been lifted is not something this page can tell you. If you live in the EU, the UK or the US, treat earning here as permitted by one document and refused by the other.

There is no list of served countries. The single place where countries are named positively is the tax clause 11.1: Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Moldova, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan "и иные государства — участники СНГ", and other CIS member states <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. Ukraine is not among them: across the three documents it appears once, as the row "Ukraine – SFMS" in the AML sanctions-screening table, that is, as a list the platform checks against <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-09-19 -->. The public currency switcher meanwhile offers "$ USD · € EUR · ₽ RUB · Br BYN · ₴ UAH" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards 2026-11-14 -->, but that is a display currency, not a statement about a country.

If you are reading this from South Asia, Africa, the Gulf or anywhere else outside the EU, the UK and the US, the honest answer is that no page names your country either way, and clause 19.2 hands you the check: "Пользователи из иных стран самостоятельно обязаны убедиться в соответствии использования Площадки законодательству страны своего проживания до начала использования" — users from other countries must satisfy themselves that using the platform is lawful where they live, before they start <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

No passport is asked for at sign-up: clause 3.2 asks for "Действующий адрес электронной почты; Имя (можно псевдоним); Пароль", a working email address, a name or a pseudonym, and a password <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. KYC is conditional and its triggers are published — large operations, unusual patterns of activity, requests from regulators or law enforcement, signs of a politically exposed person, signs of a barred jurisdiction — and a paid clip has to stay publicly available for 30 calendar days <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. No follower minimum and no application are published anywhere.

## Where Prime Oracles fits better than DareBay, and where it does not

DareBay's side of the ledger: nothing is taken from a task payout, a withdrawal costs 10% (default) of the request deducted inside a request of at least 10 USDT, and the only cash rail is USDT on the TON network. Every request is checked by a person, so no settlement time is promised, and there is no country exclusion list, no follower minimum and no application. The interface is in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish and Spanish. Its task card states the rate per 1,000 eligible views and any cap, and the task sets its own threshold: [how withdrawals work](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals), [what DareBay charges](/en/help/what-commission), [DareBay at a glance](/en/about/darebay-at-a-glance).

Prime Oracles is the better fit when:

- **You want US dollars rather than crypto and can hold a verified Garna account.** The agreement offers USD through Garna (Cyprus) to a verified Garna account, and Garna's own agreement closes its service to countries under UN, EU, UK or US financial sanctions without naming them. DareBay has one rail and it is crypto.
- **Your wallet is not on TON, or you want a lower floor.** Five chains, and a withdrawal floor the agreement puts at 5 to 12 USD depending on the network, against 10 USDT.
- **You want to see your own country written into the document.** Clause 11.1 names ten CIS states by name.
- **You want every task's budget frozen, not some of them.** The reward balance is frozen at activation in all cases, with 15% of it reserved for the platform's commission; on continuously tracked UGC and clip rewards, earnings accrued beyond the reward pool are not secured by it <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->. On DareBay only a wallet-backed task locks its budget on the platform before the start, while a manual-payout task is labelled on the card.
- **You want a second income stream in the same account.** A digital-goods marketplace with escrow, with products listed from $5 to $499 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/marketplace 2026-09-19 -->.

The other side of it: residents and citizens of the EU, the UK and the US may only earn as creators under the agreement of 9 November 2026, while the AML policy still lists them as not served and geo-blocked; Ukraine is not among the named countries, one video is paid once, the customer approves every payout, funds can be frozen for up to 30 days, and the USD route through Garna needs a verified Garna account. ### Which countries can use Prime Oracles?

There is no list of served countries. Under the agreement of 9 November 2026, residents and citizens of the EU, the UK and the US may only earn as creators and may not fund rewards or buy on the marketplace <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-11-14 -->; the AML policy of 1 June 2026 still says they are not served <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-11-14 -->. Anywhere the use of crypto services is restricted is barred outright. Ten CIS states are named in the tax clause; Ukraine is not among them, and no country in South Asia, MENA or Africa is named either way.

### Does Prime Oracles require KYC?

Not at sign-up: the agreement asks for an email, a name or a pseudonym and a password. After that it is conditional, and the triggers are published. On the Garna USD channel KYC is always required, and Garna performs it.
