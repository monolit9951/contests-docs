## What Prime Oracles is, and who runs it

The platform describes itself in the title of its own home page: "Prime Oracles (Прайм Ораклс) — маркетплейс цифровых продуктов и реварды", a marketplace of digital products and rewards <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->. There are two services behind that. One is a task board where "Заказчики пополняют Баланс Награды в криптовалюте" — customers fund a reward balance in crypto and post content tasks against it. The other is a marketplace where the buyer's money is "удерживаются в эскроу Площадки до подтверждения сделки", held in the platform's escrow until the deal is confirmed <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

The header of all three legal documents names the operator: a sole trader registered in Georgia, with an address in Tbilisi. His name and tax ID are printed there; we do not repeat them. Georgian law applies, and disputes that survive negotiation go to the courts of Tbilisi <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. The AML policy adds that no dedicated compliance officer has been appointed yet and that the operator performs the role personally <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-09-19 -->. The agreement also states what a balance is not: "Баланс Пользователя и Баланс Награды не являются банковскими счетами, депозитами или иной формой хранения денежных средств", not bank accounts, deposits or any other form of holding money, and no interest is paid on what sits there <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

The interface runs in two languages, Russian and English <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->. English pages exist throughout, and clause 22.5 settles which text counts: the Russian version prevails over any translation <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

## What a reward card prints

A card is readable before you register, and it prints the rate, the minimum payout per video together with the view count that reaches it, the maximum per video, the budget, how much has been paid out and what is left, the dates, the platforms, the brief and any penalties: "RATE PER 1K $1 · MIN. PAYOUT PER VIDEO $1 (1K views) · MAX. PAYOUT PER VIDEO $100 · CAMPAIGN BUDGET $2,500 · PAID OUT $2,023 (81%)" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards/861acf3b-b3cd-4168-a7f2-4f8219d0d2a6 2026-09-19 -->.

On 19 September 2026 there were 20 open rewards, with rates from $0.02 to $8.00 per 1,000 views and per-clip caps from $15 to $300 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards 2026-09-19 -->. The conversion to 1,000 views is ours: cards quote per 1K, per 10K or per 100K. Banner formats sit at the bottom of that range and UGC at the top, which the platform states itself, calling UGC the format with "самые высокие ставки за 1к просмотров", the highest rates per 1,000 views <!-- source: https://t.me/primeoracles/51 2026-09-19 -->. Fourteen of the twenty cards open that day carried Russian titles, by our count of what the board published; the platforms a card can ask for are TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->.

There is no platform-wide view threshold. It is derived by dividing the minimum payout by the rate: "$1 (1K views)" at $1 per 1,000, "$1 (5K views)" at $0.20 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards/ab95250c-fc25-4cda-afd1-ec1bdd688c89 2026-09-19 -->.

The mechanic that decides your income here is not the rate but the moment you submit: "одно видео — одна заявка", one video, one request. You choose when the post has gathered enough views, and "После выплаты повторно подать это же видео нельзя" — once it is paid, the same video cannot be submitted again <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->. The second limit is the till: "Заявки оплачиваются в порядке отправки. Дошли до нуля - награда закрывается, даже если срок формально ещё идёт", submissions are paid in the order they arrive and the reward shuts when the budget reaches zero, even if its end date has not passed <!-- source: https://t.me/primeoracles/40 2026-09-19 -->. And without the result there is no payment, "даже если контент создан, опубликован и соответствует всем требованиям", even where the content was made, published and met every requirement <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

## What a withdrawal costs and where it goes

The agreement prints the table of rails: USDT on Tron (TRC-20), BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20) and Solana (SPL), USDC on Ethereum (ERC-20) and Solana, and TON on its own network. Payments run "через сервис NowPayments (Эстония)", the minimum withdrawal is "от 5 до 12 USD в зависимости от выбранной сети", and the network fee comes off the withdrawal on top of everything else <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. The exact figure per network the agreement leaves to the interface and does not state itself.

The fee is published in the agreement itself: "Получение вознаграждения Креатором — 10% от суммы вывода", 10% of the withdrawal for a creator, against "Создание Награды (активация) — 0%" for posting a task <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. The same 10% appears in the home-page FAQ <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 --> and on the About page <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/about 2026-09-19 -->.

The card rail is written into the agreement. Clause 9.4 provides for withdrawal "в долларах США (USD) через платёжного партнёра Garna (Кипр)", in US dollars through the payment partner Garna in Cyprus, and says KYC on that channel is performed by Garna alone <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. What Garna charges on its own side the agreement does not publish. Neither does the platform publish whether the channel is open to an ordinary clipper, so check your own account before you count on it. Garna's own page says "Garna is not a financial institution" and that its fintech services come from certified partners <!-- source: https://garna.io/en/for-contractors 2026-09-19 -->; its user agreement promises payment "within 10 (ten) banking days" and bars withdrawals to Crimea, Cuba, the so-called DPR and LPR, Iran, North Korea and Syria. Russia, Belarus and Kazakhstan are not on that list, but the next paragraph of the same agreement closes the service to anyone located in a country under a comprehensive embargo or financial sanctions of the UN Security Council, the EU, the UK or the US, and Garna does not name those countries <!-- source: https://app.garna.io/api/documents/agreement?lang=en 2026-09-19 -->. From a Garna account the money can move on to "your bank in USD, local currencies, debit cards, PayPal, Payoneer, or crypto wallets" <!-- source: https://garna.io/en/for-contractors 2026-09-19 -->, which is what the route is worth to a clipper who turns out to have it.

No timing is promised to a clipper. The home page says "Вывод средств — автоматический", calling the withdrawal automatic <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/ 2026-09-19 -->, but no document says how long the money takes to travel from a request to a wallet. Money can be frozen "до 30 (тридцати) календарных дней", up to thirty calendar days; a customer is asked to review a submission within 48 hours, and after seven days of silence the platform may decide it instead <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

## Who is barred, and which countries are named

Clause 19.1 closes three markets at once, in the platform's own English wording: "The Platform's services are not provided to residents or nationals of the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States of America, or any jurisdiction in which the use of cryptocurrency services is restricted or prohibited under applicable law." That is not a disclaimer only: the platform "применяет технические меры (геоблокировка, скрининг кошельков, проверка цифровых отпечатков)", geo-blocking, wallet screening and device fingerprinting. The age floor is 18 <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

There is no list of served countries. The single place where countries are named positively is the tax clause 11.1: Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Moldova, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan "и иные государства — участники СНГ", and other CIS member states <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. Ukraine is not among them: across the three documents it appears once, as the row "Ukraine – SFMS" in the AML sanctions-screening table, that is, as a list the platform checks against <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/aml-policy 2026-09-19 -->. The public currency switcher meanwhile offers "$ USD · ₽ RUB · Br BYN · ₴ UAH" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/rewards 2026-09-19 -->, but that is a display currency, not a statement about a country.

If you are reading this from South Asia, Africa, the Gulf or anywhere else outside the barred three, the honest answer is that no page names your country either way, and clause 19.2 hands you the check: "Пользователи из иных стран самостоятельно обязаны убедиться в соответствии использования Площадки законодательству страны своего проживания до начала использования" — users from other countries must satisfy themselves that using the platform is lawful where they live, before they start <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->.

No passport is asked for at sign-up: clause 3.2 asks for "Действующий адрес электронной почты; Имя (можно псевдоним); Пароль", a working email address, a name or a pseudonym, and a password <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. KYC is conditional and its triggers are published — large operations, unusual patterns of activity, requests from regulators or law enforcement, signs of a politically exposed person, signs of a barred jurisdiction — and a paid clip has to stay publicly available for 30 calendar days <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/terms-agreement 2026-09-19 -->. No follower minimum and no application are published anywhere.

## What Prime Oracles does not publish

Any settlement time for a clipper, either from an approved submission to a credited balance or from a withdrawal request to money arriving. A list of the countries it serves, and any answer on whether it pays to Ukraine. Whether the Garna card channel is open to an ordinary account. The size of network fees, and what Garna charges on its own side. A team page: the About page says only "The platform is built by professionals in cybersecurity, fintech, and media" <!-- source: https://www.primeoracles.com/about 2026-09-19 -->. An archive of finished tasks, and what to do if a withdrawal request is not approved.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Prime Oracles?

A marketplace of digital products plus a board of video tasks paid per view. The operator is a sole trader registered in Tbilisi, Georgia, and the platform's interface runs in Russian and English.

### Is Prime Oracles legit, and where do its reviews live?

It names its operator, publishes terms, a privacy policy and an AML policy under Georgian law, and prints each task's budget and how much of it is left. What is missing is other people's experience: a search on 19 September 2026 found no review page on any aggregator, so no payout record can be confirmed from outside. The platform also pays for video reviews of itself, which is worth knowing before you weigh one.

### Does Prime Oracles pay?

Its counters on 19 September 2026 showed $22,674 paid out, and the customer's reward balance is set aside before the task goes live. The customer approves each payout, however, and the platform publishes no settlement time.

### How do you withdraw money from Prime Oracles?

In USDT, USDC or TON to a wallet: the agreement puts the minimum at 5 to 12 USD depending on the network. US-dollar withdrawal to a card or bank account through Garna is described in the terms and needs a verified Garna account with KYC. Whether that route is open to an ordinary account is not published, so check it in your own account.

### What fee does Prime Oracles charge?

10% of the withdrawal amount, charged on what you earned, with 0% to create a task and network fees on top. What Garna charges on its own side of the card route is not published in the agreement.

### Which countries can use Prime Oracles?

There is no list of served countries, only a bar: residents and citizens of the EU, the UK and the USA, and anywhere the use of crypto services is restricted. Ten CIS states are named in the tax clause; Ukraine is not among them, and no country in South Asia, MENA or Africa is named either way.

### Does Prime Oracles require KYC?

Not at sign-up: the agreement asks for an email, a name or a pseudonym and a password. After that it is conditional, and the triggers are published. On the Garna card channel KYC is always required, and Garna performs it.
