# How to start earning from clips with nothing

You need no followers, no portfolio and no editing experience. You film short videos to a contest brief, publish with the hashtag, and the platform counts the views and pays by formula. Rates across live contests run from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views** (median **$0.50**). A submission has to reach at least **1000 views** (the default threshold) to join the split. The cap per submission is **$50**. The contest budget is locked in advance and the payout runs automatically against counted views — the buyer cannot take the money back while the contest is running.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## A worked example

At the median rate of **$0.50 per 1000 views** with a **$50** cap per submission:

- **5,000 views**: $0.50 * 5,000 / 1000 = **$2.50**
- **20,000 views**: $0.50 * 20,000 / 1000 = **$10.00**
- **100,000 views**: the formula gives $50, and the cap holds it at **$50**

The cap applies to each submission separately. Entering several contests, or several submissions where that is allowed, means each is counted on its own with its own cap. Two clips at the cap is $100.

This is arithmetic on the inputs, not a promise of income. The rate, threshold and cap are set by the buyer when the contest is created.

## What you need to start

| What you need | What for |
|---------------|----------|
| A DareBay account | Signing up, choosing contests, submitting work |
| An account on the platform (TikTok, YouTube) | Publishing the video; views are counted through its API |
| A phone or a camera | Filming to the brief |
| **1000 views** on the submission (the threshold) | The minimum to join the budget split |

Not needed: followers, channel verification, a partner programme, a portfolio, an invite, paid access.

## The beginner's path

1. **Sign up at [DareBay](https://darebay.com).** Free, with no audience requirement.
2. **Pick a contest.** The catalogue shows every parameter: rate, threshold, cap, deadlines, brief. Look for a subject you understand and a rate you like.
3. **Film the clip to the brief.** Read the terms on the card first.
4. **Publish it with the hashtag.** Upload to the platform (TikTok, YouTube Shorts) with the hashtag named in the contest, then submit it on DareBay.
5. **Get past the threshold.** The default is **1000 views**. Below it, the submission takes no part in the split.
6. **Get paid.** When the contest ends the platform counts views through the platforms' APIs and splits the budget automatically.

The platform acts as intermediary and guarantor: the budget is locked until results are settled and the view count is independent. The buyer cannot change the terms after launch.

These are offer parameters, not an income promise. All of them are on the contest card before you submit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need followers?

No. Followers affect neither eligibility nor the payout. An account with zero competes on the same terms as one with a million.

### What will I actually make?

It depends on the contest rate and your views. At the median $0.50 per 1000 with 20,000 views, the payout is $10. The cap per submission in typical contests is $50.

### How high is the view threshold?

The default is **1000 views** on one submission. For short video on TikTok or YouTube Shorts that is reachable even on a new account: the algorithms surface clips regardless of follower count.

### Where do I start with no experience?

Pick a contest with a simple, clearly stated brief. Streamer clips need minimal editing: find a strong moment, cut a short video, add the hashtag.

### What does taking part cost?

Nothing. The commission is paid by the buyer when the contest is created, never by the participant.

### How does the money arrive?

Several ways: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts.

### How are views counted?

Automatically through the platforms' APIs (TikTok, YouTube). Neither the buyer nor the participant types a figure in by hand. More in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).
