# How to start earning from clips with nothing

You can start today: no followers, no portfolio, no editing experience. You film a short video to the contest task, publish it with the hashtag and send in the link, and the platform counts the views and pays by formula. Rates across live contests run from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views** (median **$0.50**). A submission joins the split once it reaches **1000 views**, the default threshold. The cap per submission is **$50**. The contest budget was locked before you started: the buyer cannot pull the money back, and the views you earn get paid for.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## A worked example

At the median rate of **$0.50 per 1000 views** with a **$50** cap per submission:

- The clip reaches **5,000 views**: $0.50 * 5,000 / 1000 = **$2.50**
- The clip reaches **20,000 views**: $0.50 * 20,000 / 1000 = **$10.00**
- The clip reaches **100,000 views**: the formula gives $50, and the cap holds it at **$50**

The cap applies to each submission separately. Enter several contests, or several submissions where that is allowed, and each is counted on its own with its own cap. Two clips at the cap is $100.

This is arithmetic on the inputs. The rate, threshold and cap are set by the buyer when the contest is created, and all three are open on the card before you submit.

## What you need to start

| What you need | What for |
|---------------|----------|
| A DareBay account | Signing up, choosing contests, submitting work |
| An account on the platform (TikTok, YouTube) | Publishing the video; views are counted through its API |
| A phone or a camera | Filming to the task |
| **1000 views** on the submission (the threshold) | The minimum to join the budget split |

Not needed: followers, channel verification, a partner programme, a portfolio, an invite, paid access.

## The beginner's path

1. **Sign up at [DareBay](https://darebay.com).** Free, with no audience requirement.
2. **Pick a contest.** The catalogue shows every parameter: rate, threshold, cap, deadlines, task. Take a subject you understand and the highest rate you can find.
3. **Film the clip to the task.** Read the terms on the card first.
4. **Publish it with the hashtag.** Upload to the platform (TikTok, YouTube Shorts) with the hashtag named in the contest.
5. **Send the link.** Paste the link to the published video into the contest card on DareBay. Only then does the submission join the budget split: the hashtag alone will not get it picked up.
6. **Get past the threshold.** The default is **1000 views**. Below it, the submission takes no part in the split.
7. **Receive a credit or obligation.** At contest close, the platform counts views and records the amount. Fulfilment can be manual.

The platform acts as intermediary and guarantor: the budget is locked until results are settled and the view count is independent. The buyer cannot change the terms after launch.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need followers?

No. Followers affect neither eligibility nor the payout. An account with zero competes on the same terms as one with a million. The money follows the views of that one submission.

### What will I actually make?

Run the formula: `rate * views / 1000`, capped at the per-submission limit. At the median $0.50 per 1000 with 20,000 views, the payout is $10. The cap per submission in typical contests is $50, and several clips mean several payouts of up to $50. Two numbers decide it: the contest rate and the reach of your video.

### How high is the view threshold?

The default is **1000 views** on one submission. For short video on TikTok or YouTube Shorts that is reachable even on a new account: the algorithms surface clips regardless of follower count.

### Where do I start with no experience?

Pick a contest with a simple, clearly stated task. Streamer clips need minimal editing: find a strong moment, cut a short video, add the hashtag. Start with one contest and watch how the views come in.

### What does taking part cost?

Nothing. Entry is free, and contest creation currently carries no fee. A later withdrawal of a credited balance has separate terms.

### How does the money arrive?

The contest defines its reward method. The balance-withdrawal wizard offers USDT to an external wallet and Telegram Stars; the form shows the available option, and processing is manual.

### How are views counted?

Automatically through the platforms' APIs (TikTok, YouTube). It is an independent count: neither the buyer nor the participant types a figure in by hand or can nudge it. More in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).
