## What counts as a clipper's income

Start with the basis of the payment: counted views under task terms, a fixed editing fee or another agreed reward. Keep the task card or contract and payer details. The app name, payment in USDT and a similar amount do not determine the income classification.

Timing is harder. Every payout has three dates: the credit to your balance, the withdrawal to your TON wallet and the swap into local currency. Which one is the date of income depends on the country and the regime; ask your tax office and meanwhile record all three.

## Worldwide: the freelancer's baseline

Three rules hold almost everywhere. Tax is paid where you are tax resident, usually where you spend most of the year. For a resident, income from abroad is income like any other; a foreign platform does not exempt it. Regular task earnings count in many countries as self-employment with its own status, sole trader or freelancer; whether and from what turnover you must register depends on the country.

What to keep:

- the TON wallet statement;
- credits exported from your DareBay cabinet, task by task;
- the task terms and the platform's offer;
- every USDT-to-fiat swap with date and rate.

Country pages elsewhere in this section carry tax notes of their own: [clipping platforms that pay in India](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-india) quotes the Income Tax Department on the flat rate for virtual digital assets and the Schedule VDA disclosure, [clipping platforms that pay in Nigeria, Kenya and Ghana](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-nigeria-and-kenya) quotes the Nigeria Revenue Service on income received in virtual assets and the KRA excise duty, and [clipping platforms that pay in Egypt and Arab countries](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-egypt-and-arab-countries) points to the turnover-based regime Egypt set out in Law No. 6 of 2025. The pages on [Bangladesh and Nepal](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-bangladesh-and-nepal) and on [Indonesia and the Philippines](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-indonesia-and-the-philippines) do the same: the first covers the exemption for listed digital services that Bangladesh's Finance Act 2026 widens to freelancing and content creation, and the advance tax deducted in Nepal from foreign-currency pay for social-media content; the second sets Indonesia's PPh brackets beside the flat-rate option the BIR sets out for the self-employed.

## Russia: self-employment and NPD

Self-employment in Russia is the tax on professional income (NPD), a regime for individuals and sole traders with no staff. The Federal Tax Service lists 4% on payments from individuals and 6% from sole traders and companies <!-- source: https://npd.nalog.ru/ 2026-09-04 -->, an income cap of 2.4 million rubles a year <!-- source: https://npd.nalog.ru/ 2026-09-04 -->, no tax return with income logged in the Moy Nalog app <!-- source: https://npd.nalog.ru/ 2026-09-04 -->, and access for citizens of Russia, the EAEU and Ukraine <!-- source: https://npd.nalog.ru/ 2026-09-04 -->.

The Federal Tax Service bars NPD, USN and AUSN payers from their regime if they issue and then sell digital currency <!-- source: https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/promo/mining/ 2026-09-04 -->, and taxes an individual's sale of digital currency at 13%, or 15% above 2.4 million rubles a year <!-- source: https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/promo/mining/ 2026-09-04 -->. Payment for work in USDT is a different case the NPD page does not cover: how to log it in Moy Nalog is a question for your inspectorate or an accountant.

## Ukraine: FOP and personal income

First option: a FOP (private entrepreneur) on the single tax. Group 3 pays 5% of income, or 3% for VAT payers <!-- source: https://zir.tax.gov.ua/docs/stavki/sproshena_sistema_opodatkuvannya_(ediniy_podatok).pdf 2026-09-04 -->, within an annual limit of 1,167 minimum wages <!-- source: https://zir.tax.gov.ua/docs/stavki/sproshena_sistema_opodatkuvannya_(ediniy_podatok).pdf 2026-09-04 -->, plus a 1% military levy <!-- source: https://zir.tax.gov.ua/docs/stavki/viysjkoviy_zbir.pdf 2026-09-04 -->. The key condition: settlements must be monetary, cash or non-cash, and income received in any other form is taxed at 15% <!-- source: https://zir.tax.gov.ua/docs/stavki/sproshena_sistema_opodatkuvannya_(ediniy_podatok).pdf 2026-09-04 -->. Whether a USDT transfer counts as monetary is the tax service's call; ask it or an accountant before routing payouts to a FOP.

Second option: an individual without a FOP declares income from abroad as foreign income in the annual declaration of property and income, taxed at 18% personal income tax <!-- source: https://zir.tax.gov.ua/docs/stavki/podatok_na_dohody_fizychnyh_osib.pdf 2026-09-04 --> plus a 5% military levy <!-- source: https://zir.tax.gov.ua/docs/stavki/viysjkoviy_zbir.pdf 2026-09-04 -->, with USDT converted to hryvnia at the rate on the date of receipt. Rules for virtual assets are still changing; check the current status on tax.gov.ua.

## Kazakhstan and the rest of CIS

Since 1 January 2026 Kazakhstan runs an updated regime based on the simplified declaration; for a clipper that means an IP, an individual entrepreneur. The rate is 4% of income, local maslikhats may move it by up to 50% <!-- source: https://vko.kgd.gov.kz/ru/news/specialnyy-nalogovyy-rezhim-na-osnove-uproshchennoy-deklaracii-8-160720 2026-09-04 -->, and Astana set 3% for 2026 <!-- source: https://astana.kgd.gov.kz/ru/news/snr-na-osnove-uproshchennoy-deklaracii-kto-smozhet-primenyat-rezhim-v-2026-godu-2-163012 2026-09-04 -->. The income ceiling is 600,000 MCI a year, the tax period a half-year <!-- source: https://vko.kgd.gov.kz/ru/news/specialnyy-nalogovyy-rezhim-na-osnove-uproshchennoy-deklaracii-8-160720 2026-09-04 -->. A separate self-employed regime also exists <!-- source: https://vko.kgd.gov.kz/ru/news/izmeneniya-v-specialnyh-nalogovyh-rezhimah-8-163528 2026-09-04 -->; whether clipping fits it and how USDT receipts are recorded, ask your state revenue department.

Elsewhere in CIS the principle is the same: declare income in local currency at the rate on the date of receipt, and check your tax authority for a self-employed regime.

## What the platform does and does not do

DareBay pays for counted views in USDT but is not a tax agent: it withholds no tax and issues no statements in your tax office's format. It keeps the history of credits and withdrawals in your cabinet; no tax status is needed to take a task, so that decision is yours.

In a wallet-backed task, the balance is credited when the task ends. If the task offers "Claim now", approved credits can be locked in earlier after the displayed maturation period. Withdrawal is a separate operation: a request of at least 10 USDT, a default 10% fee deducted inside the request, and the team checks the details and sends it; see [DareBay withdrawals](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals) and [DareBay fees](/en/help/what-commission). Record both the requested and the received amount: they differ by that fee (a 100 USDT request at the default rate pays out 90 USDT), and a tax office may ask for either. Telegram Stars are "get it in Stars", not a cash withdrawal; how a tax office treats them is its call.

## A ten-minute monthly ledger

Once a month, combine cabinet, wallet and exchange operations in one ledger. Record actual dates and amounts; fill the tax valuation and recognition date according to the rule your accountant or tax office confirms for your regime.

| Date | Task | Credited, USDT | Withdrawn, USDT | Rate | Local currency |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| submission approved | task, clip link | cabinet amount | 0 | rate that day | credited × rate |
| withdrawal request | request number | 0 | requested / received | rate that day | received × rate |
| swap | exchange | 0 | 0 | deal rate | deal amount |

Which total you report, credited or withdrawn, depends on how your country reads the moment of income; the table shows either. Size the columns with the [clipping earnings calculator](/en/earnings/clipping-earnings-calculator); rate, threshold and cap are explained in [how much clipping pays](/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays).

## One work payment, three linked events

Hypothetical example: a task credits 20 USDT, a withdrawal request for 20 USDT sends 18 USDT after the default 10% fee, and you later exchange it for local currency. These are linked events, rather than three equal work payments. Give them one earnings reference and separate operation IDs:

| Event | Evidence to keep | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Work credit | Task, clip URL, date and cabinet entry | 20 USDT |
| Withdrawal | Request ID, fee, wallet address and transaction hash | 20 requested, 2 fee, 18 USDT received |
| Exchange | Trade document, date, actual rate and exchange charge | Local currency received |

Give your accountant the chain before filing. Ask about the income recognition date, USDT valuation method, fee treatment and any separate consequences of the exchange. Russia's mining and digital-currency disposal guidance cannot automatically determine the treatment of editing fees paid in USDT: the source and type of transaction differ. <!-- source: https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/promo/mining/ 2026-10-12 -->

For an organizer's direct payment, keep their confirmation and payment evidence. The DareBay wallet history cannot substitute for an external payment it never recorded.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I owe tax while the USDT sits in my DareBay wallet?

It depends on which date your country treats as the moment of income: credit, withdrawal or swap. Record all three and ask your tax office.

### How do I report a USDT payout in my own currency?

First establish which recognition date and valuation method your tax regime requires for services paid in USDT. Keep the actual quote and exchange document; do not automatically substitute the dollar rate, because USDT and USD are different assets. The Federal Tax Service mining page covers specific transactions rather than a universal rule for editing fees. Ask your tax office or accountant which method applies. <!-- source: https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/promo/mining/ 2026-10-12 -->

### Can I start clipping first and register as self-employed later?

Yes: DareBay needs no status to pay you, but the duty to pay tax does not wait: income arises from the first payout, not from the registration date.

### Does DareBay issue an income statement for my tax office?

No. It issues no statements in a tax office's format and withholds no tax; your evidence is the history of credits and withdrawals in the cabinet, the TON wallet statement and the task terms.
