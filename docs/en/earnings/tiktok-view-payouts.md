# What TikTok pays for views

There are two routes to getting paid for views on TikTok. The first is TikTok's own creator programme (the Creativity Program), where the platform pays you, but you need an established audience and steady reach. The second is an intermediary platform like DareBay, where a specific buyer pays for the views of your video out of a budget locked in advance. DareBay has no audience requirement: rates run from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views**, the median rate is **$0.50 per 1000 views**, and the typical threshold for joining the split is **2000 views** (the threshold is the organizer's to set, and the exact figure is on the contest card).

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## Two routes to monetizing views

### The TikTok creator programme (Creativity Program)

TikTok pays creators out of its own fund. What it takes to get in:

- An established audience (tens of thousands of followers)
- Videos longer than a minute
- Consistently large reach over the past month
- Only certain regions, and the list of eligible countries is short

Rates depend on where your audience is, on your niche and on the algorithms. They are opaque and can shift month to month. TikTok does not publish exact figures.

### An intermediary platform (DareBay)

DareBay's model is different: the rate is set by a specific buyer (the contest organizer) and is fixed for the whole contest. The budget is locked on the platform in advance: the platform acts as intermediary and guarantor, and the payment comes out of the buyer's locked pool rather than a platform fund. There is no follower requirement. Beginner clippers compete on the same terms as experienced authors.

## The payment parameters on DareBay

| Parameter | Value | What it means |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Minimum rate | $0.30 per 1000 views | The low end across live contests |
| Median rate | $0.50 per 1000 views | Half of contests pay this or more |
| Maximum rate | $1.00 per 1000 views | The high end across live contests |
| View threshold | 2000 views | The median across live contests; the exact threshold is the organizer's and is on the card |
| Payout model | PPV (pay-per-view) | Paid in proportion to views out of a locked budget |

The rate is stated on the contest card: you see the terms before you submit.

## Side by side: the TikTok programme vs an intermediary platform

| Criterion | TikTok programme | DareBay (intermediary) |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Who pays | The platform, out of a fund | The buyer, out of a locked budget |
| Followers needed | Yes, an established audience to get in | No requirement |
| Rate | Opaque, floating | Fixed, visible on the card |
| View threshold | A high monthly reach bar to get in | Typically 2000 on one submission |
| Payout guarantee | Depends on the fund | The budget is locked in advance |
| Payout method | An internal TikTok balance | Wallet, card, bank, Stars, gifts |

## How the payout is worked out on DareBay

The formula: `(your submission's views / 1000) * the contest rate`.

At the median rate of $0.50 per 1000 views:

- 5,000 views: 5,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$2.50**
- 20,000 views: 20,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$10.00**
- 100,000 views: 100,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = **$50.00**

The typical cap per submission across live contests is **$50** (the median) - in the example above, 100,000 views at $0.50 land exactly on it. The cap applies to the submission, not to the author: you can enter several different clips, and several submissions at the cap already add up to $100 and more. The cap is on the contest card. Views are pulled automatically through the TikTok API: neither the buyer nor the participant types figures in by hand.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need followers to take part?

No. DareBay has no minimum follower requirement. The only condition is clearing the view threshold (typically 2000, stated on the contest card) on a specific submission. That is the fundamental difference from the TikTok programme, which needs an established audience just to get in.

### What kind of videos work?

Videos that match the brief of a specific contest. Every contest describes the content it wants. It might be a review, an unboxing, a challenge or any other format the buyer specified. The video is published on TikTok (or another platform) with the required hashtag.

### When does the money arrive?

After the contest ends there is a selection and results-confirmation window (usually a few days). Then the payout runs automatically. The budget was locked in advance and the platform as intermediary guarantees the payout. The methods: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars, gifts.

### Can the buyer change the rate after launch?

No. The rate is fixed when the contest is created. The organizer cannot lower it after launch. The budget is frozen and the terms are fixed.

### Why is there a view threshold?

The threshold keeps the budget from being spread too thin. Without it, dozens of submissions with a handful of views each would take the budget apart penny by penny. The threshold rewards content that actually gets reach.

### How does the platform guarantee the payout?

The buyer locks the budget when creating the contest. The money sits with the platform as intermediary until the contest ends and is then split automatically by formula: it either goes to the participants or returns to the buyer as the remainder.

### Is this an alternative to the TikTok programme?

It is an additional monetization channel. The TikTok programme and DareBay do not exclude each other; you can use both. The difference: the programme requires an audience and pays out of a platform fund, while DareBay requires no followers and pays out of a specific buyer's budget.
