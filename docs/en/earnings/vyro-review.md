## Vyro in three numbers

Vyro quotes rates per million views: in early September live campaigns stood at $1,000–$2,000 per 1M, which is $1–$2 per 1,000 views <!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-09-02 -->, and on 5 September the six open campaigns showed $1,500 or $2,000 per 1M <!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-09-05 -->.

The second number is the threshold: each approved post must reach 5,000 views on its own before it earns anything, not cumulatively across the campaign <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-09-05 -->. If your average clip does not clear five thousand, most of your work pays zero.

The third is the cap: one clip is paid for at most 1 million eligible views, typically $500–$2,000, and each campaign prints its own maximum <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-09-05 -->. The cap touches only a viral clip; the threshold touches every one.

A hypothetical 20,000-view clip on Vyro at $2,000 per 1M: 20,000 / 1,000 × $2.00 = $40.00. A 4,000-view clip on Vyro earns $0. On DareBay with a typical threshold of 2,000 views: 20,000 / 1,000 × $1.00 = **$20.00** at the floor of open tasks, the threshold is not deducted and the typical cap per clip of $100 is far off; the 4,000-view clip is paid too: 4,000 / 1,000 × $1.00 = $4.00. The rate matters less than the share of clips that gets paid.

Inside are the terms and a folder of approved footage <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/where-can-i-find-content-to-clip 2026-09-05 -->. The route in: sign up, join a campaign, read the brief, upload the clip, get approved <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/how-can-i-get-started 2026-09-05 -->. Accounts are 18+ <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/how-old-do-i-need-to-be-to-use-vyro 2026-09-05 --> with verified social accounts <!-- source: https://www.vyro.com/clipper-terms 2026-09-05 -->.

Earnings count from the moment of submission, not posting <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/why-arent-my-earnings-matching-the-cpm-on-the-campaign 2026-09-05 -->, so submit at once. A campaign you can see but cannot join is usually near its end date or budget limit <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/campaigns/why-am-i-unable-to-join-a-campaign 2026-09-05 -->.

## Countries Vyro does not support

The unsupported list runs to 70 countries and territories, and the restriction comes from the payment processor, not the platform <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-05 -->. From CIS and its neighbours:

- Russia, Belarus, Ukraine;
- Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia, Moldova;
- Turkey, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, Egypt, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Thailand <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-05 -->.

Kazakhstan is not listed, and unlisted countries can use Vyro if they meet the other requirements <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/what-countries-are-supported 2026-09-05 -->.

Do not route around the list with a borrowed PayPal: the terms require legal residence where the platform is available, and Vyro can claw back payouts <!-- source: https://www.vyro.com/clipper-terms 2026-09-05 -->. ## The upsides worth admitting

- **Top-tier campaigns.** MrBeast, the U.S. Open, D23, Teamfight Tactics: footage that pulls views on its own.
- **Terms are written down.** Threshold, cap, payout timing and the country list are in a public help center.
- **The budget is real.** Brands fund it upfront, $5,000 minimum <!-- source: https://vyro.com/for-brands 2026-09-05 -->, and the unused part is refunded.
- **A low cash-out minimum.** From $10 <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-can-i-withdraw-my-earnings 2026-09-05 -->, with no follower requirement published.

## The downsides reviews keep quiet about

- **Money arrives only after the campaign ends.** It sits in Pending Balance until a final review, about 7–10 days later <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/when-will-i-receive-my-earnings 2026-09-05 -->.
- **Cash-outs once every 7 days, PayPal or Stripe only** <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-can-i-withdraw-my-earnings 2026-09-05 -->. Vyro publishes no platform fee, and the terms warn that payment providers may deduct fees <!-- source: https://www.vyro.com/clipper-terms 2026-09-05 -->.
- **The rate on the card is not guaranteed.** Near the end of the budget payments are prorated among all clippers <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/why-arent-my-earnings-matching-the-cpm-on-the-campaign 2026-09-05 -->, and views after it runs out are unpaid unless the brand tops it up <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/what-are-eligible-views 2026-09-05 -->.
- **Payouts can be reclaimed.** The terms let Vyro claw back money for activity later judged ineligible, and cash-outs take at least 72 hours <!-- source: https://www.vyro.com/clipper-terms 2026-09-05 -->.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Vyro legit?

Yes. Vyro is a MrBeast company built by Beast Industries <!-- source: https://vyro.com/about 2026-09-05 -->; the terms are in a public help center and brands fund the budget upfront. Check the country list first: a blocked country can neither use the platform nor get paid.

### What is Vyro's payout threshold?

Two. A post earns after 5,000 views of its own, per clip. Cash-outs start at $10, once every 7 days, via PayPal or Stripe, after the money moves from Pending to Available Balance, about 7–10 days after the campaign ends.

### Does Vyro work in Russia, Ukraine or Kazakhstan?

Russia, Belarus and Ukraine are on the unsupported list with Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova. Kazakhstan is not, and unlisted countries can use the platform if they meet the other requirements. DareBay pays in all of them; it has no list.

### What happens to views after a Vyro campaign's budget runs out?

They stay eligible but unpaid unless the brand tops up the budget; near its end payouts are prorated among all clippers, so the effective rate can fall below the card.
