## Vyro in three numbers

Vyro quotes rates per million views: on 14 November 2026 its public board listed 12 campaigns at $1,000–$2,500 per 1M, which is $1.00–$2.50 per 1,000 views, and six of the twelve were locked for new posts <!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-11-14 -->.

The second number is the threshold: each approved post must reach the campaign's minimum views on its own before it earns anything, not cumulatively across the campaign. For most campaigns that is 5,000 views, and the brief shows it as "Min. views per post" <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-11-14 -->. If your average clip does not clear that minimum, most of your work pays zero.

The third is the cap, and here Vyro's own documents disagree. The help center says one clip can earn up to $1,000, with the exact maximum stated in each campaign <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whats-the-most-i-can-earn-per-clip 2026-11-14 -->. The Creator Terms, last modified on 28 October 2026, say "there is no per-clip cap on View Payouts", which run until the campaign's views pool is spent <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->. Plan around the maximum printed on the campaign. Either way the cap touches only a viral clip; the threshold touches every one.

A dollar cap is not a universal view cap. Hypothetically, at $1 CPM a $1,000 cap is reached at 1 million eligible views; at $2 CPM the same cap is reached at 500,000; at $2.50 CPM, the top of the 14 November board, at 400,000. Read the maximum in the brief before estimating a viral clip.

A hypothetical 20,000-view clip on Vyro at $2,000 per 1M: 20,000 / 1,000 × $2.00 = $40.00. A 4,000-view clip on a Vyro campaign with the usual 5,000-view minimum earns $0. On DareBay, where each task sets its rate per 1,000 eligible views, take the bottom of that band: 20,000 / 1,000 × $1.00 = **$20.00**, and the threshold is not deducted; under the system default threshold of 1,000 views, the 4,000-view clip is paid too: 4,000 / 1,000 × $1.00 = $4.00<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/earnings/how-much-clipping-pays 2026-09-24 -->. The rate matters less than the share of clips that gets paid.

## Campaigns: what they look like and how to get in

A campaign card shows the creator or studio, the rate per 1M views and the posting date <!-- source: https://vyro.com/campaigns 2026-09-05 -->. Inside are the terms and a folder of approved footage <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/where-can-i-find-content-to-clip 2026-09-05 -->. The route in: sign up, join a campaign, read the brief, upload the clip, get approved <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/how-can-i-get-started 2026-09-05 -->. One step now comes first: before you can join a campaign you are asked to complete identity verification and payout setup, both handled by Whop <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->. Accounts are 18+ <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/getting-started/how-old-do-i-need-to-be-to-use-vyro 2026-09-05 -->, and connecting the social accounts you post from is a condition of taking part <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->.

You post the clip on your own TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or X account; Facebook isn't supported <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/clips-and-content/which-platforms-can-i-post-on 2026-11-14 -->. Then press Submit post inside the campaign and add the link <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/campaigns/how-can-i-upload-a-video-to-a-campaign 2026-09-05 -->. Earnings count from the moment of submission, not posting <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/why-arent-my-earnings-matching-the-cpm-on-the-campaign 2026-09-05 -->, so submit at once. A campaign you can see but cannot join has usually been locked for new posts, because it is close to its budget or because the Vyro team locked it <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/campaigns/why-am-i-unable-to-join-a-campaign 2026-11-14 -->; one marked "Not available in your region" is a separate case, covered in the next section.

## Countries Vyro does not support

Vyro's help center now lists 28 countries to which it "doesn't currently support payouts" <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->. The list we read in September was more than twice as long; the page that carried it now returns an error, and the list sits in the Eligibility article, last updated on 30 October 2026. The 28 names:

- Russia, Belarus and Ukraine, the last as a whole country with no regional qualifier;
- Burundi, Central African Republic, Comoros, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Lesotho, Liberia, Libya, Nigeria, Somalia, South Sudan and Sudan;
- Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria and Yemen;
- Afghanistan, Myanmar, North Korea and Papua New Guinea;
- Cuba, Haiti and Venezuela <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->.

None of the other countries our September reading named is on the list now: Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia, Moldova, Turkey, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, Egypt, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Thailand are gone from it, and Kazakhstan was never on it <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->. That is not a promise that Vyro pays there. Vyro publishes no list of supported countries, and three checks remain for anyone off the list. Whop decides which ID documents and personal details it accepts in your country <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whop-verification 2026-11-14 -->. You must select the country where you legally reside and hold valid identification, and have a bank account in that country <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->. And each brand chooses the regions its campaign runs in.

That last limit is separate from the payout list. A campaign that does not run in your region still shows in the list, dimmed and marked "Not available in your region", and Vyro checks both the country in your profile and the country you are connecting from <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/campaigns/why-am-i-not-seeing-any-campaigns 2026-11-14 -->. Vyro does not publish which regions a campaign runs in.

Do not route around the list with someone else's account: the Creator Terms require legal residence where the platform is available, and Vyro can claw back payouts <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->. Platforms that state they pay in your country are collected on [clipping platforms that pay in Russia and CIS](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-russia-and-cis); DareBay is compared below.

## The upsides worth admitting

- **Top-tier campaigns.** MrBeast's own videos and campaigns from large creators and studios: footage that pulls views on its own.
- **Terms are written down.** Threshold, cap, payout timing and the country list are in a public help center, though the help center and the Creator Terms disagree on the cap and on the cash-out minimum.
- **The budget is funded upfront, with a caveat.** The About page says brands fund a campaign budget upfront and that unused budget is refunded when a campaign ends <!-- source: https://vyro.com/about 2026-11-14 -->. The Creator Terms add that a campaign may be published before the brand's funds have settled, and that if they fail to arrive Vyro may cancel it "without issuing any Payouts, even if submissions have already been approved" <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->.
- **A low cash-out minimum.** $10 per the help center <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/payouts 2026-11-14 -->, though the Creator Terms say $25 <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->. Vyro sets no platform-wide follower minimum <!-- source: https://vyro.com/about 2026-11-14 -->, though a campaign can ask for one <!-- source: https://vyro.com/changelog/follower-and-audience-requirements 2026-11-14 -->.

## The downsides reviews keep quiet about

- **Money arrives only after the campaign ends.** It sits in Pending Balance until a final review, about 7–10 days later <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/when-will-i-receive-my-earnings 2026-09-05 -->.
- **Cash-outs once every 7 days, and only to a Whop wallet** <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/payouts 2026-11-14 -->. Since 2 November 2026 "Whop replaces Stripe and PayPal": you withdraw from Whop to a bank account, PayPal, Venmo, a mobile wallet or crypto, depending on your country, and anyone who was paid through Stripe or PayPal has to connect a Whop account by 30 November 2026 <!-- source: https://vyro.com/changelog/more-ways-to-get-paid-with-whop 2026-11-14 -->. Vyro creates a new Whop account for you under the email on your Vyro account, and you cannot link one you already have <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/whop-verification 2026-11-14 -->. Vyro says it charges nothing to send money to your Whop wallet and that it sends a cash-out there right away; cashing out of Whop can take additional time, depending on the payout method, and Whop can charge a fee for some methods and for conversion from USD <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/payouts 2026-11-14 --> <!-- source: https://vyro.com/changelog/more-ways-to-get-paid-with-whop 2026-11-14 -->.
- **Two Vyro pages still describe the old rail.** The Creators page says "Withdrawals are processed via Stripe or PayPal depending on your location" <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creators 2026-11-14 -->, and the About page says earnings "can be withdrawn weekly (minimum $10) via Stripe or PayPal" <!-- source: https://vyro.com/about 2026-11-14 -->. The help center and the changelog are the ones that match what the Creator Terms require: "You must use Whop to receive and withdraw Payouts" <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->.
- **The rate on the card is not guaranteed.** Vyro can change a rate during a campaign, and accounts added with a bio code earn 25% less <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/why-arent-my-earnings-matching-the-cpm-on-the-campaign 2026-11-14 -->. Near the end of the budget payments are prorated among all clippers <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/why-arent-my-earnings-matching-the-cpm-on-the-campaign 2026-09-05 -->, and views after it runs out are unpaid unless the brand tops it up <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/what-are-eligible-views 2026-09-05 -->.
- **Payouts can be reclaimed.** The Creator Terms let Vyro claw back money for activity later judged ineligible, and say payouts may take at least 72 hours of processing <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Vyro legit?

Yes. Vyro is a MrBeast company built by Beast Industries <!-- source: https://vyro.com/about 2026-09-05 -->, and its Creator Terms say the platform is owned or controlled by Beast GCP, LLC <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->; the terms are in a public help center, and its About page says brands fund the budget upfront, though the Creator Terms let Vyro cancel a campaign without payouts if the brand's funds fail to arrive <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->. Check the country list first: Vyro does not pay 28 countries, and identity verification and payout setup through Whop come before you can join a campaign <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->.

### What is Vyro's payout threshold?

Two. A post earns once it reaches the campaign's minimum views on its own, 5,000 on most campaigns <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/how-many-views-do-i-need-before-i-can-start-earning 2026-11-14 -->. Cash-outs go to a Whop wallet once every 7 days, from $10 per the help center and the changelog <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/payouts 2026-11-14 --> or from $25 per the Creator Terms of 28 October 2026 <!-- source: https://vyro.com/creator-terms 2026-11-14 -->, after the money moves from Pending to Available Balance, usually 7 to 10 days after the campaign ends <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/when-will-i-receive-my-earnings 2026-11-14 -->.

### Does Vyro work in Russia, Ukraine or Kazakhstan?

Russia, Belarus and the whole of Ukraine are on Vyro's list of 28 countries it does not pay. Kazakhstan is not on it, and neither are Uzbekistan, Armenia, Georgia or Moldova, which the earlier list named. Being off the list is not a promise of payment: cash-outs go through Whop, which runs the identity check, and you need a bank account in the country you select <!-- source: https://vyro.com/help/earnings-and-payments/eligibility 2026-11-14 -->. DareBay pays in all of them: it keeps no country list, and only people on sanctions lists are barred<!-- source: https://darebay.com/en/legal/terms 2026-09-24 -->.

### What happens to views after a Vyro campaign's budget runs out?

They stay eligible but unpaid unless the brand tops up the budget; near its end payouts are prorated among all clippers, so the effective rate can fall below the card.
