# Where to find clipping work - a marketplace for clips

Paid clipping work sits on DareBay's open contest board, where a clipper picks their own briefs with no application, no interview and no portfolio review. Pay per view across live contests runs from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views**, with a payout threshold of **2000 views** per submission (set by the buyer; 2000 is the median across live contests). The buyer's money is locked on the platform before you film, views are counted independently, and the payout follows the formula: the platform works as intermediary and guarantor. You take the reward to a wallet (USDT on TON), a bank card, a bank transfer, Telegram Stars or Telegram gifts. The commission comes out of the buyer's side: **5%** on fiat payments (card, bank) and **8%** when paying from a wallet. Taking part costs a clipper nothing.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## A worked example

You find a contest paying $0.50 per 1000 views. You cut the clip, publish it on TikTok with the required hashtag and submit it. Your clip reaches 40,000 counted views:

`40,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = $20`

The whole amount arrives once the contest closes. The budget was locked by the platform before you started and the buyer cannot pull it back.

If the same clip reaches 150,000 views:

`150,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = $75, but the cap trims the payout to $50`

The typical cap per submission is **$50**. The buyer sets the exact figure when creating the contest and it sits on the brief card before you submit.

You can enter several submissions in one contest and work several contests at once. Each submission is counted on its own and the cap applies to each separately, so two clips at the cap make $100.

## How to start earning from clips

1. Sign up at [DareBay](https://darebay.com); an account is all you need, with no followers and no portfolio
2. Open the contest board and pick a brief on a subject that suits you (streamer clips, challenges, reviews)
3. Read the terms on the card: rate per 1000 views, cap per submission, total budget, deadlines
4. Film the video to the brief and publish it on TikTok with the required hashtag
5. Send DareBay the link to the published video, which is how the work enters the contest
6. The contest closes, the platform takes the views from the platforms' APIs and credits the payout

More on how views are counted and the budget is split in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

## Getting paid across borders

You do not need an agency, a manager or an account with a local payment processor to collect. A wallet payout (USDT on TON) settles across borders in minutes, and a bank card, a bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts sit next to it as separate options you choose from. Nothing about the reward depends on where the buyer banks: the money is already on the platform when you start filming.

## Why an intermediary and guarantor, not direct payment

When a clipper takes work directly, in Telegram chats or on freelance boards, the risk of not being paid lands entirely on them. The buyer vanishes, renegotiates after the fact or understates the view count, and there is nothing to hold them to.

DareBay closes that off. The buyer locks the budget on the platform when creating the contest. Views are counted independently from the platforms' APIs. The payout is computed by formula and goes to the participants without the buyer's involvement: their consent is not needed at that step, because they gave it when they froze the money.

This is not "take our word for it", it is a concrete mechanic: the budget is locked, the count is independent, and the payout follows rules fixed before the work began.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an audience to take work?

No. There is no minimum follower requirement. An account with zero followers competes on the same terms as one with a million. Payment tracks the views of a specific submission and nothing else. The payout threshold in a typical live contest is **2000 views** per submission, which is about a clip's reach, not about your audience.

### What is the commission and who pays it?

The buyer pays it when creating the contest: **5%** on fiat payments (card, bank) and **8%** when paying from a wallet (USDT on TON). The clipper pays no commission, and nothing is withheld from your payout.

### How do I get the money?

Five methods, and you choose: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts. All of them pay out.

### Is it safe?

Yes, and that rests on the mechanic rather than on reputation. The contest budget is locked before the start and the buyer cannot pull it back. Views are counted independently. The payout follows the formula without the buyer's involvement. More on the guarantees in [does DareBay really pay?](/en/about/does-darebay-really-pay)

### What kind of briefs are there?

The subject is up to the buyer: streamer clips, product reviews, challenges, creative videos to a brief. Every contest spells out exactly what it wants filmed, and which briefs you take is your call.

### How many briefs can I take at once?

As many as you like. Work several contests in parallel and submit several clips to one contest where its terms allow it.

### What is the minimum view threshold?

The buyer sets the threshold in each contest, and across live contests the typical figure (the median) is **2000 views** per submission. That is the minimum for a submission to join the budget split. For short video on TikTok, a new account clears it too.
