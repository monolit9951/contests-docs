# Where to find clipping work - a marketplace for clips

On DareBay a clipper picks their own briefs from an open board of contests. Pay per view across live contests runs from **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views**, with a payout threshold of **2000 views** per submission (set by the buyer; 2000 is the median across live contests). The budget is locked in advance, the payout is automatic and follows an independent count, and the platform works as intermediary and guarantor. The platform is open to Russia and the CIS, and payouts arrive to a wallet (USDT on TON), a bank card or by bank transfer; the reward can also be taken in Telegram Stars or gifts. The commission is paid by the buyer: **5%** on fiat payments (card, bank) and **8%** when paying from a wallet. Taking part costs a clipper nothing.

Every figure on this page comes from the platform's own data (snapshot: 2026-07-10).

## A worked example

Say you find a contest with a rate of $0.50 per 1000 views. You cut the clip, publish it on TikTok with the required hashtag and submit it. Your clip reaches 40,000 counted views:

`40,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = $20`

The whole amount arrives automatically once the contest ends. The budget is already locked by the platform and the buyer cannot pull it back.

If the same clip reaches 150,000 views:

`150,000 / 1000 * $0.50 = $75, but the cap trims the payout to $50`

The typical cap per submission is **$50**. The exact figure is set by the buyer when creating the contest and is on the brief card before you submit.

You can enter several submissions in one contest and take part in several contests at once. Each submission is counted on its own, and the cap applies to each separately: two clips at the cap is $100.

## How to start earning from clips

1. Sign up at [DareBay](https://darebay.com) - all you need is an account, no followers and no portfolio
2. Open the contest board and pick a brief on a subject that suits you (streamer clips, challenges, reviews)
3. Read the terms on the card: rate per 1000 views, cap per submission, total budget, deadlines
4. Film the video to the brief and publish it on TikTok with the required hashtag
5. Submit the work to the contest
6. When the contest ends the platform counts views through the platforms' APIs and credits the payout automatically

More on how views are counted and the budget is split in [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

Many Western UGC marketplaces are unavailable from Russia and the CIS, or do not support withdrawal to local bank cards. DareBay works without a geo-block for users in that region and supports several payout methods.

## Why an intermediary and guarantor, not direct payment

When a clipper takes work directly (in Telegram chats, on freelance boards), the risk of not being paid lands on them. The buyer can refuse to pay, understate the view count, or simply vanish.

On DareBay the buyer locks the budget on the platform when creating the contest. Views are counted automatically through the platforms' APIs. The payout is computed by formula and goes to the participants without the buyer's involvement. The money is frozen before you start work.

This is not "take our word for it", it is a concrete mechanic: the budget is locked, the count is independent, the payout is automatic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an audience to take work?

No. There is no minimum follower requirement. An account with zero followers competes on the same terms as one with a million. The payment depends only on the views of a specific submission. The payout threshold in a typical live contest is **2000 views** per submission, and that is about a clip's reach, not about your audience.

### What is the commission and who pays it?

The buyer pays it when creating the contest: **5%** on fiat payments (card, bank) and **8%** when paying from a wallet (USDT on TON). The participant (the clipper) pays no commission.

### How do I get the money?

DareBay supports several methods: wallet (USDT on TON), bank card, bank transfer, Telegram Stars and Telegram gifts. You choose. Withdrawal works from Russia and the CIS.

### Is it safe?

The platform works as intermediary and guarantor. The contest budget is locked in advance and the buyer cannot pull it back after launch. Views are counted automatically through the platforms' APIs. The payout follows the formula without the buyer's involvement. More on the guarantees in [does DareBay really pay?](/en/about/does-darebay-really-pay)

### What kind of briefs are there?

The subject is up to the buyer: streamer clips, product reviews, challenges, creative videos to a brief. Every contest describes exactly what it wants filmed. You choose which briefs to take.

### How many briefs can I take at once?

There is no limit. You can take part in several contests in parallel and submit several works to one contest where the terms allow it.

### What is the minimum view threshold?

The threshold is set by the buyer in each contest; across live contests the typical figure (the median) is **2000 views** per submission. That is the minimum for a submission to join the budget split. For short video on TikTok it is reachable even on a new account.
