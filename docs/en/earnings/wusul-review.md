## Who runs Wusul

The terms name the operator: Wusul is "operated by Camaran Group OÜ, a company registered in Estonia", with disputes "subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Estonia" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/terms 2026-09-19 -->. Nothing else is published: no registration number, address, phone, founding year or person.

Its own pages date it. The blog runs from 10 to 27 August 2026 and no further <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/blog 2026-09-19 -->, and four home-page counters hold phrases where numbers belong, captioned "Fresh off launch — these grow every day" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en 2026-09-19 -->.

## Five legal pages, all marked drafts

Terms of service, clipper terms, privacy, cookie policy and acceptable use each carry the same banner: "Template — pending legal review… It has not been reviewed by a lawyer" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->. All five close on "legal@wusul.example (placeholder — to be replaced with a live contact address)" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/privacy 2026-09-19 -->. The working address, hello@wusulclips.com, sits only on the contact page <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/contact 2026-09-19 -->.

None carries a date, although section 10 promises changes posted "here with a new effective date". Two clauses matter: liability capped at "the amount you were owed, if any, in the three months before the claim arose", and access ending "at any time, with or without notice… for any other reason at our reasonable discretion" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/terms 2026-09-19 -->.

## Who holds the money is not published

Section 1 says brand billing and clipper payouts are "currently handled directly by our team rather than through automated in-platform checkout", and section 5 that budgets are "arranged directly with our team" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/terms 2026-09-19 -->. The home page says "Wusul pays clippers for every 1,000 real views" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en 2026-09-19 -->, yet no clause in either set of terms states who owes you the money once a clip is approved — the brand, or Camaran Group OÜ. On [Vues](/en/earnings/vues-review) one sentence of the terms settles that.

The word "escrow" appears nowhere on the site. The privacy policy names Supabase and Resend, no payment provider at all, and says "payout details are exchanged directly when a payment is arranged" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/privacy 2026-09-19 -->. The nearest thing to a promise is marketing: "We never show a balance we can't pay" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/about 2026-09-19 -->.

## What can cost you earnings

Views "must come from real, organic reach"; bots, view farms and engagement pods bring rejection "and may result in account suspension and forfeiture of related earnings", and each submission is read by a person, "a manual, hands-on process rather than an automated one" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->. A refusal shows "the reason… in your submissions list" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/blog/verified-views-explained 2026-09-19 -->.

Three things are missing, not strict. No page sets out an appeal, a dispute procedure or arbitration. No rule makes you keep a clip online once a campaign ends, unlike the 60-day rule in the [Reach.cat review](/en/earnings/reach-cat-review). And there is no identity check: no page mentions KYC or a government-issued document; a bio code proves the account is yours, "no passwords, no app access" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en 2026-09-19 -->.

## Who picks the payout rail: three answers

The clipper terms leave it to you: "Choose whichever method works where you bank" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->. The about page makes it your country's <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/about 2026-09-19 -->. The blog makes it the brief's: "The campaign's currency (EGP or USD) decides how you're paid" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/blog/payouts-instapay-egp 2026-09-19 -->. Timing splits the same way: the home page says "Cash out the moment your views clear 1,000" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en 2026-09-19 -->, while the clipper terms say "Once a campaign closes, your approved clips are reviewed and paid within 30 days" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->.

## Who should pick Wusul over DareBay, and who should not

DareBay's side: it takes nothing out of a task payout, a withdrawal costs 10% of the request and is deducted inside a request of at least 10 USDT, the one cash rail is USDT on the TON network, and each request is checked by a person with no settlement time promised — [how withdrawals work](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals), [the fees](/en/help/what-commission).

Wusul fits better when:

- **You are in Egypt and need pounds in an account.** Its clipper terms list "Instapay, PayPal, and USDT" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->, and its about page puts Instapay on the Egyptian side of that split <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/about 2026-09-19 -->; no page says who sends that transfer, or from where. In Egypt, Algeria and Iraq a DareBay balance has no withdrawal route today, as [the country page](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-egypt-and-arab-countries) shows regulator by regulator.
- **You want to work in Arabic.** Wusul runs a full right-to-left Arabic locale; DareBay's interface is English, Russian and Ukrainian.
- **Your wallet is not on TON.** Its payout block names USDT on Tron (TRC-20), USDT and USDC on Ethereum (ERC-20) <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en 2026-09-19 -->; DareBay offers nothing else.
- **You want no platform cut on the way out.** "Wusul takes its cut from the brand's campaign budget, not from your earnings" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/for-clippers 2026-09-19 -->, and it publishes no fee of its own on the way out, though its clipper terms leave you "any fees charged by your payout provider" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->; DareBay's 10% comes out inside the request.

It is the worse choice if the money matters this week: no campaign is open, the leaderboard is empty, the rules an undated template with a stand-in contact, nobody named as holding a budget — where a wallet-backed DareBay task locks its budget before it starts. Neither publishes a country list or asks for followers or an application, so those are not advantages either <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/compare/wusul-vs-clipping-net 2026-09-19 -->.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Wusul legit?

It names an operator, prints rates per 1,000 verified views and a payment clause, says of an average clipper income "We won't invent one" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/blog/how-much-do-arabic-clippers-earn 2026-09-19 --> and captions its brand logos as illustrative. It does not publish a registration number, dated terms, a payment provider or anyone holding the budget, and on 19 September 2026 it had no open campaign. Two checks: ask hello@wusulclips.com for the entity and its registration number, and wait for a card to open.

### Does Wusul pay clippers in Egypt through InstaPay?

That is what its pages say: Instapay is one of three payout methods and the one assigned to Egypt, in pounds to a bank or mobile wallet. No page names the bank, the operator or the sending jurisdiction, and no campaign was open to test.

### Are there any live Wusul campaigns right now?

Not on 19 September 2026. The board announced "3 campaigns available" and every card read Closed with 0 days left. The clipper page agreed: "No live campaigns right now".

### Who runs Wusul?

Camaran Group OÜ, registered in Estonia, under Estonian law and its courts. Registration number, address, founders and team are not published.

### How many views or followers do I need?

One thousand views on a clip: clips that miss it "earn nothing for that submission, even if the content itself is approved" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/clipper-terms 2026-09-19 -->. Followers are not counted — "Wusul pays per verified view, not per follower" <!-- source: https://www.wusulclips.com/en/platforms/tiktok 2026-09-19 --> — and a code in your bio replaces any document.

### What are the alternatives if Wusul has nothing open?

[The Egypt and Arab countries page](/en/earnings/clipping-platforms-that-pay-in-egypt-and-arab-countries) sorts the boards by the rail that reaches your country. DareBay has no exclusion list and an open board, but one cash rail in USDT on TON, so in Egypt, Algeria and Iraq a balance has no withdrawal route today.
