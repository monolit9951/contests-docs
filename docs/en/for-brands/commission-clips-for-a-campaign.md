# How to commission clips for a campaign

Commissioning clips on DareBay takes no negotiation and no advance payment to a freelancer: you create a contest with a brief, lock the budget on the platform, and the clippers take the brief themselves. You pay only for validated views, not for the fact that a clip was made. The budget is locked from the first second and the platform works as an intermediary and guarantor: the buyer cannot take the money back before the contest ends, and the clipper is paid automatically by counted results. You never pay for work nobody watched.

## The model: the buyer creates a contest, clippers take the brief

On DareBay the buyer does not hunt for a contractor and does not haggle. You create a PPV contest (pay-per-view) with a brief and terms, the budget is locked on the platform, and clippers find the contest in the feed and submit their work. The platform counts views automatically, the budget is split by formula among the submissions that cleared the threshold, and whatever is left comes back to your balance. The full mechanic step by step: [how pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

## The parameters the buyer sets

You configure the terms of the offer yourself when creating the contest. Clippers see every parameter on the card before they submit.

| Parameter | Who sets it and typical values | What it is for |
|-----------|--------------------------------|----------------|
| Rate per 1000 views | set by the buyer (across live contests $0.30 - $1.00 per 1000 views) | Defines what a clipper gets for every thousand counted views |
| View threshold | set by the buyer (median across live contests 2000, system default 1000) | Submissions below the threshold do not take part in the split, so the budget is not spread too thin |
| Cap per submission | set by the buyer | Limits the maximum payout to one creator so a single video cannot eat the whole budget |
| Total budget | set by the buyer | The full amount locked on the platform |
| Brief and platforms | set by the buyer | The description, the hashtags, the platforms to post on |

The rate of **$0.30 to $1.00 per 1000 views** reflects the range across live contests, and the threshold of **2000 views** is the typical (median) value in live contests, not a platform limit: the system default and floor is 1000 views, and above that the buyer sets whatever threshold they like (platform data, snapshot: 2026-07-10). More on the threshold: [the view threshold for a payout](/en/earnings/view-threshold).

## What to weigh up: a checklist for the buyer

1. **The rate.** A high rate attracts more clippers but burns the budget faster. A low rate saves budget but may not interest creators. The live range: $0.30 - $1.00 per 1000 views.
2. **The threshold.** It protects the budget from submissions with a handful of views. The typical threshold in live contests: 2000.
3. **The cap.** It stops one video that went viral from taking the whole budget. Pick it so the budget spreads across several creators.
4. **The brief.** A clear description (format, style, key moments) raises the quality of the work. Clippers do better when they know what is expected.
5. **The platform.** Today pay-per-view contests run on TikTok: the oracle pulls views through its API.
6. **The budget.** Work out the total from the expected number of participants and the rate. Anything unspent comes back.
7. **The payment method.** It determines the commission (see below).

## Commission by payment method

The commission depends on the payment method: fiat (card, bank transfer) 5%, wallet (USDT on TON) 8%, COINS 10%. It is calculated on the prize amount and paid by the buyer on top of the budget, once, when the contest is created: the prize pool goes to the clippers in full and the clipper pays nothing. Exact rates and worked examples: [what commission DareBay takes](/en/help/what-commission).

## Budget protection: guarantor, oracle, automatic split

DareBay works as an intermediary and guarantor between the buyer and the clippers:

- **The budget is locked up front.** The money is locked when the contest is created. The buyer cannot take it back before the end, and the clipper can see that the money is on the platform.
- **The oracle against view fraud.** Views are pulled automatically through platform APIs and validated. Suspicious spikes (bots, bought views) are reviewed and can be rejected.
- **The automatic split by counted results.** The payout happens automatically by formula, without the buyer touching it. Neither side can adjust the result.

So that neither side gets burned: the buyer will not pay for bought views, and the clipper gets their money without depending on the buyer's goodwill.

DareBay works for users from Russia and the CIS: registration is open, and payment goes through a wallet (USDT on TON), a card or a bank transfer. The interface is in Russian. That makes the platform a practical option for buyers who need Russian-speaking clippers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I protect myself from bought views?

The platform pulls views through the platform API (TikTok today) and validates them automatically. Neither the buyer nor the clipper types a number in by hand. Suspicious spikes are reviewed and can be rejected. You pay only for counted views.

### What is the commission?

It depends on the payment method: fiat 5%, wallet 8%, COINS 10%. It is calculated on the prize amount and paid by the buyer on top of the budget when the contest is created, so the prize pool goes to the clippers in full.

### How do I get the unspent budget back?

Automatically. If the budget is not spent in full (few participants, submissions that did not get views), the remainder returns to the buyer balance once the contest ends. There is nothing to do by hand.

### Can DareBay be used from Russia?

Yes. Registration is open and the platform works for users from Russia and the CIS. Payment goes through a wallet, a card or a bank transfer. The interface is in Russian.

### How many clippers will take the brief?

It depends on the rate, the platform and the description. The more attractive the terms and the clearer the brief, the more creators will submit. The buyer is not limited in the number of participants.

### Can a contest be cancelled after it is created?

No, and that is the point of the guarantee: once created, the budget is locked until the contest ends, and you cannot change your mind and take the money back. Cancellation is possible only right after creation, while there is not a single submission. The unspent remainder comes back automatically after the end in any case.

### Do I have to review the clippers' work by hand?

In a PPV contest, moderation is part of the process: the buyer checks that submissions match the brief during the selection window. The views themselves are counted by the platform automatically.
