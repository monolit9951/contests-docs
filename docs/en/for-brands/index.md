# For brands: commission clips and manage payment

On DareBay a buyer publishes a brief and creators make and post videos to its terms. For pay-per-view work you set the rate, eligible-view threshold and any per-submission cap. Creating and funding a contest is free; the contest payout fee is 0%. Participation, publication speed and reach depend on the brief and material, so opening a contest does not guarantee dozens of videos.

## Write a brief a creator can check

Name the audience and product, attach footage you can authorize, and list required elements and rejection reasons. Decide whether you want reach, production or confirmed sales; those goals need different payment terms. Use the [brief template](/en/for-brands/clipping-brief-template) before recruiting.

## Keep funding and performance separate

A wallet-backed contest locks its budget against the contest on DareBay and returns unspent funds to your balance. In manual mode the organizer pays creators directly and DareBay does not hold that prize budget. Agree the mode before launch so creators know who sends payment.

The platform collects published view counts and applies the task's terms to eligible submissions that pass checks. A creator's screenshot does not replace collected statistics; a public view count does not establish sales either. Assess accepted clips, eligible views, spend and your target action separately. [Setting up a contest](/en/for-brands/set-up-a-clipping-contest) covers launch and permitted changes.

The [business page](/en/for-business) describes collaboration options, while [fee help](/en/help/what-commission) lists money operations. Use the pilot's actual rejection reasons and results to improve the next brief before increasing the budget.
