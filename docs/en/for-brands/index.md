# For brands

You fund the budget up front, set a rate per thousand views and a cap per submission.
Clippers film, publish and send in their links, and the platform pays each of them for the
views they actually got, counted independently of you and of them. If the views do not come,
the rest of the budget comes back to you in full.

Everything the paying side needs is here: how pay-per-view works from the buyer's seat, how
to set up a contest for clippers, and how to commission clips for a campaign. You will not
pay for inflated views: the count comes from the platform the video was published on, not
from the creator who submitted it.

Ready to run one - the [business page](/en/for-business) covers pricing and terms, and the
contest itself is set up on [darebay.com](https://darebay.com).
