# How to pay clippers per view

Pay-per-view on DareBay works like this: you fund the budget and set the rate per 1000 views, the threshold and the cap. Clippers film, publish and send in their links; the platform independently counts the views and records what each creator has earned. DareBay holds the funded budget as intermediary and guarantor, so the calculation does not depend on a number supplied by either side. Whatever is left of the budget comes back to you.

## How the payment model works

1. **You create the contest and lock the wallet-backed budget.** Open [contest creation on DareBay](https://darebay.com), choose pay-per-view, write the task for the clippers and pay in the wallet-backed budget. The amount is locked and tied to that contest: you cannot take it back before it ends. That is the guarantee for the clippers - the wallet-backed money is already on the platform, and the organizer cannot change their mind.
2. **Clippers publish their work and send the links.** Creators see your contest in the public feed, take the task, film the content and post the videos with the required hashtag. Each of them then sends DareBay the link to their video, and only then does the work enter the contest.
3. **Neither you nor the creator counts the views.** The number comes from the platform the video was published on (TikTok today). Inflated views do not get through: bot views are not counted, and a submission with bought views loses its payout entirely. For more on the count, see [the dedicated page](/en/earnings/how-views-are-counted).
4. **Amounts are calculated by formula.** The platform records the obligations to creators and returns the unused remainder to the organizer balance. Payout fulfilment is handled manually through payout details and statuses; it is not an automatic transfer immediately after counting.

For the step-by-step setup (how to pick the rate, the threshold and the cap for your case), see [how to set up a clipping contest](/en/for-brands/set-up-a-clipping-contest).

## Rate, threshold, cap and the formula

Three parameters define the payment model:

- **The rate** - what you pay per 1000 views. You choose the amount when creating the contest.
- **The threshold** - the minimum views for a submission to take part in the split. Anything below it costs you nothing.
- **The cap** - the maximum per submission. One viral video will not take the whole budget.

When a contest ends, the system calculates the payout for each submission:

> submission views / 1000 * rate

Submissions below the threshold do not take part. The cap limits the maximum per submission.

## Cost and fees

Creating a contest and topping up its budget currently carry no fee. The organizer funds the prize budget. Entry is free for creators, but a later withdrawal of an available balance is a separate operation with a fee and minimum request.

| Parameter | Value | What it means |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Contest creation | no fee | The organizer funds the budget |
| Budget top-up | no fee | No additional top-up charge |
| Creator withdrawal | separate terms | Fee, minimum and manual processing are stated on the withdrawal page |
| Refund of the remainder | the whole remainder | Whatever is left of the budget goes back to the organizer balance |

See [current DareBay fees](/en/help/what-commission) for the operational terms.

## Why this works for the buyer

**The budget is protected.** The money sits on the platform and only leaves for views that actually happened. No views, no spend: it comes back to you.

**No manual calculation for every creator.** One contest replaces ten spreadsheets: clippers take the task themselves, and the platform counts the result and records the payout obligations.

**An independent count.** The views come from the platform the video is published on. Neither you nor the clippers can nudge that number, so you pay for exactly what the audience watched.

**The calculation runs without you.** Every creator's share follows the formula. The transfer itself is not instant: payout details and statuses are processed manually.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does it cost to create a contest?

Creating a contest and topping up its budget currently carry no fee. The organizer funds the prize budget itself. Other operations are covered under [current DareBay fees](/en/help/what-commission).

### What if the clippers do not get the views?

Then you pay nothing. If no submission clears the view threshold, or nobody entered at all, the whole budget goes back to your balance. Locked wallet-backed money is never burned and never stays with the platform.

### How does the remainder come back?

It lands on the organizer balance once the contest ends and the payouts are distributed, with no claim to file. Budget $200, paid out $70 - the remaining $130 comes back to you.

### Can the rate be changed after the contest is created?

The rate and the threshold are fixed when the contest is created and do not change after that. It is part of the guarantee: the clippers see the terms in advance, and nobody rewrites them afterwards. The cap per submission is the one parameter you can adjust after launch.

### How do clippers get the money?

After result checks, the platform records the amount owed to each creator. Fulfilment then runs manually through the available method and payout details; it is not an automatic transfer at contest close. Balance withdrawals have [separate terms](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals).

### Do I have to check the views myself?

No. The views come from the platform the video is published on and land in the calculation without you. Neither you nor the creator has to recount, confirm or argue about the number.
