# DareBay supported countries and payout routes

DareBay keeps no list of supported countries and no list of excluded ones. Creators join from anywhere under the same [Terms](/en/legal/terms), take the same tasks and are paid on the same rail: USDT on the TON network, or Telegram Stars. What differs from one country to the next is the last step, turning a payout into local money, and the local rules around it. This page states the DareBay side once and points to the guide written for your country.

## Who can take a task

The Terms set the conditions, and none of them is a country:

- an account can be opened from the age of **13**;
- receiving prizes or withdrawing money requires **the age of majority in your country**;
- a person named on the UK, EU or UN restricted lists cannot use the platform.

Nothing else stands between you and a task: no follower count, no portfolio and no application. The rate per 1,000 counted views, the view threshold and the cap per clip are printed on the task card before you take it.

Your side of the rule is local law. Taking part and receiving a payout have to be permitted where you live, and declaring the income is yours to do. DareBay being available to you and a crypto payout being allowed in your country are two separate questions; the country guides below quote each regulator on the second one.

## How a payout leaves DareBay, wherever you are

1. **Earnings reach your balance.** When a wallet-backed task ends, the platform counts the views and credits each clip's earnings to your balance.
2. **You file one request.** A withdrawal request starts at **10 USDT**. The fee is 10% (default) of the requested amount, taken inside the request, and the form shows the amount to receive before you confirm.
3. **The team checks it and sends it.** The balance leaves as **USDT to your own wallet on the TON network**, or you receive the payout in **Telegram Stars**, which stay inside Telegram.

The steps are the same in Lagos, Karachi, Jakarta and Warsaw; the full walkthrough is on the [withdrawals page](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals), and the fee arithmetic is on the [commission page](/en/help/what-commission). A task can also name a card, a bank transfer or a Telegram gift as its reward method; the organizer then delivers that reward under the task's own terms, separately from a balance withdrawal.

## From USDT to local money

Converting USDT into rupees, naira, pesos or tenge happens outside DareBay, at an exchange or payment service you choose. Which services operate legally, what they ask from you and how the income is taxed depend on your country, so each guide below is built on what the local regulator and the local payment services publish themselves, with the date each page was read. The guides compare every platform that pays in that country, so they also cover rails DareBay does not use, such as PayPal, Payoneer, UPI or mobile money.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is DareBay available in a country that is not in the table?

Yes. The table lists the countries we have written a local guide for; it is not a list of supported countries, because DareBay keeps none. The conditions are the ones in the Terms: age, and not being on the UK, EU or UN restricted lists.

### Does the withdrawal fee or the minimum change with my country?

No. A request starts at 10 USDT and carries the 10% (default) fee everywhere. An account can have another applicable rate, so the form always shows the exact fee and the amount to receive before you confirm.

### Can a DareBay payout arrive on a card, in a bank account or on mobile money?

A balance withdrawal is sent as USDT on TON or received in Telegram Stars. A particular task can name a card or a bank transfer as its reward method; the organizer then pays that reward directly, under the task's own terms. Moving USDT into a bank account or a mobile wallet is the step you do with a local service.

### Which languages does DareBay speak?

The interface is in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish and Spanish, and the guides are also published in Arabic. A task is written in the language its organizer chose.

### Who decides whether I may receive a crypto payout where I live?

Your country's law does, and checking it is your responsibility. DareBay does not sort accounts by country, but it also cannot make a transfer lawful where the local rules say otherwise. Read the regulator quotes in your country guide before your first withdrawal.
