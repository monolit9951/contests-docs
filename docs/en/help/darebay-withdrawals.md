# DareBay withdrawals - minimum, timing and what arrives

Your DareBay balance leaves in one move: request a withdrawal from **10 USDT** and the platform does the rest. The fee is **10% of the requested amount**, deducted inside the request, and the form shows the amount to receive before you confirm. Every request is checked before it goes out, so the money reaches the right wallet.

Entering a contest is free. The current fee to create or top up a contest is 0%; a withdrawal is a separate operation after a reward has been credited, and its 10% fee is the only charge on what you earn.

## How to request a withdrawal

1. **Collect your earnings.** Your earnings reach your balance automatically when a wallet-backed task ends, or earlier with Claim now where the task shows it; a claimed clip then stops earning.
2. **Open the withdrawal form.** Enter an amount of at least 10 USDT and choose an available method.
3. **Review the calculation.** Before confirmation, the form shows the requested amount, the 10% fee and the amount to receive.
4. **Submit the request.** The platform does the rest: it checks the details and sends the transfer, and you follow the status in DareBay.

For example, a 10 USDT request has a 1 USDT fee and a 9 USDT amount to receive, and a 100 USDT request pays out 90 USDT. The request records the rate that applied when it was created.

## Available methods

The withdrawal wizard sends your balance as USDT to an external wallet on TON, or you can receive the payout in Telegram Stars, which stay inside Telegram. A contest can also name a card, a bank transfer, a Telegram gift or a physical item as its reward method; that reward is delivered under the contest's own terms, separately from a balance withdrawal.

Always rely on the methods shown in your request form. Never send money to an external address to “activate”, “verify” or “speed up” a payout.

## How long it takes

The first part runs by itself: earnings reach your balance automatically once a wallet-backed task ends, or sooner with Claim now where the task shows it. After that a withdrawal is one request from 10 USDT: the platform checks the details and sends the transfer, and the request status shows each step.

If you have a question about a request, check the payout details you entered and write to support@darebay.com with the request identifier. Do not create duplicate requests or transfer the fee separately.

## A legitimate withdrawal versus a scam request

A legitimate DareBay withdrawal:

- is filed inside the form, which shows the amount to receive before confirmation;
- carries a fee of 10% of the requested amount, deducted from that amount, so nobody ever asks you to pay anything on top;
- never requires a separate transfer to a member of staff, a bot or a “guarantor”;
- is recorded on the request together with the amount to receive.

A message asking you to send money to an unknown wallet first is not the DareBay withdrawal process.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a minimum withdrawal?

Yes. The minimum available-balance withdrawal request is 10 USDT.

### What is the withdrawal fee?

It is 10% of the requested amount, deducted inside the request: a 100 USDT request pays out 90 USDT. The exact calculation and amount to receive are shown before confirmation.

### What happens after I submit a request?

Nothing more is needed from you: the platform checks the details and sends the transfer to the method you chose, and the request status in DareBay shows where it is.

### Why is the reward credited but the amount received is lower?

Contest credit and withdrawal are different operations. The reward is recorded on the available balance in full; a 10% fee is deducted from the amount requested for withdrawal.

### Do I pay any fee separately?

No. The fee is deducted inside the request, and nothing is ever paid in advance. Never transfer money to anyone to unlock a payout.
