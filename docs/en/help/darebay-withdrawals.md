# DareBay withdrawals - minimum, timing and what arrives

Your DareBay balance leaves in one move: you file a withdrawal request, and the team checks the details manually and sends the transfer. The minimum request is **10 USDT**, and there is **no withdrawal fee**: the amount you request is the amount you receive. Because a person checks every request, there is no fixed promise of settlement “within minutes”.

Entering a contest is free. The current fee to create or top up a contest is 0%; a withdrawal is a separate operation after a reward has been credited, and it carries no fee either.

## How to request a withdrawal

1. **Wait for an available balance.** While a contest or its result is still being finalized, funds may remain frozen.
2. **Open the withdrawal form.** Enter an amount of at least 10 USDT and choose an available method.
3. **Review the calculation.** Before confirmation, the form shows the requested amount and the amount to receive, which are the same.
4. **Submit the request.** The team checks the details manually and sends the transfer. Follow its status in DareBay.

For example, a 10 USDT request is a 10 USDT amount to receive. The request records the terms that applied when it was created.

## Available methods

The balance-withdrawal wizard supports Telegram Stars and USDT to an external wallet on TON. A card, bank transfer, Telegram gift or physical item can instead be the reward method defined by a particular contest and may be fulfilled separately; do not treat those as automatic balance-withdrawal rails.

Always rely on the methods shown in your request form. Never send money to an external address to “activate”, “verify” or “speed up” a payout.

## How long it takes

The contest must first finish and pass the relevant result checks. After the reward reaches the available balance, the user submits a separate withdrawal request. The team handles that request, so timing depends on detail checks and the chosen method; instant or automatic settlement is not guaranteed.

If the status does not change for an unusual amount of time, check the payout details and contact support@darebay.com with the request identifier. Do not create duplicate requests or transfer anything separately.

## A legitimate withdrawal versus a scam request

A legitimate DareBay withdrawal:

- is filed inside the form, which shows the amount to receive before confirmation;
- carries no fee, so nobody ever asks you to pay anything;
- never requires a separate transfer to a member of staff, a bot or a “guarantor”;
- is recorded on the request together with the amount to receive.

A message asking you to send money to an unknown wallet first is not the DareBay withdrawal process.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a minimum withdrawal?

Yes. The minimum available-balance withdrawal request is 10 USDT.

### What is the withdrawal fee?

There is none: the amount you request is the amount you receive, and the form shows both before confirmation.

### Is withdrawal automatic?

No. Once submitted, the request is handled by the team; nothing more is needed from you. DareBay does not promise settlement within minutes.

### Why is the reward credited but the amount received is lower?

Contest credit and withdrawal are different operations. The reward is recorded on the available balance; no fee is deducted from the amount requested for withdrawal.

### Do I pay any fee separately?

No. There is no withdrawal fee, and nothing is ever paid in advance. Never transfer money to anyone to unlock a payout.

Withdrawing an available balance requires a request of at least 10 USDT.
