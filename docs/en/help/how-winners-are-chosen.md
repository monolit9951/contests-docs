# How are winners chosen?

Every DareBay contest uses **one of three** selection types. The organizer
picks it when creating the contest and cannot change it mid-flight.

## RANDOM (among the most-liked)

When the entry window closes, the system takes the top N most-liked
submissions and draws the winners at random from that pool.

- **Good for:** giveaways, contests with no expert judging, hype
  campaigns.
- **Feels like:** a lottery with a popularity filter.
- **What the participant does:** submits work that people will like; after
  that it is luck.

## CREATOR_DECISION

After the deadline the organizer reviews the submissions and names the
winner themselves, by the **selection deadline** they fixed at creation.

- **Good for:** subjective quality (design, copy, craft), curated
  contests.
- **Feels like:** classic judging.
- **What the participant does:** follows the brief exactly and shows real
  quality.
- **On trust:** contests decided by the organizer lean heavily on their
  **rating**. Low-rated organizers running subjective selection get fewer
  entries, and rightly so.

## VIEWER_VOTING

Submissions move into a voting phase where viewers can vote (one vote per
account per contest). The top of the vote count wins.

- **Good for:** community contests, brand activations, anything where "who
  the crowd picked" is the whole point.
- **Feels like:** a talent show final.
- **What the participant does:** submits, then gathers support honestly
  (sharing is fine, bots are not).

## Protection against vote inflation

For all three types:

- Likes and votes from sockpuppets are caught and do not count. Repeat
  attempts mean an account ban.
- Organizers do not vote in their own contests.
- Participants do not vote for their own submission.
- Suspicious patterns (thousands of votes from one IP, coordinated timing,
  freshly created accounts voting in unison) trigger a review, and a
  recount if confirmed.

## What if the winner never responds

If a named winner does not claim the prize in time, it either passes down
to the next in line (voting and top-liked contests) or the organizer picks
again (creator-decision). The claim deadline is always stated in the
notification the winner receives.
