# DareBay help


## Find the stage you need help with

| Situation | Read | Have ready |
|---|---|---|
| First participation | [Quick start](/en/help/quick-start) | Your role and the chosen brief |
| A submission link fails | [Submit your work](/en/help/submit-your-work) | A public link that opens without signing in |
| A reward exists but no transfer arrived | [Prizes and payouts](/en/help/prizes-and-payouts), then [withdrawals](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals) | Earnings status, available balance and request ID |
| The amount looks wrong | [Commission](/en/help/what-commission) | The operation, gross amount and net amount |

A wallet-backed contest holds its budget on DareBay. Manual mode means direct payment by the organizer; it does not hold that prize budget on the platform. A verified profile, a rank and confirmed funding also answer different questions. When contacting support, include the contest, public work link and specific status. Do not send login codes, passwords or wallet secrets.

Everything worth knowing before your first contest lives on these pages: how a reward is set and
held, how a winner is chosen, what a withdrawal costs and what protects your result. Three of them
are worth having up front. In a wallet-backed contest the budget is locked on the platform at
creation, earlier than anyone starts working. The amount is set by a formula and reaches your
balance automatically when that contest ends. The commission on contest payouts is 0%. The
default withdrawal fee is 10%; a personal account rate may differ. The form accepts requests
from 10 USDT and shows the net amount before confirmation. Follow the request status through
processing and receipt. If your question is about trust rather than mechanics, that is the next
section: [about the project](/en/about/).
