# Can you pay with crypto on DareBay?

Yes. The DareBay wallet supports USDT on the TON network. You can use it to:

- **Top up** your wallet balance.
- **Pay the prize pool and the commission** when creating a contest.
- **Receive winnings** into your wallet.

Crypto is not mandatory, though: it is one option among several.

## What we support

Right now the wallet works with USDT on the TON network. The current list of methods is always shown on the top-up and withdrawal screens in the app.

## Is crypto required?

No. A reward can be paid out without crypto: to a bank card, by bank transfer, and also in Telegram Stars or Telegram gifts. Fiat works on equal terms with the wallet, and no hidden conversion happens in the background.
