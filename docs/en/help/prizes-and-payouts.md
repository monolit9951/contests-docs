# Prizes and payouts

DareBay is simple. You launch a task, people make their content, and the
winners get their reward for it. Fairly and reliably. The platform holds the
reward from the very start and releases it automatically once the result is
confirmed, so nobody gets cheated. That is how it works, and it cannot be
switched off.

## Where it works

DareBay opens in two places, and both are equal:

- **On the web** at [darebay.com](https://darebay.com), right in the browser.
- **In Telegram**, through the app, the bot and the group.

Same DareBay, two equal doors.

## What you can put up as a reward

A reward is one of two things: **money** or **an item**. And there are several
ways to pay, all of them available right now:

- **Money** as ordinary money (card, bank transfer, local methods) or through
  a wallet. Held and settled on your DareBay balance.
- **An item or a gift**, described in text ("a signed vinyl", "a weekend in
  Krakow"). The handover is arranged between the organizer and the winner.
  DareBay does not hold the item itself, but it does make sure the promise
  is kept.
- **Telegram Stars** as a reward inside Telegram.

You can mix methods across places. For example: $500 for 1st place, a signed
book for 2nd.

## Balance and topping up

Every DareBay account has a balance. You can top it up:

- With ordinary money (card, bank transfer, local methods). Works right now.
- Through a wallet (supported networks and stablecoins).

The balance is shown in whichever currency you picked for display, but it is
held in what you put in. Conversion only happens when it is genuinely needed,
with no hidden operations.

## Freezing the prize pool

When you publish a contest, the reward leaves your available balance and moves
into a locked state tied to that contest. Locked money cannot be spent on
anything else, which is how participants can see the reward is really there.
Cancel the contest before it starts and the funds are unlocked and returned to
available (minus the commission already taken, see
[commission](/en/help/what-commission)).

## Commission

Commission comes **out of the prize pool**, not on top of it. You see the exact
amount in your currency before you confirm. It depends on your verification
level: verified accounts pay less.

## Paying the winners

Once the contest is over and the winner is confirmed:

- **Money rewards** move from the contest freeze onto the winner's balance.
  Internal settlement is near instant; withdrawal to an outside account
  follows the usual withdrawal rules.
- **Items, gifts and Stars** trigger a notification to both sides, and the
  organizer is obliged to hand the reward over within the agreed time.

## How payouts work under the hood

DareBay acts as intermediary and guarantor. The platform holds the reward from
the moment the contest starts and releases it itself against an independently
counted result, so neither side can cheat the other. Money can be paid in
several ways, and crypto (the USDT stablecoin on the TON network via a smart
contract) is only one of them, alongside ordinary money, items, gifts and
Telegram Stars.
