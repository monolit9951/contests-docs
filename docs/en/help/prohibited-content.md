# What to do about prohibited or dangerous content?

DareBay is not a stage for illegal, violent or hateful content.
We take reports seriously and process them fast.

## What counts as zero tolerance

The zero-tolerance categories:

- **Illegal content** under the jurisdiction of the person reporting it
  and/or DareBay's own jurisdictions: CSAM, intimate material shared
  without consent, incitement to specific violence, blatant fraud and so
  on.
- **Threats of violence** against real people or groups.
- **Hate speech** against protected categories.
- **Harassment** of specific participants, organizers or viewers.
- **Doxxing**: publishing private personal data without consent.

## How to report

- On any submission or comment: the **⋯** menu has **Report**.
- On an account: the **Report** link on the profile.
- For urgent or illegal content that has to come down immediately, write to
  **trust@darebay.com** with a link and a short description. Trusted
  reporters get a priority queue.

## What happens next

- **Automated triage** runs straight away.
- **Human moderation** reviews everything that clears triage. Target times
  are hours, not days; for zero tolerance, faster still.
- **Confirmed violation** → the content is removed and the account banned
  (no "three strikes" for zero tolerance). Money in the linked contests
  goes back to the organizer, or is withheld if the organizer was the one
  in violation.
- **Not confirmed** → the reporter gets a short explanation. Anyone who
  files reports in bad faith on a regular basis ends up with restrictions
  on *their own* account.

## Legally

DareBay cooperates with law enforcement under the process in force in every
jurisdiction where it operates. We publish a transparency report with
aggregate statistics on requests received and how we responded.
