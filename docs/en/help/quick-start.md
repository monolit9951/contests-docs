# Quick start

DareBay runs three roles at once. You can be an **organizer** (you set the
activity and the reward), a **participant** (you make your own version of the
content and go for the win) or a **viewer** (you watch, vote, comment, and
sometimes that is exactly what decides it). Most accounts end up doing all three.

DareBay has two equal front doors and one account behind them: the site
[**darebay.com**](https://darebay.com) and Telegram (the app, the bot, the group).
Come in either way and you land in the same place; your activities, your work
and your wallet stay with you in both.

## If you want to launch a contest

Put up the prize, write the terms, publish. Entries come in, a winner is
picked, the money goes out. The fastest way to get the content you need
made to order.

→ [**Create your first contest**](/en/help/your-first-contest)

## If you want to take part

You browse active contests, read the terms, send in your work (text, image
or video) and wait for the selection stage. If you win, the prize is already
frozen and it lands on your balance.

→ [**Submit your work**](/en/help/submit-your-work)

## If you just want to watch

Scroll the feed, like, comment and, in voting contests, vote. Viewers shape
the outcome without submitting a single entry.

→ [**Watch, vote, win**](/en/help/watch-vote-win)

## Read this before you start

- **[Prizes and payouts](/en/help/prizes-and-payouts)** - what you can put up as
  a reward (money, an item, Telegram Stars or a gift) and how it reaches the
  winners. Money arrives by card or bank transfer, to a wallet or as Stars,
  whichever suits you.
- **[Verification and trust](/en/help/verification)** - what the badge on an
  account means and why it matters to everyone dealing with it.
