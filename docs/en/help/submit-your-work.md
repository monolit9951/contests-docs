# Submit your work

The one thing to get right: **a submission enters a contest through the link you send.** In a
pay-per-view contest, publishing the video is only half of it - until the URL reaches DareBay,
your work is not in the contest and not in the budget split. Everything else on this page is
detail around that.

## 1. Find a contest that fits

Scroll the feed or filter by category. Open the contest card. **Read the terms
carefully**: most disqualifications happen because somebody skimmed and missed a
requirement.

Check:

- Is it still **ACTIVE**? ([How winners are chosen](/en/help/how-winners-are-chosen).) (Closed contests do not accept entries.)
- Which format does it want? (Text, image or video, always just one.)
- The hard limits: language, originality, length.
- In a pay-per-view contest: the rate per 1000 views, the view threshold and the
  cap per submission. All three sit on the card and none of them move after launch,
  so you know the price of your reach before you open the editor.
- Are source materials attached? A buyer can attach them, and plenty do not. If
  the task has none, finding the source is your job.

## 2. Make the work

Actually make it. [Stolen content, AI slop passed off as your own](/en/help/prohibited-content), other
shortcuts: all of it gets removed and the accounts get flagged. Your
**participant rating** follows you across the whole platform, and it is worth
more than any single prize.

## 3. Send it in

From here the contest type decides everything. There are two routes, and mixing
them up is the most common way to end up unpaid.

### Pay-per-view contests (PPV)

The video lives on a public site rather than with us. Statistics are collected today for
**TikTok, YouTube and Instagram**, and the contest terms say which one it wants. For Instagram it
has to be a video with a view count: a photo post produces no such metric, so there is nothing to
read. New to the mechanic? [How pay-per-view works](/en/earnings/how-pay-per-view-works).

The order is:

1. Post the video on the site named in the task.
2. Come back to the contest card on DareBay and **send the link to the published video**.

There is no hashtag import. The platform does not crawl social networks looking for entries, so
posting and waiting gets you nothing. A hashtag is required where the task explicitly asks for one,
and it is mandatory if you are submitting anonymously - in that case the tag in the description is
what shows the video was made for this task.

After that the platform's independent counter reads the published view counter on the site you posted
on. Neither you nor the buyer types that figure in by hand. Moderation can still reject a
submission that breaks the task terms.

### Contests where you upload the work

Here the work is stored with us. On the contest page hit **Submit**, then follow
the format:

- **Text**: type it or paste it.
- **Image**: upload (jpg, png, webp).
- **Video**: upload; the video goes into DareBay storage and is encoded for
  streaming. Stay within the size limit stated in the form.

You can add a short note explaining the work: it helps in contests where the
organizer picks the winner.

## 4. Wait for the selection stage

After the entry deadline the contest moves into **SELECTION_IN_PROGRESS**. What
happens next is decided by the selection type:

- **Community voting**: viewers vote, and you can share your work to gather
  support.
- **Organizer decides**: the organizer looks through the entries and picks.
- **Seeded draw**: the system shuffles every eligible active submission. Likes
  change nothing here.
- **Pay per view**: no picking at all. The formula splits the budget across every
  submission that cleared the view threshold.

## 5. If you win

You get a notification, and the platform records what you are owed with a status you can follow.
In a wallet-backed contest that money left the organizer's balance back when the contest was
created, so nothing depends on their goodwill at this point. Fulfilment itself can be processed by
hand and carries no promised date. Entry is free, while [withdrawal](/en/help/darebay-withdrawals)
is a separate request with a minimum, a fee and manual processing; the form shows the method
available to you. For an **ITEM** prize the organizer gets in touch to arrange the handover.

## 6. If you do not win

That is fine. Enter the next one. Your participant rating grows simply from
sending in real work and not breaking the rules: reputation is not built by
winners alone.
