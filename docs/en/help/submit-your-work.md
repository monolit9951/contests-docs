# Submit your work

Submitting work on DareBay is faster than reading the instructions for it.

## 1. Find a contest that fits

Scroll the feed or filter by category. Open the contest card. **Read the terms
carefully**: most disqualifications happen because somebody skimmed and missed
a requirement.

Check:

- Is it still **ACTIVE**? (Closed contests do not accept entries.)
- Which format does it want? (Text / image / video, usually only one.)
- The hard limits: language, originality, length.

## 2. Make the work

Actually make it. Stolen content, AI slop passed off as your own, other
shortcuts: all of it gets removed and the accounts get flagged. Your
**participant rating** follows you across the whole platform, and it is worth
more than any single prize.

## 3. Upload it

On the contest page hit **Submit**. Depending on the format:

- **Text** - type it or paste it.
- **Image** - upload (jpg, png, webp).
- **Video** - upload; the video goes into DareBay storage and is encoded for
  streaming. Stay within the size limit stated in the form.

You can add a short note explaining the work, which helps in contests where
the organizer picks the winner.

## 4. Wait for the selection stage

After the entry deadline the contest moves into **SELECTION_IN_PROGRESS**.
What happens next depends on the selection type:

- **Community voting** - viewers vote; you can share your work to gather
  support.
- **Organizer decides** - the organizer looks through the entries and picks.
- **Random among the top** - the system picks.

## 5. If you win

You get a notification. For **MONEY** prizes the funds are already frozen in
the contest; they move onto your balance as soon as the organizer confirms
(or automatically, depending on the type). For **ITEM** prizes the organizer
gets in touch to arrange the handover.

## 6. If you do not win

That is fine. Enter the next one. Your participant rating grows simply from
sending in real work and not breaking the rules: reputation is not built by
winners alone.
