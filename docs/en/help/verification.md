# Verification and trust

Half of what a participant weighs up is the prize itself. The other half: *is
this organizer real, and will they fulfil the obligation?* The platform records
the budget, result and credit or payout status. Fulfilment itself can be manual.
Verification and open ratings answer who exactly is behind the contest.

## Verification levels

There are three types of verified account:

- **BLOGGER**: individual creators with a public track record. Good for personal
  brand contests.
- **STORE**: e-commerce businesses running promotions.
- **COMPANY**: registered companies running marketing or hiring campaigns.

Verified accounts pass a document check (ID or incorporation papers). The badge
then shows on the profile and on every contest that account runs, so a
participant can see who they are dealing with straight from the feed. The badge
does not by itself change a fee: current terms depend on the operation and are
listed [here](/en/help/what-commission).

A contest without a badge has its budget and obligation recorded in the same way.
The badge answers a different question - who wrote the task and what their track
record looks like - and does not promise an automatic payout.

## Dual ratings

Every DareBay account carries **two ratings**:

- **Organizer rating** is built from the contests you have run. Did you pay the
  prizes on time? Was the winner picked fairly? How many disputes?
- **Participant rating** is built from the contests you have entered. Is your
  work original? Do you follow the rules? Do you pile on other participants or
  organizers?

Both ratings are visible on your profile. They cannot be bought, and they are not
erased by creating a new account: an account with no history looks suspicious all
by itself.

## What a low rating means in practice

- Low organizer rating → participants skip your contests, and the feed shows them
  less often.
- Low participant rating → your submissions go through manual moderation before
  they appear in the public feed.
- Very low → account limits or a ban.

## How to earn trust fast

If you are just starting out:

- **Organizers**: run one small contest you are certain you can close cleanly. A
  carefully run $50 contest is worth more than a $5,000 one that fell apart.
- **Participants**: enter contests in the niche you actually work in. Your first
  2-3 submissions, even the ones that do not win, establish a pattern of genuine
  authorship.

Verification can be done at any point, and you do not need it to start.
