# Verification and trust

Half of how a participant judges a contest is the prize itself. The other
half: *is this organizer real, and will they actually pay?* DareBay's
answer is verification plus open ratings.

## Verification levels

There are three types of verified account:

- **BLOGGER** - individual creators with a public track record. Good for
  personal brand contests.
- **STORE** - e-commerce businesses running promotions.
- **COMPANY** - registered companies running marketing or hiring campaigns.

Verified accounts pass a document check (ID / incorporation papers). A
badge then shows on the profile and on every contest they run. They
usually pay a lower commission, see [commission](/en/help/what-commission).

A contest without a badge can be perfectly legitimate, but participants
are entitled to look at it more closely.

## Dual ratings

Every DareBay account carries **two ratings**:

- **Organizer rating** is built from the contests you have run. Did you pay
  the prizes on time? Was the winner picked fairly? How many disputes?
- **Participant rating** is built from the contests you have entered. Is
  your work original? Do you follow the rules? Do you pile on other
  participants or organizers?

Both ratings are visible on your profile. They cannot be bought, and they
are not erased by creating a new account: an account with no history looks
suspicious all by itself.

## What a low rating means in practice

- Low organizer rating → participants skip your contests, and the feed
  shows them less often.
- Low participant rating → your submissions may need manual moderation
  before they appear in the public feed.
- Very low → account limits or a ban, depending on the reason.

## How to earn trust fast

If you are just starting out:

- **Organizers** - run one small contest you are certain you can close
  cleanly. A carefully run $50 contest is worth more than a $5,000 one
  that fell apart.
- **Participants** - enter contests in the niche you actually work in. Your
  first 2-3 submissions, even the ones that do not win, establish a
  pattern of genuine authorship.

Verification can be done at any point, and it is not required to start.
