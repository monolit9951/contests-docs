# What fees DareBay charges

Every DareBay charge is tied to an operation rather than to your account, and the whole list fits in one paragraph. Creating a contest and topping up its budget currently carry a **0% fee** for fiat, USDT and COINS. Taking an available balance out carries **no fee**, with a **10 USDT minimum** request, and the team checks the details manually before the money goes anywhere. The form shows the amount to receive before you confirm. A store purchase is a separate flow and carries an **8% fee**.

Entering a contest remains free. There is no charge to register, submit work or be included in the results.

## Current terms

| Operation | Fee | Important condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Create a contest | 0% | The organizer funds only the contest budget |
| Top up a contest budget | 0% | No additional top-up fee |
| Store purchase | 8% | Separate from contest fees |
| Withdraw an available balance | 0% | Minimum request: 10 USDT |

Before confirmation, the form shows the requested amount, the fee and the amount to receive. The rate is recorded on the request, so an existing request is not repriced later if the public terms change.

## How withdrawals are processed

A withdrawal is a separate step after a reward has been credited to the available balance. The user submits a request and the team handles the rest. DareBay therefore does not promise an automatic transfer or settlement “within minutes”. The product shows the request status; if it is delayed, contact support@darebay.com.

Never send a separate payment to “unlock” a prize. DareBay takes no withdrawal fee at all, and never asks for an advance transfer to somebody else's address.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to create a contest?

The current contest-creation fee is 0% across supported payment methods. The organizer funds the prize budget that is assigned to that contest.

### Is there a fee to top up the budget?

No. Contest budget top-ups also carry a 0% fee.

### Does DareBay charge participants?

Entering is free, and withdrawing an available balance is free too; the minimum request is 10 USDT.

### Does the winner receive the whole credited reward?

Contest results determine what is credited to the balance. A later withdrawal carries no fee, so the credited reward and the amount received are the same figure.

### Is the payout automatic?

No. Withdrawal requests are checked manually by the team before they are sent. Do not rely on promises of instant settlement; the status in DareBay is the source for a specific request.

### Where do these terms come from?

This page reflects the active product configuration on 15 August 2026. Operational terms are also shown before confirmation; if the public rates change, the form is authoritative for a new request.
