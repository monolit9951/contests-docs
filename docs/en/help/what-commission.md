# What commission DareBay charges

DareBay charges a commission only to the organizer, and only at contest creation. The rate depends on the payment method: fiat (card, bank transfer) is **5%**, the wallet (USDT on TON) is **8%**, and the internal COINS currency is **10%**. The commission is calculated as a percentage of the prize amount and charged right away at creation. The participant (the clipper) pays nothing: not to enter, not to withdraw, not for anything else. The platform deducts nothing from the winner's payout.

> **Launch promo (since July 2026):** the first organizers pay no commission at all, 0% instead of the base rates. The base rates below stay on record and come back once the promo ends.

## Commission rates by payment method

| Payment method | Rate | When it is charged |
|---|---|---|
| Fiat (card, bank transfer) | 5% of the prize amount | At contest creation |
| Wallet (USDT on TON) | 8% of the prize amount | At contest creation |
| COINS (internal currency) | 10% of the prize amount | At contest creation |

In every case the commission is a one-off charge taken before the contest is published. There are no further deductions at payout or withdrawal.

## How the commission works

DareBay's commission strategy is a percentage of the prize amount, charged to the organizer at contest creation. Here is how it plays out:

1. **The organizer creates a contest and sets a budget.** The platform calculates the commission as a percentage of the prize amount.
2. **The commission is charged immediately.** It comes off the organizer's balance together with the contest budget. Both amounts are then frozen on the platform.
3. **Nothing is deducted from payouts.** When the contest ends, the system distributes the prize by results. The winner receives their amount with no deductions; in pay-per-view contests payouts follow the formula for counted views, and whatever budget is left over goes back to the organizer.

The key point: the commission is not deducted from the clipper's payout. If an organizer creates a 100 USDT contest through the wallet, they pay 8 USDT of commission on top of the budget. The winner receives all 100 USDT.

## Why the rates differ

The rates are set by the platform and depend on the payment method:

- **Fiat (5%)** is the lowest rate.
- **Wallet (8%)** sits in the middle.
- **COINS (10%)** is the highest rate.

The exact percentage is visible at contest creation, before anything is charged. Picking a cheaper payment method is the simplest way to cut what you spend on commission. If fiat suits you, it works out cheaper.
