# Create your first contest

A contest is a prize plus terms plus a deadline. If all three are already in
your head, publishing takes under ten minutes.

## 1. Open the creation page

Hit **Create contest** in the DareBay header. The whole form sits on one
screen, no five-step wizards.

## 2. Write a title people will click

Short, concrete, to the point. "Film yourself making breakfast in a single
take" beats "best morning content". The title is the only thing most viewers
read before deciding whether to open the terms at all.

## 3. Write the terms

The description is a contract. What exactly has to be in the entry? What gets
it disqualified? Does it have to be in English? Does the work have to be
original? Be as precise as you can: ambiguity hurts you more than it hurts
the participants.

## 4. Pick a category and a subcategory

That is how people find you. Choose the narrowest category you still fit
into. Narrower = a more relevant audience.

## 5. Entry format

One of three:

- **Text** - written entries, up to the character limit you set.
- **Image** - one photo or a small gallery.
- **Video** - a short video, uploaded to DareBay storage.

One format per contest, no mixing.

## 6. How the winner is picked

One of three selection types:

- **RANDOM** - a random pick among the most liked entries at the end.
  Good for light giveaway campaigns.
- **CREATOR_DECISION** - you pick the winner yourself after the deadline.
  Good when quality is subjective and you trust your own taste.
- **VIEWER_VOTING** - the community votes and the top of the tally wins.
  Good if you want the contest to be about the community.

The selection type is locked in at creation: once published, it cannot be
changed.

## 7. The prize (or prizes)

Prizes are either **MONEY** (USD, EUR, PLN, UAH or a supported crypto) or
**ITEM** (described in text: "a signed vinyl record", "concert tickets").
You can set several prize places (1st, 2nd, 3rd) and even several winners
in one place.

Money prizes are frozen in your wallet the moment you publish. They cannot
be unfrozen while the contest is running.

## 8. Dates

- **Start** - when entries open. "Right now" is a perfectly normal choice.
- **Entry deadline** - when new entries stop being accepted.
- **Selection deadline** (CREATOR_DECISION only) - by when you commit to
  naming the winner.

## 9. Check the commission

DareBay takes a small commission from the prize pool, and you see the exact
amount in your currency before you confirm. Details are in the [section on
commission](/en/help/what-commission).

## 10. Publish

One click. The contest moves from **UPCOMING** to **ACTIVE**, the prize pool
is frozen, and participants can start submitting.
