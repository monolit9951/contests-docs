# Legal documents

Two documents that describe how DareBay is built, what we promise you
and what we expect from you. Read them before you put money in or send
in work.

- **[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy)** - what data we collect, why, how
  long we keep it and how to delete it.
- **[Terms of Use](/en/legal/terms)** - the platform rules: activities,
  prizes, the wallet, content, and what gets you removed from the service.

If something here stops matching how DareBay actually works, reality
wins. Write to us at **legal@darebay.com** and we will fix the document.
