# Legal documents

Two documents that set out how DareBay works: what we promise you and what
we expect from you. Read them before you put money in or send work in.

- **[Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy)** - what data we collect, why, how
  long we keep it and how to delete it.
- **[Terms of Use](/en/legal/terms)** - the platform rules: activities,
  prizes, the wallet, content, and what gets you removed from the service.

If anything here stops matching how DareBay actually works, reality wins.
Write to us at **legal@darebay.com** and we will fix the document.
