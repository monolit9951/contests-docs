An antidetect browser is a browser that runs each account in its own profile, with a separate fingerprint, cookies and proxy, so dozens of logins stay open side by side without mixing. A cloud phone does the same for mobile apps: it is an Android device hosted in the cloud with the major functions of a physical smartphone, minus the hardware <!-- source: https://www.geelark.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

This explainer is published by DareBay, a platform that pays clippers for counted views, so it also covers what these tools don't change about a clip. Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, checked on September 29, 2026 and rechecked on September 30; tools are listed alphabetically, and there are no affiliate links. Editors and AI tools are in the [tools catalog for Reels and Shorts](/en/tools/).

## How a browser profile keeps accounts separate

Every browser reports dozens of details to a site, from language and time zone to fonts and graphics card. Together they form a fingerprint that identifies the device even after cookies are cleared, and a regular browser shows the same one for every account. An antidetect browser gives each account its own set.

AdsPower defines a profile as a unique set of browser settings that mimics an individual fingerprint, such as the User Agent, IP address and Canvas fingerprint <!-- source: https://www.adspower.com/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, and syncs encrypted profile data across computers <!-- source: https://www.adspower.com/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. Team plans add roles: Dolphin{anty} adds Admin, Team Lead and User roles and permission-based profile sharing from its Base plan, with an audit log on every plan <!-- source: https://dolphin-anty.net/tarifs/ 2026-09-30 -->.

## Cloud phones: an Android device per account

A cloud phone replaces the browser profile with a virtual Android device. Multilogin describes its cloud phone profiles as full Android devices in the cloud, one per account, with 300+ apps such as TikTok, Instagram and WhatsApp preinstalled <!-- source: https://multilogin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->; AdsPower lists a Cloud Phone <!-- source: https://www.adspower.com/pricing 2026-09-29 --> and GoLogin a Cloud Android <!-- source: https://gologin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

Cloud phones are billed for time. GeeLark charges $0.007 a minute by default, capped at $1.20 per device per day on a paid plan, or rents a phone for $29.90 a month with unlimited use <!-- source: https://www.geelark.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->. Racks of real handsets are a different thing, usually called a phone farm, and [how a content farm differs from a phone farm](/en/content-farm/what-is-a-content-farm) is covered separately.

## Who actually uses them

Vendors name their own markets. AdsPower lists digital agencies and media buying, social marketing and e-commerce <!-- source: https://www.adspower.com/pricing 2026-09-29 -->; Octo Browser, digital agencies, e-commerce and content monetization <!-- source: https://octobrowser.net/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->; GeeLark, social media marketing, app tests and ad verification <!-- source: https://www.geelark.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->. That adds up to three jobs:

- **Agencies running client pages.** Each client gets a profile, and staff log in by role.
- **Sellers with several storefronts.** Shops and ad accounts stay in separate profiles.
- **Teams testing apps and ads.** Cloud phones supply Android models without buying hardware.

A clipper with a handful of accounts rarely needs any of this. TikTok lets you add accounts to one device and switch between them if each has its own phone number, email or social login, and its help asks for no separate IP address <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821549574052364 2026-09-29 -->; the rules are in [how many TikTok accounts you can have](/en/content-farm/multiple-tiktok-accounts). What matters as the count grows is that each account keeps its own topic, schedule and version of every clip, as the [account warm-up guide](/en/earnings/account-warm-up-and-shadowban) lays out. On a team, each member connects their own accounts, as [how to start a clipping agency](/en/content-farm/how-to-start-a-clipping-agency) explains.

## What a new fingerprint doesn't change

- **Originality.** A profile changes the browser, not the video. TikTok makes unoriginal or reused material with nothing new ineligible for the For You feed <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->, so one file in ten profiles is still ten copies.
- **Real viewers.** TikTok removes fake likes, followers and other inflated signals <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. DareBay tasks pay for live views only, and [why clips get rejected](/en/earnings/why-clips-get-rejected) lists every reason a clip is turned down.
- **The person behind the accounts.** TikTok strictly prohibits automation tools, scripts or other tricks designed to bypass its systems and, for deceptive behavior, may also ban additional or new accounts you create <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. For a serious violation or an attempt to dodge enforcement, it may ban all of a person's accounts, including associated ones <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/accounts-features 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Login security.** Cloud sync puts every session in one place, so guard the antidetect password like a master password.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free antidetect browser?

Yes. Dolphin{anty} <!-- source: https://dolphin-anty.net/tarifs/ 2026-09-30 --> and Multilogin <!-- source: https://multilogin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 --> give 5 profiles free, GoLogin 3 <!-- source: https://gologin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 --> and AdsPower 2 <!-- source: https://www.adspower.com/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, and Octo Browser gives new users 100 profiles in its mobile app <!-- source: https://octobrowser.net/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

### What do antidetect browsers cost for 10 profiles?

On monthly billing, AdsPower <!-- source: https://www.adspower.com/pricing 2026-09-29 --> and GoLogin <!-- source: https://gologin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 --> charge $9 for 10 profiles and Multilogin $11 <!-- source: https://multilogin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->. Billed yearly, GoLogin comes to $4.50 a month <!-- source: https://gologin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 --> and Multilogin to $7.08 <!-- source: https://multilogin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

### What does a cloud phone cost?

At GeeLark, a rented phone costs $29.90 a month with unlimited use, or you pay $0.007 a minute, capped at $1.20 a day on a paid plan <!-- source: https://www.geelark.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can TikTok ban you for using an antidetect browser?

TikTok's Community Guidelines name no browser, but they prohibit tricks designed to bypass the platform's systems and using another account to get around a restriction or a ban <!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->. A profile that hides the link between accounts, or brings a banned user back, is exactly that.

### How can a team share accounts without sharing passwords?

Start with the platform's own roles: YouTube channel permissions let people manage a channel without access to the owner's Google Account <!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328 2026-09-30 -->. In antidetect browsers, GoLogin shares profiles with viewing, editing or managing rights <!-- source: https://gologin.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->, and Dolphin{anty} adds roles and permission-based sharing from its Base plan <!-- source: https://dolphin-anty.net/tarifs/ 2026-09-30 -->.
