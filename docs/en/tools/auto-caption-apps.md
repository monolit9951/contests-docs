## Built-in captions on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube

All three platforms caption speech for free:

- **TikTok** auto-generates editable captions in the language you pick when posting, and its creator captions let you change the font style and color<!-- source: https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/accessibility 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Instagram** writes automatic closed captions at the bottom of a reel once you switch them on before posting<!-- source: https://help.instagram.com/7487270478066359/ 2026-09-29 -->.
- **YouTube** auto-captions long videos and Shorts in 67 languages, warns that quality may vary and by default replaces potentially inappropriate words with "[ __ ]"<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6373554 2026-09-29 -->.

Viewers can hide these closed captions, the platform sets their look, and they stay behind when the clip moves. For one look everywhere, burn captions in with an app below.

## Frequently asked questions

### What's the best free app for auto captions on a phone?

Edits or Adobe Premiere: both are free, caption speech automatically and export 4K without a watermark<!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/edits-video-editor/id6738967378 2026-09-29 --><!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adobe-premiere-ai-video-editor/id6742757464 2026-09-29 -->. YouTube Create is free too, but only in 21 countries<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13952912 2026-09-29 -->.

### Does CapCut still have free auto captions?

You can sometimes apply them free, but CapCut's help center says auto captions include Pro-only functionality, and exports containing Pro features require Pro. To stay free, remove or replace anything marked with the Pro icon<!-- source: https://www.capcut.com/help/capcut-pro-prompt-standard-user 2026-09-29 -->.

### Is the Captions app better than CapCut for captions?

It depends on the plan: Captions' free plan offers one caption template, and customizable captions start with Max at $24.99 a month<!-- source: https://captions.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. CapCut Pro costs $19.99 a month on the US App Store<!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/capcut-photo-video-editor/id1500855883 2026-09-29 --> and can be required to export captions<!-- source: https://www.capcut.com/help/capcut-pro-prompt-standard-user 2026-09-29 -->.

### What's a cheaper alternative to Submagic?

Free: Edits, Adobe Premiere, or Vizard's generator, whose free videos carry a watermark<!-- source: https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8767572-what-does-the-free-plan-for-vizard-offer 2026-09-29 -->. Paid: Kapwing Pro, $16 a month billed yearly for up to 1,000 minutes of auto-subtitling<!-- source: https://www.kapwing.com/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, against 15 videos for $19 a month on Submagic Starter<!-- source: https://www.submagic.co/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.

### Which caption apps support Urdu or Hinglish?

Captions lists both among its caption languages<!-- source: https://captions.ai/help/docs/captions/add-captions 2026-09-29 -->, while YouTube's automatic captions<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6373554 2026-09-29 --> and Vizard<!-- source: https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8767561-what-languages-are-supported 2026-09-29 --> list neither.

### Do captions make a reposted clip original?

No. Instagram says re-uploading someone else's work with only a border, watermark, subtitles or a credit added doesn't qualify as original<!-- source: https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram 2026-09-29 -->. Captions make a clip watchable on mute; the right to post the footage comes from the task or the owner.
