## What no AI tool fixes: originality

The platforms judge the result, not the tool.

- **TikTok** calls content unoriginal when it is copied, largely repurposed without creative edits, stitched from sources with little added value, or carries someone else's watermark; it may leave the For You feed and can't earn in Creator Rewards<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Instagram** says a border, watermark, subtitles or credit on a re-uploaded reel don't make it original, and accounts that mostly post such reels lose recommendations<!-- source: https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Facebook** deprioritizes low-value changes to someone else's reel, such as borders, inserted captions or a new speed<!-- source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/ 2026-09-29 -->.

Use AI on footage you may use; [where to get source footage](/en/earnings/where-to-find-clipping-work) lists the options. For several accounts, change what viewers see and hear in each version: in DareBay's own test in September 2026, color, noise, speed and new metadata left a clip nearly identical for copy-matching tools, while a split-screen layout and a new voiceover changed it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which parts of a faceless Reel need an AI label?

The realistic ones. YouTube asks creators to disclose realistic AI scenes and AI-generated music, but not AI-written scripts, captions or a clone of their own voice<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 2026-09-29 -->, and TikTok requires a label whenever AI content looks like real scenes or real people<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can I use a free AI plan for clips I get paid for?

Check the license first. TTSMaker allows commercial use on its free tier<!-- source: https://ttsmaker.com/ 2026-09-29 -->, but ElevenLabs grants a commercial license from its $6 Starter plan<!-- source: https://elevenlabs.io/pricing 2026-09-29 --> and Kling AI allows commercial use from Standard<!-- source: https://kling.ai/app/membership/membership-plan 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can I voice my clips with a clone of my own voice?

Yes: ElevenLabs includes Instant Voice Cloning from its $6 Starter plan<!-- source: https://elevenlabs.io/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, and YouTube needs no AI disclosure when you clone your own voice for voiceovers or dubs<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 2026-09-29 -->. Clone only your own voice, never someone else's.

### Does an AI label reduce a clip's reach?

According to YouTube, an AI disclosure neither narrows a video's audience nor affects its ability to earn<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 2026-09-29 -->. Skipping it is riskier: TikTok may remove, restrict or label unlabeled realistic AI content itself<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity 2026-09-29 -->.
