Tools for Reels, Shorts and TikTok are the apps and web services that handle each stage of a short video: editing, cutting a long recording into clips, captions, voiceover, AI video, posting and analytics. This catalog sorts them by stage and lists them A to Z within each one, and every card shows the price as of September 29, 2026, the free plan if there is one, and where the tool runs.

DareBay, a platform that pays clippers per counted view on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, publishes this catalog. Its own tools sit in a separate, labeled group, and [how we pick tools](#how-we-pick) explains what gets listed.

A subscription buys you editing, clipping and a voice, but not the part that matters most: someone who pays for the views. DareBay covers that part of the stack: its tasks pay for the views, and offer tasks come with ready clips, a separate version for every account, and a setup for each platform.

## Build your stack {#stack}

A stack is the handful of tools that takes a clip from raw footage to posted. The four below are built from cards in this catalog and priced as of September 29, 2026, and each one says where it runs.

### Start free, on your phone

For your first month, while you test a niche and a format.

- **Edit, caption and voice.** VN is free with no watermarks, lists auto captions and text to speech among its features, and runs on iOS, Android, Mac and Windows<!-- source: https://www.vlognow.me/ 2026-09-29 -->. Instagram's Edits app runs on iPhone and Android<!-- source: https://creators.instagram.com/edits/ 2026-09-29 -->, is free and exports up to 4K with no watermark<!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/edits-video-editor/id6738967378 2026-09-29 -->.
- **AI clipping on trial.** Vizard's free plan covers 60 minutes of uploaded video a month, with 720p export and a watermark<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. OpusClip's free plan gives 60 credits a month, with a watermark and 3 days to export<!-- source: https://www.opus.pro/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Posting.** Post from each platform's own app; TikTok Studio on the web also lets you schedule posts up to 30 days ahead<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 -->.

**Total: $0 a month.** When you want those clips to earn, see [how a content farm gets paid per view on DareBay](/en/content-farm/).

## How we pick tools {#how-we-pick}

DareBay, the publisher of this page and a platform that pays clippers per counted view, puts this catalog together. So it covers tools for making and posting your own clips for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook Reels, from the first cut to the stats.

- **Order.** Tools are grouped by stage and listed A to Z within each stage. There is no ranking.
- **DareBay's own tools** sit in a separate, labeled group at the top of the catalog and aren't part of the stage-by-stage comparison.
- **Prices** come from each tool's own pricing page, with the date we checked, currently September 29, 2026. Some tools price by visitor country, so the card says which region a price is for.
- **Where it runs** comes from each tool's own site and its app store listings.
- **Links** to tools are plain links with no affiliate tracking for now; if that changes, affiliate links will be marked on the card and here.
- **What we leave out:** view botting, account sellers, tools for downloading other people's videos, and tools that publish no price or terms on their own pages.

Spotted an outdated price or a missing tool? Tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/unCNzuhcHP). Who writes DareBay's guides and where the numbers come from is on [who writes the DareBay guides](/en/about/who-writes-darebay-guides).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Reels and Shorts tools are free to start with?

VN edits for free with no watermarks<!-- source: https://www.vlognow.me/ 2026-09-29 -->, Vizard's online generator adds animated captions for free<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/tools/auto-subtitle-generator-online 2026-09-29 -->, and TTSMaker voices up to 20,000 characters a week<!-- source: https://ttsmaker.com/ 2026-09-29 -->. For AI clipping only trial allowances are free: 60 minutes a month on Vizard<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 --> and 60 credits a month on OpusClip, both with a watermark<!-- source: https://www.opus.pro/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can a phone handle the whole stack?

Mostly, yes: VN and Edits edit and caption on a phone<!-- source: https://www.vlognow.me/ 2026-09-29 --><!-- source: https://creators.instagram.com/edits/ 2026-09-29 -->, Vizard has apps on the App Store and Google Play<!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vizard-ai-video-clip-maker/id6748490660 2026-09-29 --><!-- source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.vizard.android 2026-09-29 -->, and OpusClip has an iOS app plus an Android app in eight countries<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/does-opusclip-have-a-mobile-app 2026-09-29 --><!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/android-app-supported-unsupported-features 2026-09-29 -->. Scheduling for several accounts is easier on a computer, because TikTok schedules posts in TikTok Studio on the web<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 -->.

### How much does a clipping tool stack cost per month?

$0 on free plans while their limits last; paid AI clipping costs $15 a month on OpusClip Starter<!-- source: https://www.opus.pro/pricing 2026-09-29 --> or $14.50 a month on Vizard Creator billed yearly<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, and a cross-platform scheduler such as Metricool Starter adds $20 a month billed yearly<!-- source: https://metricool.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->. The four ready-made stacks with monthly totals are in [Build your stack](#stack).

### How do I make a different version of a clip for each account?

Change what viewers see and hear: the layout of the frame, the captions, the voiceover. TikTok counts content repurposed from another source without creative edits as unoriginal and may remove it from the For You feed<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy 2026-09-29 -->, so a filter or added noise isn't enough; post one version per account and never the same file twice.
