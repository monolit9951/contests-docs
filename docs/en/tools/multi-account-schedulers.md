To schedule clips for several TikTok, Reels and Shorts accounts, start with the free schedulers built into each app, then add a multi-account tool such as Later, Metricool, Publer, Repurpose.io or SocialPilot once switching between accounts starts eating your day. A multi-account scheduler is a service that holds posts for many profiles in one calendar and publishes each at its set time, so you upload once instead of logging in and out of every account.

DareBay, a platform that pays clippers per counted view, put this comparison together. The schedulers belong to other companies, and no link here carries an affiliate tag. Every price and limit was read on September 29, 2026 on each service's own page; prices are in US dollars, and tools are listed A to Z. Tools for the other stages of a clip, from AI clipping to editing, are in the [tools catalog for Reels, Shorts and TikTok](/en/tools/).

## What the built-in schedulers allow

| Platform | Where you schedule | How far ahead | Worth knowing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | In the app, when you post a reel, photo or carousel | Up to 30 days, at most 25 posts a day<!-- source: https://help.instagram.com/439971288310029/ 2026-09-29 --> | Public accounts only; product tags, collabs, sponsored posts and fundraisers can't be scheduled<!-- source: https://help.instagram.com/439971288310029/ 2026-09-29 --> |
| TikTok | TikTok Studio on the web | Up to 30 days<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 --> | Creators with more than 1,000 followers can bulk-upload up to 30 videos at once<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 --> |
| YouTube | YouTube Studio or the YouTube app | You pick the date, time and time zone; until then the video stays private<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en 2026-09-29 --> | With a Community Guidelines strike, a scheduled video won't publish during the penalty period<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en 2026-09-29 --> |

Native schedulers cost nothing and publish as the account itself, so no password goes to a third party. Their limit is scope: each works inside one account, so five TikTok accounts mean five scheduling sessions.

## Which scheduler fits which setup

- **One account per platform.** The built-in schedulers are enough.
- **Three accounts on a budget.** Publer's free plan covers 3 accounts with 10 queued posts each<!-- source: https://publer.com/plans 2026-09-29 -->, and Metricool's free brand covers one TikTok, one Instagram and one YouTube profile with 20 scheduled posts a month<!-- source: https://metricool.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Ten or more accounts on one platform.** Brands and social sets hold one profile per network, so ten TikTok accounts are ten units, and bills differ widely: Metricool's Starter with 10 brands is $36 a month<!-- source: https://metricool.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->, while Later's Growth plus eight extra sets is $37.50 + 8 × $11.25 = $127.50 a month, both billed yearly<!-- source: https://later.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.
- **A team.** SocialPilot's Standard plan includes 3 users and 15 accounts<!-- source: https://www.socialpilot.co/plans 2026-09-29 -->, and Later's Growth plan includes 2 users<!-- source: https://later.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

## Where bulk posting costs you reach

A scheduler saves time; it can't tell a good calendar from spam.

- **One file on every account.** Cross-posting pushes the same video everywhere, but platforms treat one file on several accounts as a repeat and cut its reach: every account needs its own version. TikTok's rules on this are in [how many TikTok accounts you can have](/en/content-farm/multiple-tiktok-accounts).
- **Ten clips in one minute.** A burst of posts looks like bot activity. A working pace is one or two clips a day per account, at the hours its viewers are online; the other reasons reach drops are in the [account warm-up and shadowban guide](/en/earnings/account-warm-up-and-shadowban).
- **A clip that went out private.** A scheduled YouTube video stays private until its time<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en 2026-09-29 -->, and TikTok keeps posts from unaudited apps private<!-- source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-get-started 2026-09-29 -->. Check the clip is public before you count it as posted.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you bulk upload videos to TikTok?

Yes, on the web: TikTok Studio lets creators with more than 1,000 followers upload up to 30 videos at once<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 -->. After the upload you can still adjust scheduling and post settings<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/advanced-desktop-tools 2026-09-29 -->.

### How far ahead can you schedule a TikTok?

Up to 30 days in TikTok Studio on the web, according to TikTok's guide for creators<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tool-web-creation-intro 2026-09-29 -->. TikTok describes scheduling as a desktop feature<!-- source: https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/advanced-desktop-tools 2026-09-29 -->.

### Can I schedule Instagram Reels without a professional account?

Instagram's help center names one requirement: the account must be public. Public accounts can schedule reels, photos and carousels in the app up to 30 days ahead, 25 posts a day at most<!-- source: https://help.instagram.com/439971288310029/ 2026-09-29 -->.

### How much does it cost to schedule 10 TikTok accounts?

On September 29, 2026, Metricool's Starter plan with 10 brands cost $36 a month billed annually<!-- source: https://metricool.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->, and Repurpose.io's Pro plan, which takes up to 10 accounts per network, cost $790 a year<!-- source: https://repurpose.io/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->.

### Why did my scheduled TikTok go out as private?

TikTok keeps everything an unaudited app posts private until the app passes TikTok's audit<!-- source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-get-started 2026-09-29 -->. Ask the scheduler whether its TikTok integration is audited.

### Does a scheduled clip count in a DareBay task?

Yes. DareBay, which publishes this page, counts the views on the published clip, however it was posted. Send the link once the clip is live, since a scheduled YouTube video stays private until its set time<!-- source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1270709?hl=en 2026-09-29 -->.
