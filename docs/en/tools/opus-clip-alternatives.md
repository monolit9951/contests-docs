The main OpusClip alternatives for clippers are Vizard, Klap, 2short.ai, Choppity, quso.ai and Spikes Studio, and what separates them most is the cost of an hour of footage: from about $1 on 2short.ai's Premium plan<!-- source: https://2short.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 --> to $17.40 on quso.ai's smallest plan billed monthly<!-- source: https://quso.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. Like OpusClip, each one is an AI clipper: a tool that finds the strongest moments in a long recording, reframes them to vertical 9:16 and adds captions.

DareBay, a platform that pays clippers per counted view, put this comparison together. Links to the tools carry no affiliate tracking, and there is no ranking: OpusClip comes first as the baseline, then the alternatives A to Z. Prices were read off each tool's own pages on September 29, 2026; tools for editing, captions and posting are in the [tools catalog for Reels, Shorts and TikTok](/en/tools/).

## Cost per hour of footage, plan by plan

Most AI clippers bill the minutes you upload, not the clips you keep: OpusClip<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed 2026-09-29 -->, Vizard<!-- source: https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/12017624-how-credits-are-used-for-ai-processing 2026-09-29 --> and quso.ai<!-- source: https://quso.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 --> count a credit as about one minute of video, and 2short.ai, Choppity and Spikes Studio sell hours or minutes of analysis. A three-hour stream costs 180 credits whether it yields five clips or fifty.

## Free plans: minutes, watermark and export window

Free plans are for testing on your own footage, and most of them brand every clip.

- **OpusClip:** 60 credits a month, clips up to 1080p with a watermark, exportable for 3 days<!-- source: https://www.opus.pro/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **2short.ai:** 30 minutes of analysis a month on the free plan card (a line further down says 15), and 2short.ai says every plan gets all features, watermark-free 1080p exports included<!-- source: https://2short.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Choppity:** 1 hour of analysis a month and 3-day project storage<!-- source: https://www.choppity.com/pricing/ 2026-09-29 -->; its FAQ says free exports carry Choppity branding<!-- source: https://www.choppity.com/tools/free-ai-clip-maker/ 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Klap:** one video free to try, no credit card needed<!-- source: https://klap.app/ 2026-09-29 -->.
- **quso.ai:** 75 credits a month, 720p renders and 7 days of data retention<!-- source: https://quso.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Spikes Studio:** up to 30 minutes of video a month, with a watermark and 720p export<!-- source: https://www.spikes.studio/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Vizard:** 60 credits a month and 720p export<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, with a Vizard.ai watermark and projects that expire after 3 days<!-- source: https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8767572-what-does-the-free-plan-for-vizard-offer 2026-09-29 -->.

## Phone apps and country support

OpusClip and Vizard have clipping apps for phones. OpusClip has an iOS app<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/does-opusclip-have-a-mobile-app 2026-09-29 --> and an Android app for Android 12 and later, offered at launch only in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Spain and Brazil, in English<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/android-app-supported-unsupported-features 2026-09-29 -->, so clippers in India, Pakistan, Nigeria or the Philippines can't get it yet. Vizard is on the App Store<!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vizard-ai-video-clip-maker/id6748490660 2026-09-29 --> and Google Play<!-- source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.vizard.android 2026-09-29 -->. The other tools here run in a web browser.

OpusClip opens from anywhere, but its official country list includes India and the Philippines and leaves out Nigeria, Pakistan and Kenya<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/supported-countries-regions-territories 2026-09-29 -->; it takes only payment methods that Stripe supports, with UPI in India<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/payments-and-billing-info 2026-09-29 -->. Vizard accepts cards, bank accounts, Google Pay and Cash App<!-- source: https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8984329-what-types-of-payment-methods-does-vizard-offer 2026-09-29 -->, and Spikes Studio offers PayPal<!-- source: https://www.spikes.studio/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.

## Which alternative fits which clipper

- **Cheap hours from YouTube videos:** 2short.ai, which takes YouTube links and processes only videos with captions; Lite at $9.90 a month adds Google Drive and public URLs<!-- source: https://2short.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **High volume, billed yearly:** Vizard Creator at $1.45 an hour<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, or quso.ai Growth, $33 a month for 1,200 credits, about $1.65<!-- source: https://quso.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.
- **Gaming streams with little talking:** OpusClip's ClipAnything reads visual, audio and sentiment cues, including in videos with little to no dialogue<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/9947095-clip-anything 2026-09-29 -->, while Klap says its algorithm relies heavily on speech detection<!-- source: https://klap.app/ 2026-09-29 -->.

Whichever tool cuts, plan a finishing pass for the captions, the first second and the frame; free editors for it are compared in [CapCut alternatives](/en/tools/capcut-alternatives). If one clip goes to several accounts, give each account its own version rather than the same file.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest OpusClip alternative per hour of footage?

Among the tools compared here, 2short.ai: Premium works out to about $1 an hour and Pro to $1.33<!-- source: https://2short.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. Vizard Creator billed yearly comes next at $1.45<!-- source: https://vizard.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, while OpusClip itself costs $6 an hour on Starter and $2.90 on Pro billed yearly<!-- source: https://www.opus.pro/pricing 2026-09-29 -->.

### Is there a free OpusClip alternative without a watermark?

2short.ai says every plan, the free one included, gets all features, watermark-free exports among them<!-- source: https://2short.ai/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. OpusClip<!-- source: https://www.opus.pro/pricing 2026-09-29 -->, Spikes Studio<!-- source: https://www.spikes.studio/pricing 2026-09-29 --> and Vizard<!-- source: https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8767572-what-does-the-free-plan-for-vizard-offer 2026-09-29 --> watermark free-plan clips, and Choppity brands free exports<!-- source: https://www.choppity.com/tools/free-ai-clip-maker/ 2026-09-29 -->.

### Which OpusClip alternatives have a phone app?

Vizard, with apps on the App Store and Google Play<!-- source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vizard-ai-video-clip-maker/id6748490660 2026-09-29 --><!-- source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.vizard.android 2026-09-29 -->. OpusClip itself has an iOS app<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/does-opusclip-have-a-mobile-app 2026-09-29 --> and an Android app in eight countries<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/android-app-supported-unsupported-features 2026-09-29 -->.

### Does OpusClip work in Nigeria, Pakistan or Kenya?

It opens from anywhere, but Nigeria, Pakistan and Kenya are not on its list of officially supported countries, while India and the Philippines are<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/supported-countries-regions-territories 2026-09-29 -->. It takes payment through Stripe only<!-- source: https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/payments-and-billing-info 2026-09-29 -->, so check your card before you rely on it.

### Is Submagic an OpusClip alternative?

Partly: its plans count short videos, 15 of up to 2 minutes on the $19 Starter plan<!-- source: https://www.submagic.co/pricing 2026-09-29 -->. It has a long-video mode, Magic Clips, but its pricing page doesn't say how much footage that covers.

### Will DareBay pay for clips cut with an AI tool?

Yes, if the clip meets the task's terms and collects counted views: DareBay, which publishes this page, pays for the result, whichever editor you used. Inflated views are not paid, and the footage must be something you have the right to clip.
