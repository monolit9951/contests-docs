# Can I pay with crypto?

Yes. The DareBay wallet accepts both fiat and crypto. You can:

- **Top up** in crypto (supported stablecoins and chains — shown in your
  wallet's deposit screen).
- **Pay for a contest's prize + commission** from that crypto balance.
- **Receive winnings** in the same asset you deposited, or in the asset the
  organizer chose for the prize.

## What's supported

The supported list is maintained in the wallet UI and updated as we add
chains. As of launch, DareBay supports the major stablecoins on the
widely-adopted EVM chains, plus a few non-EVM options.

## Do I *have* to use crypto?

No. Fiat (card, bank transfer, supported regional methods) works the same
way. The wallet is currency-agnostic — what goes in stays in that currency
until you explicitly convert.

## Conversion

If you deposit in USDT and want to publish a contest with a prize in EUR,
DareBay offers an explicit conversion step at the current rate. You see the
rate and the final amount before you confirm; nothing happens silently in
the background.

## Privacy

Crypto deposits are tied to your DareBay account the same way card payments
are — this is a prize-payout platform, not an anonymity layer. KYC
requirements follow the jurisdiction you're in and the amounts you're
moving.
