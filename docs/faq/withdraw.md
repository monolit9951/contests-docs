# Can I withdraw my winnings?

Yes. Any balance in your DareBay wallet that's in **available** state can be
withdrawn at any time.

## Methods

You can withdraw to:

- **A bank account or card** — for fiat balances.
- **A crypto wallet address** — for crypto balances, on the same chain you
  deposited (or one of the supported ones).

The options shown depend on your region and verification level.

## Timing

- **On-platform** settlement (prize → wallet) is near-instant when a contest
  finishes.
- **Off-platform** withdrawal depends on the rail:
  - Crypto — usually minutes, subject to network confirmation.
  - Card — typically same day in supported regions.
  - Bank transfer — 1–3 business days depending on country.

## "Available" vs "locked" balance

Your wallet shows two numbers:

- **Available** — what you can spend or withdraw right now.
- **Locked** — tied up in active contests (as an organizer) or held during
  dispute review.

You can only withdraw from available. Locked funds unlock automatically when
the contest they're attached to finishes (or, for disputed prizes, when the
review is resolved).

## Limits

Withdrawal limits depend on:

- Your verification tier (higher tier = higher limits).
- The rail you're using (some have network-side caps).
- Regional compliance rules.

Your current limits are always visible in the wallet settings.

## Fees

DareBay doesn't charge a platform fee on withdrawals. Network-side fees
(blockchain gas, bank wire charges) are passed through at cost.
