# Create your first contest

A contest is a prize + a brief + a deadline. If you have all three in your
head, you can publish in under ten minutes.

## 1. Open the creation page

Click **Create Contest** from your DareBay header. You'll land on a single
form — everything fits on one screen; no five-step wizard.

## 2. Pick a title people will click

Short, concrete, specific. "Film yourself cooking breakfast in one take" beats
"best morning content." Your title is the only thing most viewers read before
deciding whether to open the full brief.

## 3. Write the brief

Treat the description like a contract. What exactly has to be in the
submission? What's disqualifying? Is English required? Is original work
required? Be explicit — ambiguity hurts you more than it hurts participants.

## 4. Choose the category and subcategory

This is how people find your contest. Pick the narrowest category that still
fits. Narrower = more relevant eyeballs.

## 5. Pick the work format

One of three:

- **Text** — written submissions, up to the character limit you set.
- **Image** — single image or small gallery.
- **Video** — short video, uploaded to DareBay storage.

You can require only one format per contest.

## 6. Pick how winners are chosen

One of three selection types:

- **RANDOM** — among the top-liked works when the contest closes, the system
  picks at random. Good for low-friction giveaways.
- **CREATOR_DECISION** — you pick the winner yourself after the deadline.
  Good when quality is subjective and you trust your own taste.
- **VIEWER_VOTING** — the community votes; top vote-getters win. Good when
  you want the contest to feel communal.

You commit to one type at creation — it can't be changed after publish.

## 7. Set the prize(s)

Prizes can be **MONEY** (USD, EUR, PLN, UAH, or supported crypto) or an
**ITEM** (described in text — "a signed vinyl", "a pair of concert tickets").
You can have multiple prize tiers (1st, 2nd, 3rd) and even multiple winners
per tier.

Money prizes are locked in your wallet at publish time. You won't be able to
un-lock them during the contest.

## 8. Set the schedule

- **Start** — when submissions open. "Right now" is fine.
- **Submission deadline** — when new works are no longer accepted.
- **Selection deadline** (CREATOR_DECISION only) — by when you commit to
  naming winners.

## 9. Review the commission

DareBay charges a small commission on prize funds — you see the exact amount
(in your currency) before you confirm. See [fees](../faq/fees) for the full
table.

## 10. Publish

One click. Your contest moves from **UPCOMING** to **ACTIVE**; the prize
locks; participants can start submitting.
