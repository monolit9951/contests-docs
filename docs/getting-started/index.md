# Getting Started

DareBay works three ways at once. You can be an **organizer** (you set the
challenge and put up the prize), a **participant** (you submit work and try to
win), or a **viewer** (you watch, vote, comment — and sometimes that decides
who wins). Most accounts end up doing all three.

## If you want to run a contest

Put up a prize, define what people have to do to win, and publish. Submissions
come in, winners get picked, prizes pay out. This is the fastest way to get a
piece of content made on demand.

→ [**Create your first contest**](./create-your-first-contest)

## If you want to enter a contest

Browse active contests, read the brief, submit your work (text, image, or
video), and wait for the selection phase. If you win, the prize is already
funded — it lands in your wallet.

→ [**Submit a work**](./submit-a-work)

## If you just want to watch

Scroll the feed, like works, leave comments, and — in voting-type contests —
vote. Viewers shape outcomes without ever submitting a thing.

→ [**Watch, vote & win**](./watch-vote-win)
