# Submit a work

Entering a DareBay contest takes less time than reading about it.

## 1. Find a contest that fits

Browse the feed or filter by category. Open a contest card. **Read the brief
carefully** — most disqualifications happen because someone skimmed the rules
and missed a requirement.

Check:

- Is it still **ACTIVE**? (Closed contests can't accept submissions.)
- What format does it require? (Text / image / video — usually only one.)
- Any hard disqualifiers — language, originality, length limits?

## 2. Make the thing

Make it real. Stolen work, AI output passed off as original, and other
shortcuts get submissions removed and accounts flagged. Your **participant
rating** follows you across the platform — it's worth more than any single
prize.

## 3. Upload your submission

Click **Submit** on the contest page. Depending on format:

- **Text** — type directly or paste.
- **Image** — upload (jpg, png, webp).
- **Video** — upload; videos go to DareBay's media storage and are
  transcoded for streaming. Keep it under the size limit shown on the form.

You can also include a short note explaining your entry — useful for
creator-judged contests.

## 4. Wait for the selection phase

After the submission deadline, the contest moves into
**SELECTION_IN_PROGRESS**. What happens next depends on the selection type
the organizer picked:

- **Community voting** — viewers vote; you can share your submission to pull
  in support.
- **Creator's pick** — the organizer reviews and decides.
- **Random among top-liked** — the system picks.

## 5. If you win

You'll get a notification. For **MONEY** prizes, the funds are already locked
in the contest — they move to your wallet as soon as the organizer confirms
(or automatically, depending on selection type). For **ITEM** prizes, the
organizer reaches out to arrange delivery.

## 6. If you don't

That's okay. Enter the next one. Your participant rating ticks up just for
submitting real work and not breaking rules — winners aren't the only ones
who gain standing.
