# Watch, vote & win

Most of DareBay's traffic isn't organizers or participants. It's viewers —
people who scroll the feed, like what they like, and vote when the contest
asks them to.

## Scroll the feed

The home feed shows submissions across active contests, sorted by recency and
engagement. Open any work to see the full brief of the contest it belongs to.

## Like (or dislike)

Reactions matter. In **RANDOM** contests, the system picks winners from the
top-liked works. A like isn't just a vibe — it's a vote that narrows the pool.

## Comment

Most contests let you leave a comment on a submission. Use it for real
feedback. Trash-talking losers and harassing participants is a banned pattern —
it's the single fastest way to lose your account.

## Vote (in voting contests)

If a contest uses **VIEWER_VOTING**, each submission gets a vote button during
the selection phase. One vote per account per contest. The totals decide the
winner.

Vote rigging — sockpuppet accounts, coordinated inauthentic campaigns — gets
submissions removed and accounts banned. Your viewer rating can go down too.

## Why your rating matters

Every DareBay account has two ratings: **organizer** and **participant**.
Viewers don't have a separate rating, but sustained good behavior on the
platform (authentic likes, substantive comments, no harassment) is visible
to moderators and affects how edge-case decisions go.
