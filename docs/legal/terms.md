## 1. What DareBay does

DareBay is a contest platform. Someone (an **Organizer**) puts up a prize
and sets a challenge. Other people (**Participants**) submit work.
Viewers watch, vote, comment. Winners get paid. The end.

We provide the platform and the wallet that moves value between
Organizers and Participants. We are **not** a Participant in any contest,
**not** a judge of taste (except when our rules require us to act), and

## 4. Contests

### 4.1 Roles

- **Organizer** — the user who creates and funds a contest.
- **Participant** — a user who submits a work to a contest.
- **Viewer** — anyone who watches, comments, votes, or reports.

### 4.2 Organizer commitments

When you publish a contest, you commit to:

- Funding the **full prize amount** at the moment of publication. The
  prize is locked in your wallet and cannot be spent on anything else.
- Honouring the **selection method** you chose:
  - **RANDOM** — top-liked works are auto-selected;
  - **CREATOR_DECISION** — you pick the winners manually within the
    selection window;
  - **VIEWER_VOTING** — community votes decide.
- Honouring the **rules** you wrote in the contest brief.
- Delivering any **ITEM** prize within the agreed window after winner
  confirmation.

You cannot cancel a contest after it has started accepting submissions
without our review and the consent of any affected Participants. Cancels
before submissions begin return funds to your wallet (minus commission
already taken — see [Section 7](#_7-commission)).

### 4.3 Participant commitments

When you submit a work, you commit that:

- The work is **yours**, or you have permission to submit it.
- It complies with the contest brief and these Terms.
- For **imported works** from TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube, you are
  the author of the source video, or you have the author's permission.
  Ownership must be verified through the connected-account flow before
  the submission counts.
- One submission per Participant per contest, unless the contest brief
  allows more.

### 4.4 Selection and prize lock

Once a contest closes:

1. The selection mechanism runs (or the Organizer picks, depending on the
   selection type chosen).
2. Winners are confirmed.
3. Prize Coins move from the contest lock to the winners' wallets.
4. Commission is settled with the Operator.

If the Organizer does not act within the selection window, fallback rules
apply (typically the top-rated submissions auto-win, with possible
escalation to platform review). The exact fallback for each selection
type is documented in **[Getting Started → How winners are chosen](../faq/choosing-winners)**.

### 4.5 Disputes

Disputes about a contest (e.g. "this winner submitted stolen work") go
through:

1. Reporting in the platform.
2. Operator review, including evidence from both parties.
3. A decision: stand, redo selection, refund, or ban.

The Operator's decision in a dispute is final, but you retain any rights
you have under the consumer-protection law of your country.

When you connect a TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube account, you authorize
DareBay to verify that you own a video you've submitted to a contest by
matching your authenticated handle against the public author handle of

### 8.2 What we won't do

We won't post on your behalf, message anyone from your account, read
your private messages, or use your platform data for anything other than
ownership verification. Details are in our [Privacy Policy, Section 3](./privacy#_3-tiktok-instagram-and-youtube-data).

### 8.3 Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time in **Settings → Connected accounts**.

previously verified through that connection remain attributed to you,
but no further verification flows from that link.
